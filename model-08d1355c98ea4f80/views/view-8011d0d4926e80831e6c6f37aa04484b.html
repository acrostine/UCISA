## Purpose Assessment Delivery ensures that approved assessments are executed in a controlled, fair, and accessible environment. It coordinates the physical or digital venue, verifies student identity, distributes materials, monitors the session, and collects completed work. The process upholds academic integrity and accessibility standards, producing completed assessments ready for the evaluation phase. ## Roles - Assessment Coordinator: Oversees the delivery process, manages venues and schedules - Invigilator/Proctor: Monitors the assessment session, ensures rules are followed - Student: Participates in the assessment, submits completed work - IT Support (System): Manages online platforms and technical infrastructure - Accessibility Officer: Ensures accommodations are in place for students with needs ## Upstream Inputs - Approved Assessment Materials - Student Roster - Delivery Platform or Venue Schedule - Accessibility Accommodation Requests ## Downstream Outputs - Completed Assessments (ready for evaluation) - Session Incident Report - Attendance and Submission Log ## Key Decisions - Whether a student's identity is verified successfully - Whether an incident or irregularity requires escalation - Whether the session environment meets accessibility and fairness standards - Whether all submissions are collected and accounted for ## Exception Paths - Identity verification failure: Student is flagged and directed to a verification desk for resolution - Technical platform failure: Session is paused, IT support is engaged, and a contingency plan is activated - Student misconduct or rule violation: Incident is documented, student is warned or removed, and a formal report is filed - Missing or incomplete submission: Student is allowed to resubmit within a grace period if technical issues are confirmed ## Step Walkthrough 1. Prepare Assessment Environment: The Assessment Coordinator reviews the delivery schedule, sets up the physical venue or online platform, ensures all accessibility accommodations are in place, and verifies that approved assessment materials are ready for distribution. This step establishes a fair and reliable environment for the assessment session. 2. Verify Student Identity: The Invigilator checks each student's identification document against the official student roster. If identity is confirmed, the student proceeds to receive assessment materials. If identity cannot be verified, the student is directed to a verification desk for resolution. 3. Distribute Assessment Materials: The Invigilator distributes the approved assessment materials to all students whose identity has been verified. For online assessments, the system releases the test to eligible students. This step ensures that only authorised students receive the correct materials. 4. Monitor Assessment Session: The Invigilator actively monitors the assessment session, ensuring students follow rules and that the environment remains fair. If any incident or irregularity occurs (e.g., misconduct, technical failure), it is documented and escalated for review. 5. Collect Completed Assessments: The Invigilator collects all completed assessment submissions from students, either physically or through the online platform. Each submission is checked against the roster to ensure no work is missing. Incomplete or missing submissions are flagged for follow-up. 6. Review Session Incidents: The Assessment Coordinator reviews all incident reports generated during the session, including identity issues, misconduct, or technical failures. A decision is made to either resolve the incident internally or escalate it to a formal academic integrity process. 7. Finalise Assessment Delivery: The Assessment Coordinator confirms that all completed assessments are accounted for, logs attendance and submission data, and formally hands off the completed assessments to the evaluation team. The session is closed and archived.
