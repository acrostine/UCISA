Student Health & Wellbeing ()
## Purpose This process ensures that students receive appropriate and timely support for their health and wellbeing needs, from initial intake through to plan implementation and ongoing monitoring. It integrates mental health services, counselling, and health promotion to foster a supportive campus environment. ## Roles - Student (initiator) - Wellbeing Advisor (assessor and plan developer) - Counsellor (service provider) - Health Services Manager (approver) - System (automated triage and notifications) ## Upstream Inputs - Health Assessment Submission - Wellbeing Survey Response ## Downstream Outputs - Tailored Support Plan - Preventative Initiative Notification - Escalation Report ## Key Decisions - Triage level determination (routine vs urgent) - Approval of the tailored support plan - Escalation decision based on progress monitoring ## Exception Paths - If triage identifies an urgent case, the process bypasses standard scheduling and immediately assigns a counsellor. - If the support plan is rejected, the Wellbeing Advisor revises and resubmits it. - If progress monitoring indicates deterioration, an escalation report is generated and the case is reassigned. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Triage Intake: System automatically triages the submission based on severity indicators, routing to routine or urgent path. 3. Assess Wellbeing Needs: Wellbeing Advisor conducts a comprehensive assessment of the student's health and wellbeing. 4. Develop Support Plan: Wellbeing Advisor creates a tailored support plan based on assessment findings. 5. Approve Support Plan: Health Services Manager reviews and approves the support plan. 6. Implement Support Plan: Counsellor executes the approved plan, including counselling sessions and health promotion activities. 7. Monitor Student Progress: Wellbeing Advisor monitors the student's progress and decides if escalation is needed. Steps: 1. Submit Health Intake: The student accesses the student portal and completes a health assessment form or wellbeing survey. This initiates the process and provides initial data for triage. 2. Triage Intake: The system evaluates the intake data against predefined severity criteria. It determines whether the case is routine or urgent, routing it accordingly. 3. Assess Wellbeing Needs: The Wellbeing Advisor reviews the triage result and conducts a detailed assessment of the student's mental health, wellbeing, and any risk factors. This may involve a consultation session. 4. Develop Support Plan: Based on the assessment, the Wellbeing Advisor drafts a support plan that includes counselling sessions, health promotion activities, and any necessary referrals. 5. Approve Support Plan: The Health Services Manager reviews the draft support plan for appropriateness, resource allocation, and compliance. If approved, it moves to implementation; if rejected, it is returned for revision. 6. Revise Support Plan: If the plan is rejected, the Wellbeing Advisor addresses the manager's feedback and updates the plan accordingly, then resubmits for approval. 7. Implement Support Plan: The Counsellor delivers the planned interventions, including counselling sessions, health promotion workshops, and follow-up activities as outlined in the plan. 8. Monitor Student Progress: The Wellbeing Advisor tracks the student's progress through check-ins and outcome measures. If progress is satisfactory, the process ends; if deterioration is detected, an escalation is triggered.
