Accommodation Allocation ()
## Purpose The Accommodation Allocation process assigns available residential spaces to eligible students and staff. It ensures that allocation decisions are fair, policy-compliant, and optimise the use of institutional housing stock. ## Roles - Applicant (Student/Staff) - Accommodation Officer - Accommodation Manager - System ## Upstream Inputs - Completed Accommodation Application - Room Inventory Data - Allocation Policy Rules - Applicant Preference Data ## Downstream Outputs - Confirmed Housing Assignment - Occupancy Record - Applicant Notification ## Key Decisions - Does the applicant meet eligibility criteria? - Is a suitable room available matching preferences? - Should the provisional assignment be approved? ## Exception Paths - Applicant fails eligibility check: application is rejected with reason notification. - No suitable room available: applicant is placed on a waiting list. - Manager rejects provisional assignment: allocation officer must rework the assignment. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Steps: 1. Receive Application: The applicant submits a completed accommodation application through the student portal. The system validates the submission for completeness and stores the application data for processing. Required fields include personal details, room preferences, and accommodation period. 2. Check Eligibility: The system applies institutional accommodation policy rules to the applicant's data. It checks criteria such as student status, year of study, distance from campus, and any special requirements. The result is either eligible or ineligible. 3. Review Room Inventory: The system retrieves the current room inventory and filters rooms based on the applicant's stated preferences, including room type, location, price range, and accessibility needs. A list of suitable available rooms is generated. 4. Assess Match: The accommodation officer reviews the list of suitable rooms alongside the applicant's preferences and any special notes. They select the most appropriate room, considering factors like roommate compatibility, floor, and proximity to facilities. 5. Make Provisional Assignment: The accommodation officer enters the selected room match into the system as a provisional assignment. This temporarily reserves the room and creates a pending assignment record awaiting approval. 6. Approve Assignment: The accommodation manager reviews the provisional assignment against policy, availability, and any exceptional circumstances. They either approve the assignment to proceed or reject it with comments for rework. 7. Rework Assignment: If the manager rejects the provisional assignment, the accommodation officer reviews the rejection comments, adjusts the room selection or assignment details, and resubmits for approval. 8. Confirm Assignment: Upon approval, the system finalises the housing assignment, updates the room occupancy status to occupied, and creates a permanent occupancy record linked to the applicant. 9. Notify Applicant: The system generates and sends a notification to the applicant via email and/or portal message, including room details, move-in date, and next steps.
