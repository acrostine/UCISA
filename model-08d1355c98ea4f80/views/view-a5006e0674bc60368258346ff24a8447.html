It consumes demographic insights, academic program information, and admission criteria to attract qualified candidates. The outcome includes increased domestic applications and enrolments, contributing to a balanced and diverse student population. Design Outreach Campaign: The Marketing Coordinator designs the outreach campaign, including messaging, creative assets, and channel selection. KPIs such as reach, engagement rate, and application conversion are defined. The campaign design is aligned with the target segment profiles produced in the previous step. 3. Execute Outreach Campaign: The Marketing Coordinator launches the campaign across digital and traditional channels as specified in the design. The system tracks engagement metrics in real-time. The campaign runs for a defined period, after which performance data is collected for assessment. 4. Assess Campaign Performance: The Recruitment Manager reviews the campaign performance data and compares it against the KPIs defined in the design phase. This assessment determines whether the campaign is meeting its objectives or requires adjustment. A decision is made to either proceed with the current campaign or initiate rework. 5. Rework Campaign Strategy: If the campaign is underperforming, the Marketing Coordinator reworks the strategy by adjusting messaging, channels, or budget allocation. The revised campaign is then re-executed. This step ensures continuous improvement and optimisation of recruitment efforts. 6. Receive Domestic Application: The system receives applications submitted by prospective students through the online portal. Each application is logged with a unique identifier and timestamp. Incomplete applications are flagged for follow-up. The system notifies the Admissions Officer of new applications. 7. Review Application Against Criteria: The Admissions Officer reviews the application documents and verifies them against the admission criteria. This includes checking academic transcripts, test scores, and any supplementary materials. The officer documents any missing items or discrepancies for follow-up. 8. Determine Admission Decision: Based on the review outcome, the Admissions Officer makes a final decision on the application. If the applicant meets all criteria, an acceptance decision is made. If the applicant is borderline, a waitlist decision may be applied. If criteria are not met, a rejection decision is recorded. 9. Extend Admission Offer: For accepted applicants, the system generates and sends an admission offer letter. The letter includes instructions for accepting the offer and completing enrolment steps. The system tracks whether the offer is accepted, declined, or expires. 10. Confirm Enrolment: The Enrolment Advisor verifies that the admitted student has accepted the offer, submitted required documents, and completed any pre-enrolment steps. Once all conditions are met, the student is officially enrolled. The system updates the student record and generates an enrolment confirmation.
