Commercial Tenancy ()
## Purpose This process governs the end-to-end management of commercial leasing for university-owned properties, from initial tenant inquiry through lease execution and tenant onboarding. It ensures that all tenancy arrangements are legally compliant, financially beneficial, and aligned with the university's strategic property objectives. ## Roles - Property Manager: Leads tenant evaluation, negotiation, and onboarding - Legal Counsel: Reviews and approves legal terms in tenancy agreements - Finance Officer: Validates financial terms and sets up rental billing - System: Automates credit checks and document generation ## Upstream Inputs - Market Analysis - Property Availability Data - Legal Frameworks - Tenant Application ## Downstream Outputs - Tenancy Agreement - Rental Income - Tenant Onboarding Record - Lease Register Entry ## Key Decisions - Tenant suitability assessment (creditworthiness, business reputation) - Lease term approval (duration, rental rate, special conditions) - Legal compliance check (clause validity, regulatory alignment) ## Exception Paths - Tenant application rejected due to poor credit or unsuitable business profile - Lease negotiation fails due to unresolvable term disagreements - Legal review identifies non-compliant clauses requiring renegotiation ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Tenant Inquiry: Property Manager logs the prospective tenant's expression of interest and captures initial requirements from the inquiry. 2. Assess Tenant Suitability: Property Manager evaluates the tenant's credit history, business reputation, and alignment with property use restrictions, producing a suitability recommendation. 3. Review Suitability Assessment: Legal Counsel reviews the suitability recommendation and either approves or rejects the tenant application. 4. Negotiate Lease Terms: Property Manager negotiates rental rate, lease duration, and special conditions with the tenant, producing draft lease terms. 5. Validate Financial Terms: Finance Officer reviews and validates the negotiated financial terms against market benchmarks and university policies. 6. Generate Tenancy Agreement: System generates the formal tenancy agreement document based on approved terms and legal templates. 7. Review Legal Compliance: Legal Counsel reviews the generated agreement for legal compliance and regulatory alignment, approving or flagging issues. 8. Execute Agreement: Property Manager facilitates signing of the tenancy agreement by both parties and records the executed document. 9. Onboard Tenant: Property Manager completes tenant onboarding including key handover, utility setup, and emergency contact registration. Steps: 1. Receive Tenant Inquiry: The Property Manager receives an inquiry from a prospective tenant via email, phone, or property portal. They capture the tenant's name, business type, space requirements, and preferred lease duration. The inquiry is logged into the property management system with a unique reference number. 2. Assess Tenant Suitability: The Property Manager runs a credit check and reviews the tenant's business history, references, and alignment with the property's permitted use. They compile a suitability assessment report with a recommendation to proceed or reject. 3. Review Suitability Assessment: Legal Counsel examines the suitability assessment report, focusing on legal risks, compliance with university policies, and any red flags. They either approve the tenant to proceed or reject the application with documented reasons. 4. Negotiate Lease Terms: The Property Manager engages in negotiations with the tenant covering rental rate, lease duration, renewal options, maintenance responsibilities, and any special conditions. Negotiations continue until both parties agree on draft terms or reach an impasse. 5. Validate Financial Terms: The Finance Officer checks the proposed rental rate against market benchmarks, reviews deposit and guarantee requirements, and confirms the financial viability of the lease. They either approve the terms or request adjustments. 6. Generate Tenancy Agreement: The property management system automatically generates a tenancy agreement document using approved terms, legal templates, and property-specific data. The document includes all clauses, schedules, and appendices required for a legally binding lease. 7. Review Legal Compliance: Legal Counsel examines the generated tenancy agreement to ensure all clauses comply with relevant laws, university policies, and regulatory requirements. They either approve the agreement for execution or flag non-compliant clauses for revision. 8. Execute Agreement: The Property Manager coordinates the signing process, ensuring both the university representative and the tenant execute the agreement. The signed document is scanned, uploaded to the property management system, and the lease register is updated. 9. Onboard Tenant: The Property Manager conducts a welcome meeting, provides keys and access cards, arranges utility connections, and registers emergency contacts. The tenant onboarding record is finalized and rental billing is initiated in the finance system.
