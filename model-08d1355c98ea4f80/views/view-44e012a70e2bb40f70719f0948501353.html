Financial Advisory ()
## Purpose The Financial Advisory process delivers expert analysis and strategic guidance to support financial decision-making and policy formulation. It consumes financial data, regulatory updates, and institutional objectives to produce strategic recommendations and risk assessments, enhancing decision-making, optimizing financial performance, and ensuring compliance with funding requirements. ## Roles - Financial Analyst: Gathers and reviews financial data and regulatory updates - Senior Advisor: Assesses risks and formulates strategic recommendations - Chief Financial Officer (CFO): Reviews and approves final recommendations - System: Automates data aggregation and report generation ## Upstream Inputs - Financial Data (from accounting systems and market feeds) - Regulatory Updates (from compliance and government sources) - Institutional Objectives (from strategic planning) ## Downstream Outputs - Strategic Recommendations (issued to executive leadership) - Risk Assessment Report (distributed to risk management and finance teams) ## Key Decisions - Whether financial data meets quality thresholds for analysis - Whether identified risks are acceptable or require mitigation - Whether the strategic recommendation is approved for issuance ## Exception Paths - If financial data is incomplete or inaccurate, the analyst initiates a data correction request and the process loops back to data gathering - If risk assessment identifies critical issues, the recommendation is revised before resubmission for approval - If the CFO rejects the recommendation, the senior advisor revises the analysis and resubmits ## Step Walkthrough 1. Gather Financial Data: The Financial Analyst collects financial data, regulatory updates, and institutional objectives from upstream sources. 2. Review Data Quality: The Financial Analyst checks the gathered data for completeness and accuracy; if issues are found, a data correction request is initiated. 3. Assess Financial Risks: The Senior Advisor evaluates financial risks against institutional thresholds and regulatory requirements. 4. Formulate Recommendation: The Senior Advisor develops a strategic recommendation and risk assessment report based on the analysis. 5. Review Recommendation: The CFO reviews the recommendation and risk report; if approved, it proceeds to issuance; if rejected, it is sent back for revision. 6. Issue Advisory Report: The System generates and distributes the final strategic recommendation and risk assessment report to stakeholders. Steps: 1. Gather Financial Data: The Financial Analyst retrieves financial data from accounting systems and market feeds, regulatory updates from compliance databases, and institutional objectives from strategic planning documents. All inputs are compiled into a single working dataset for analysis. 2. Review Data Quality: The Financial Analyst reviews the compiled dataset to ensure all required fields are present, values are within expected ranges, and no obvious errors exist. If quality issues are found, the data is flagged for correction. 3. Correct Data Issues: The Financial Analyst contacts upstream data providers to correct identified errors or fill missing fields. Once corrections are received, the dataset is updated and re-validated. 4. Assess Financial Risks: The Senior Advisor analyzes the validated dataset to identify financial risks, including liquidity, market, credit, and compliance risks. Each risk is rated against institutional risk appetite and regulatory standards. 5. Formulate Recommendation: The Senior Advisor synthesizes the risk assessment with institutional objectives to formulate a clear, actionable strategic recommendation. The recommendation includes options, expected outcomes, and risk mitigation strategies. 6. Review Recommendation: The CFO examines the draft recommendation and risk assessment report for alignment with institutional strategy, regulatory compliance, and financial soundness. The review results in either approval or a request for revision. 7. Revise Recommendation: The Senior Advisor incorporates the CFO's feedback into the draft recommendation, adjusting analysis, options, or risk assessments as needed. The revised document is then resubmitted for approval. 8. Issue Advisory Report: The System compiles the approved recommendation and risk assessment report into a final formatted document. The report is distributed to executive leadership, risk management, and finance teams via the advisory portal.
