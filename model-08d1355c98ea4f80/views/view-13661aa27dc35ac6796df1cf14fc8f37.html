Industry Relationship Mgt ()
## Purpose This process enables the organisation to systematically identify, engage, and manage relationships with industry partners. It ensures that collaborations are strategically aligned, legally sound, and mutually beneficial, ultimately enhancing research output, internship placements, and knowledge transfer. ## Roles - **Industry Liaison Officer**: Leads partner identification, initial outreach, and relationship management. - **Legal Counsel**: Reviews and approves partnership agreement terms and conditions. - **Head of Partnerships**: Authorises final agreements and approves joint initiative proposals. - **System**: Automates market intelligence gathering, document generation, and notification dispatch. ## Upstream Inputs - Market Intelligence Reports - Partner Feedback Data - Strategic Partnership Goals - Internship Demand Forecasts ## Downstream Outputs - Signed Partnership Agreements - Joint Initiative Proposals - Partner Performance Reviews - Updated Partner Database Records ## Key Decisions - Whether a potential partner meets strategic fit criteria - Whether partnership terms are acceptable to both parties - Whether a joint initiative proposal should be funded and launched - Whether a partnership should be renewed, modified, or terminated based on performance ## Exception Paths - Partner fails strategic fit assessment: relationship is not pursued; partner is logged for future review. - Legal review identifies unacceptable terms: negotiation is re-opened with revised terms. - Agreement authorisation is rejected: partnership is placed on hold; liaison officer notifies partner. - Partner performance falls below threshold: remediation plan is initiated; if unresolved, partnership is escalated for termination. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Identify Potential Partners**: Industry Liaison Officer reviews market intelligence and strategic goals to compile a list of candidate organisations. 2. **Assess Strategic Fit**: Liaison Officer evaluates each candidate against predefined criteria; partners that meet criteria proceed, others are logged for future review. 3. **Initiate Contact**: Liaison Officer reaches out to qualified partners to gauge interest and discuss collaboration opportunities. 4. **Negotiate Terms**: Liaison Officer and partner representative discuss scope, obligations, and resource commitments; draft terms are prepared. 5. **Review Agreement Legally**: Legal Counsel examines draft terms for compliance and risk; acceptable terms proceed, unacceptable terms are returned for renegotiation. 6. **Authorise Agreement**: Head of Partnerships reviews the final agreement and either approves or rejects it; approved agreements are signed. 7. **Generate Joint Initiatives**: Liaison Officer works with the partner to develop and submit joint initiative proposals for approval. 8. **Monitor Partnership Performance**: Liaison Officer tracks agreed metrics and collects partner feedback; performance is reviewed against targets. 9. **Conduct Performance Review**: Liaison Officer compiles a performance report and determines if the partnership meets expectations; successful partnerships are renewed, underperformers enter remediation. Steps: 1. Identify Potential Partners: The Industry Liaison Officer reviews market intelligence reports and strategic partnership goals to identify organisations that align with the institution's research and internship objectives. A preliminary list of potential partners is created for further evaluation. 2. Assess Strategic Fit: The Industry Liaison Officer assesses each candidate organisation against criteria such as industry relevance, research alignment, internship capacity, and reputation. Partners that meet the threshold proceed; those that do not are logged for future review. 3. Initiate Contact: The Industry Liaison Officer contacts each qualified partner via email or phone to introduce the collaboration opportunity, gauge interest, and schedule an exploratory meeting. Interest and initial feedback are recorded. 4. Negotiate Terms: The Industry Liaison Officer and the partner representative negotiate the scope of collaboration, including research projects, internship placements, resource contributions, and intellectual property terms. A draft agreement is prepared reflecting the agreed terms. 5. Review Agreement Legally: Legal Counsel reviews the draft partnership agreement to ensure compliance with institutional policies, regulatory requirements, and risk management standards. If terms are acceptable, the agreement is recommended for authorisation. If issues are found, the agreement is returned with required revisions. 6. Authorise Agreement: The Head of Partnerships reviews the final agreement, including legal clearance and strategic alignment. If approved, the agreement is signed and executed. If rejected, the partnership is placed on hold and the partner is notified. 7. Generate Joint Initiatives: The Industry Liaison Officer collaborates with the partner to identify specific joint initiatives such as research projects, internship programs, or knowledge exchange events. Proposals are documented and submitted to the Head of Partnerships for approval. 8. Monitor Partnership Performance: The Industry Liaison Officer monitors key performance indicators such as internship placements, research outputs, and partner satisfaction. Regular feedback is collected from the partner and recorded in the system. 9. Conduct Performance Review: The Industry Liaison Officer compiles a performance report based on metrics and feedback. If performance meets expectations, the partnership is recommended for renewal. If performance falls below threshold, a remediation plan is initiated. If remediation fails, the partnership is escalated for termination.
