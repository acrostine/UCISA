It integrates environmental data, policy requirements, and stakeholder input to produce sustainability reports and improvement plans. Collect Environmental Data: The Environmental Officer collects data on energy consumption, water usage, waste generation, and emissions from campus facilities. Stakeholder input from faculty, staff, and student groups is also gathered. All data is consolidated into a single dataset for analysis. 2. Assess Regulatory Compliance: The Compliance Analyst compares the consolidated dataset against applicable environmental regulations and university sustainability policies. This assessment identifies areas of full compliance and any gaps that require remediation. 3. Address Compliance Gaps: If compliance gaps are found, the Environmental Officer initiates corrective actions such as adjusting operational procedures, implementing new controls, or engaging with regulatory bodies. The remediation is documented and the data is updated for re-assessment. 4. Identify Improvement Opportunities: The Sustainability Manager reviews the compliance results and environmental data to identify opportunities for reducing environmental impact. This includes energy efficiency projects, waste reduction programs, and water conservation measures. 5. Prioritise Initiatives: The Sustainability Manager assesses each potential initiative based on environmental impact, cost, feasibility, and alignment with university goals. Initiatives are ranked and a prioritised list is produced for inclusion in the improvement plan. 6. Compile Sustainability Report: The System automatically compiles the prioritised initiative list, compliance results, and environmental data into a draft sustainability report and improvement plan. The report includes metrics, trends, and recommended actions. 7. Review and Approve Plan: The Sustainability Manager reviews the draft sustainability report and improvement plan for accuracy, completeness, and alignment with strategic goals. The plan is either approved for distribution or rejected with feedback for revision. 8. Revise Improvement Plan: If the plan is rejected, the Environmental Officer revises the improvement plan and report based on the feedback provided by the Sustainability Manager. The revised documents are resubmitted for re-review. 9. Distribute Report and Plan: The Environmental Officer distributes the approved sustainability report and improvement plan to university leadership, stakeholders, and operational teams. The distribution ensures awareness and initiates implementation of the improvement actions.
