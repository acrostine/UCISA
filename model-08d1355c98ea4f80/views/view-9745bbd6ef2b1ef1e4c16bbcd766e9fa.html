Gather Workforce Data: The HR Analyst extracts data from HR systems including employee headcount, turnover rates, retirement eligibility, and skills inventory. This step translates strategic objectives into projected staffing requirements by role and function. 3. Scan External Labour Market: The System automatically retrieves external labour market data including salary benchmarks, talent pool availability, and hiring trends for key roles. This data informs realistic staffing assumptions. 4. Generate Staffing Forecast: The System combines workforce baseline data, strategic requirements, and market intelligence to generate a staffing forecast. The forecast includes projected hires, promotions, and attrition for the planning period. 5. Validate Forecast: The HR Manager reviews the staffing forecast for accuracy and alignment with institutional strategy and market realities. If discrepancies are found, the forecast is sent back for rework. 6. Perform Gap Analysis: The HR Analyst compares the validated forecast against the current workforce data to identify surpluses, shortages, and skill gaps. The analysis produces a gap report highlighting critical areas. 7. Assess Budget Constraints: The Finance Officer reviews the gap analysis report and assesses the cost of filling identified gaps against the available budget. This step determines financial feasibility and flags constraints. 8. Develop Recruitment Plan: The HR Analyst develops a detailed recruitment plan that specifies roles to be filled, hiring timelines, sourcing strategies, and cost estimates. The plan incorporates budget constraints and gap priorities. 9. Approve Recruitment Plan: The CHRO reviews the recruitment plan for strategic alignment, budget compliance, and feasibility. If approved, the plan is finalized; if issues are found, it is returned for revision.
