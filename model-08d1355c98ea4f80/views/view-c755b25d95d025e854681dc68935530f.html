Graduation Event Mgt ()
## Purpose The Graduation Event Mgt process ensures that graduation ceremonies are planned and executed in a coordinated, professional manner. It transforms eligibility lists and venue data into a structured event schedule, invitations, and operational plans, delivering a positive ceremonial experience that reinforces alumni engagement and institutional reputation. ## Roles - Graduation Coordinator: Plans and coordinates all event logistics, reviews eligibility, and manages communications. - Events Team: Supports venue setup, resource allocation, and on-day execution. - Academic Records: Provides the eligibility list of graduating students. - Approver (e.g., Dean or Registrar): Reviews and approves the final event plan. - System: Automates invitation generation and distribution. ## Upstream Inputs - Eligibility List: List of students cleared for graduation. - Venue Availability: Calendar and capacity data for ceremony venues. - Logistical Details: Resource requirements, seating plans, AV needs. ## Downstream Outputs - Event Schedule: Timetable of ceremony sessions and rehearsals. - Invitations: Digital or printed invitations sent to eligible graduates. - Operational Plan: Detailed logistics plan including staffing, setup, and contingency. ## Key Decisions - Eligibility verification: Confirm each student meets graduation requirements. - Venue selection: Choose appropriate venue based on graduate count and date. - Resource allocation: Assign budget, staff, and equipment to ceremony components. - Plan approval: Authorize the final event schedule and operational plan. ## Exception Paths - Eligibility discrepancies: If a student is incorrectly listed, the coordinator contacts Academic Records for correction before proceeding. - Venue conflict: If the preferred venue is unavailable, an alternative venue is selected and the schedule adjusted. - Plan rejection: If the approver rejects the plan, the coordinator revises and resubmits. - Invitation delivery failure: Undelivered invitations trigger manual follow-up with the graduate. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Eligibility List: The Graduation Coordinator receives the official eligibility list from Academic Records. This list contains all students who have met academic requirements and are cleared to graduate. The coordinator checks the list for completeness and accuracy before proceeding. 2. Review Venue Availability: The coordinator reviews venue availability data, including dates, capacity, and any existing bookings. They select a primary venue and date that accommodates the expected graduate count and institutional calendar constraints. 3. Develop Preliminary Schedule: Using the eligibility list and venue selection, the coordinator drafts a preliminary schedule that includes ceremony start times, rehearsal slots, procession order, and key event milestones. This schedule is shared with the Events Team for input. 4. Allocate Resources: The coordinator works with the Events Team to allocate resources: budget lines, staffing assignments, AV equipment, seating arrangements, and ceremonial items (gowns, diplomas). All allocations are recorded in the operational plan. 5. Design Invitation: The coordinator designs the graduation invitation, including event date, time, venue, dress code, RSVP instructions, and guest limits. The design is reviewed for branding consistency and accuracy. 6. Review Event Plan: The coordinator compiles the preliminary schedule, resource allocation, and invitation draft into a comprehensive event plan. This plan is submitted to the Approver (Dean or Registrar) for review. The Approver may request changes or clarifications. 7. Approve Event Plan: The Approver evaluates the event plan against institutional standards, budget constraints, and risk factors. They either approve the plan, allowing it to proceed, or reject it with specific revision requests. 8. Distribute Invitations: Once the plan is approved, the system automatically generates and distributes invitations to all graduates on the eligibility list. Invitations include RSVP links and event details. Delivery status is tracked. 9. Confirm Operational Logistics: The coordinator and Events Team confirm all operational details: venue layout, seating charts, AV testing, staff assignments, and contingency plans for weather or emergencies. A final operations checklist is signed off. 10. Conduct Post-Event Review: After the ceremony, the coordinator collects feedback from graduates, staff, and stakeholders. A post-event report is compiled, highlighting successes, challenges, and recommendations for future events.
