Legal & Legislative Compliance ()
## Purpose Legal & Legislative Compliance ensures the organisation continuously monitors, interprets, and adheres to all applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies. It proactively identifies legal changes, assesses their operational impact, and implements necessary updates to procedures and communications, thereby safeguarding the institution against legal risks and promoting a culture of lawful operation. ## Roles - Legal Counsel: Reviews and interprets legislative updates, assesses impact, and drafts compliance guidance. - Compliance Officer: Coordinates the update of internal procedures and communicates obligations to stakeholders. - Head of Legal: Approves final compliance communications and guidance. - System: Automated notification and document management system. ## Upstream Inputs - Legislative Updates - Regulatory Guidance - Internal Procedure Documents - Policy Change Requests ## Downstream Outputs - Compliance Impact Assessment - Updated Internal Procedures - Compliance Communication - Compliance Obligations Register ## Key Decisions - Does the legislative change have a material impact on the organisation? - Are existing internal procedures adequate to address the new requirement? - Is the proposed compliance communication accurate and complete? ## Exception Paths - If a legislative change is assessed as having no material impact, the process ends with an informational note. - If the compliance communication is rejected by the Head of Legal, it is returned for revision and re-submission. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Update Internal Procedures: If a material impact is identified, the Compliance Officer revises the affected internal policies, procedures, or guidelines to align with the new legislative requirement. Changes are documented and version-controlled. 6. Draft Compliance Communication: Legal Counsel prepares a clear communication explaining the legislative change, its impact, and any new obligations for stakeholders. The draft includes effective dates and required actions. 7. Review and Approve Communication: The Head of Legal reviews the draft communication for accuracy, completeness, and alignment with organisational policy. They either approve it for release or return it with revision requests. 8. Communicate Compliance Obligations: The Compliance Officer sends the approved communication to all relevant stakeholders via email, intranet, or training systems. They also update the compliance obligations register to reflect the new requirement.
