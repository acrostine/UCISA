## Purpose This process ensures that all contracts entered into by the institution are legally sound, enforceable, and aligned with institutional policies and regulatory requirements. It covers the full lifecycle from initial request through drafting, review, negotiation, approval, execution, and archiving. ## Roles - Legal Counsel: Reviews terms, drafts language, negotiates with counterparties - Contract Manager: Submits requests, coordinates internal approvals, archives executed contracts - Approver (Senior Legal or Director): Provides final approval on contract terms - System: Automated notifications and document repository ## Upstream Inputs - Proposed Terms: Business requirements and draft terms from the requesting department - Institutional Policies: Current policies governing contracts and procurement - Regulatory Requirements: Applicable laws and regulations ## Downstream Outputs - Executed Contract: Final signed agreement - Contract Record: Archived contract with metadata for compliance tracking - Obligations Summary: Key obligations and deadlines extracted for monitoring ## Key Decisions - Whether proposed terms comply with institutional policies - Whether risk mitigation language is adequate - Whether to approve the contract as drafted or require further negotiation ## Exception Paths - Non-compliant terms: Sent back for revision with legal guidance - Negotiation impasse: Escalated to senior legal for resolution - Missing information: Request returned to Contract Manager for completion ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Contract Request: Legal Services receives the proposed terms and supporting documentation from the Contract Manager. 2. Review Proposed Terms: Legal Counsel reviews the terms against institutional policies and regulatory requirements, identifying gaps or risks. 3. Draft or Revise Contract: Legal Counsel drafts new contract language or revises existing language to address identified issues. 4. Negotiate with Counterparty: Legal Counsel exchanges revisions with the external party and negotiates final terms. 5. Approve Contract: The Approver reviews the negotiated contract and either approves or rejects it. 6. Execute Contract: Authorized signatories execute the final approved contract. 7. Archive Contract Record: The Contract Manager stores the executed contract and associated metadata in the contract repository. Steps: 1. Receive Contract Request: The Contract Manager submits a contract request containing proposed terms, business context, and any relevant background documents. Legal Services acknowledges receipt and logs the request into the tracking system. The request is triaged for priority and assigned to a Legal Counsel. 2. Review Proposed Terms: Legal Counsel examines each proposed term for compliance with institutional policies and applicable regulatory requirements. They identify potential legal risks, missing clauses, or ambiguous language. A preliminary risk assessment is documented. 3. Draft or Revise Contract: Based on the review assessment, Legal Counsel creates a revised version of the contract. This may involve drafting entirely new clauses, modifying existing language, or adding protective provisions. The revised draft is saved as a new version in the system. 4. Negotiate with Counterparty: Legal Counsel sends the revised draft to the counterparty and engages in negotiations. Multiple rounds of revisions may occur. All changes are tracked in the system. The goal is to reach mutually acceptable terms that still protect the institution's interests. 5. Approve Contract: The negotiated contract is submitted to the Approver (Senior Legal or Director) for final review. The Approver checks that all risks have been addressed and that the terms align with institutional strategy. They either approve the contract for execution or reject it with reasons. 6. Execute Contract: Once approved, the contract is routed to authorized signatories for execution. Signatures are collected (electronically or physically). The executed copy is date-stamped and saved as the final version. 7. Archive Contract Record: The executed contract is uploaded to the contract repository with all relevant metadata: parties, effective dates, expiration, key obligations, and approval history. The record is indexed for future retrieval and compliance monitoring. A notification is sent to relevant stakeholders.
