Compliance Monitoring & Reporting ()
## Purpose Compliance Monitoring & Reporting ensures the institution continuously tracks its adherence to external regulations and internal policies. By consuming legal frameworks, audit results, and operational data, the process identifies gaps, initiates corrective actions, and produces regular reports that support governance oversight and regulatory submissions. ## Roles - Compliance Officer: Leads monitoring, assessment, and reporting activities. - Data Steward: Provides operational data from departmental systems. - Internal Audit: Supplies audit results and findings. - Legal Counsel: Provides interpretation of regulatory requirements. - System: Automates data consolidation and report generation. ## Upstream Inputs - Legal Frameworks: Regulatory requirements and policy documents. - Audit Results: Findings from internal and external audits. - Operational Data: Departmental performance and process data. ## Downstream Outputs - Compliance Report: Final report distributed to governance committees. - Corrective Action Plan: Remediation plan for non-compliance issues. ## Key Decisions - Are compliance criteria met for each obligation? - Does the compliance level require escalation to senior management? - Is the report ready for final distribution? ## Exception Paths - If data is incomplete or missing, the process pauses and requests resubmission from the Data Steward. - If a compliance gap is identified, a corrective action loop is initiated before the report is finalized. - If the report contains critical violations, it is escalated to the Risk Committee for immediate attention. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Regulatory Data: The Compliance Officer retrieves the latest regulatory requirements, internal policy documents, and audit findings from the Legal Counsel and Internal Audit teams. Gather Operational Data: The Data Steward extracts operational data from various departmental systems, including transaction logs, process metrics, and control reports. This data is prepared for consolidation. 3. Consolidate Compliance Data: The System automatically merges the collected regulatory data and operational data into a single, structured dataset. It validates completeness and flags any missing fields for follow-up. 4. Assess Compliance Levels: The Compliance Officer reviews the consolidated dataset and compares each obligation against actual performance. This step identifies areas of adherence and non-compliance, producing a preliminary assessment. 5. Review Assessment Findings: The Compliance Officer examines the assessment results, verifying the accuracy of identified gaps and adherence. Based on the findings, a decision is made whether to proceed to reporting or to initiate corrective actions. 6. Initiate Corrective Action: For each non-compliance issue, the Compliance Officer drafts a corrective action plan, assigns responsibilities, and sets deadlines. This plan is logged in the system for tracking and closure. 7. Generate Compliance Report: The System compiles the assessment results and any corrective actions into a structured compliance report. The report includes summary statistics, gap analysis, and recommendations. 8. Distribute Compliance Report: The Compliance Officer distributes the final compliance report to the Board, Risk Committee, and relevant stakeholders. The report is archived for regulatory reference.
