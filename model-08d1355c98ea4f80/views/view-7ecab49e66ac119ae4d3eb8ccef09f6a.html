Student Engagement & Retention ()
## Purpose This process ensures that student engagement is actively monitored and enhanced through data-driven analysis of attendance and satisfaction metrics. It identifies students at risk of dropping out and deploys targeted programs and interventions to improve retention, satisfaction, and overall student success. ## Roles - Student Engagement Officer - Academic Advisor - Data Analyst - System (for automated data processing) ## Upstream Inputs - Attendance Data - Student Satisfaction Surveys - Historical Retention Records ## Downstream Outputs - Targeted Engagement Programs - Retention Interventions - Updated Risk Profiles ## Key Decisions - Determine if a student is at risk of attrition based on attendance and satisfaction thresholds - Decide which engagement program or intervention is appropriate for each risk level - Assess whether the intervention has been effective and if further action is needed ## Exception Paths - If attendance data is incomplete or missing, the process pauses for data validation before proceeding - If a student rejects the proposed intervention, the case is escalated to the Academic Advisor for alternative support - If an intervention fails to improve engagement after two cycles, the student is referred to specialised counselling services ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Attendance Data: The system automatically pulls attendance data for all enrolled students from the central LMS database. This data includes class attendance, lab participation, and tutorial attendance. The data is timestamped and validated for completeness before being stored for analysis. 2. Gather Satisfaction Surveys: The system sends out standardised satisfaction surveys to all students via email and the student portal. Responses are collected over a defined period and aggregated. Incomplete or duplicate responses are filtered out automatically. 3. Analyse Engagement Metrics: The Data Analyst combines validated attendance records and survey response data to compute individual engagement scores. Trends across cohorts are identified, and outliers are flagged. A summary report is generated for the Student Engagement Officer. 4. Assess Attrition Risk: Using the engagement score report, the Student Engagement Officer applies predefined risk thresholds to classify students as low, moderate, or high risk. The risk profile is updated in the student management system. This step determines the next course of action. 5. Design Engagement Program: For students identified as moderate risk, the Student Engagement Officer designs a program that may include mentoring, peer study groups, or extracurricular activities. The program is documented with goals, timelines, and success criteria. 6. Develop Retention Intervention: For high-risk students, the Academic Advisor conducts a one-on-one consultation to understand underlying issues. A personalised intervention plan is developed, which may include academic support, counselling referrals, or flexible study arrangements. 7. Implement Program or Intervention: The Student Engagement Officer or Academic Advisor executes the designed program or intervention. This may involve enrolling the student in activities, scheduling meetings, or providing resources. The student is informed and consent is obtained. 8. Monitor Effectiveness: After implementation, the system automatically collects new attendance and survey data for the student over a defined monitoring period. This data is compared against baseline metrics to measure change. 9. Review Outcomes: The Student Engagement Officer examines the monitoring results to determine if the intervention was effective. If engagement has improved, the case is closed. If not, the officer may loop back to design a new program or escalate to specialised services.
