Housing Advice ()
## Purpose This process delivers structured housing advice to students, covering accommodation options, tenancy rights, and welfare concerns. It uses local market data and student housing surveys to produce personalised guidance, ensuring students can make informed decisions and access additional support when needed. ## Roles - Student (initiator and recipient) - Housing Advisor (primary case handler) - Welfare Specialist (referral recipient) - System (automated data retrieval and notification) ## Upstream Inputs - Student Housing Enquiry - Student Housing Survey Results - Local Accommodation Market Information ## Downstream Outputs - Informed Housing Choice - Support Referral (if required) - Wellbeing Confirmation ## Key Decisions - Whether the student's housing situation requires a welfare specialist referral - Whether the advice provided is sufficient or further follow-up is needed ## Exception Paths - If the student's circumstances indicate serious welfare risk, a mandatory referral to the Welfare Specialist is triggered immediately. - If the student does not respond to the follow-up, the case is escalated to the Housing Advisor for proactive outreach. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Receive Housing Enquiry**: The student submits a housing enquiry via the student portal, which is logged in the case management system. 2. **Review Student Profile**: The Housing Advisor reviews the student's personal details, current accommodation status, and any prior housing notes. 3. **Gather Market Data**: The System automatically retrieves current local accommodation listings and student housing survey insights. 4. **Assess Housing Needs**: The Housing Advisor evaluates the student's requirements against the market data to identify suitable options and potential risks. 5. **Provide Tailored Advice**: The Housing Advisor delivers personalised guidance on accommodation choices, tenancy rights, and local resources. 6. **Determine Referral Need**: The Housing Advisor decides whether the student's situation requires referral to a Welfare Specialist for deeper support. 7. **Refer to Welfare Specialist**: If needed, the Housing Advisor creates a referral case and transfers it to the Welfare Specialist for follow-up. 8. **Record Informed Choice**: The student confirms their housing decision, which is documented in the system as an informed housing choice. 9. **Send Wellbeing Follow-Up**: The System sends a follow-up message to the student to check on their wellbeing and housing satisfaction. 10. **Close Housing Case**: The Housing Advisor reviews the follow-up response and closes the case, recording the final wellbeing outcome. Steps: 1. Receive Housing Enquiry: The student accesses the student portal and completes a housing enquiry form, providing details about their current accommodation, preferences, and any immediate concerns. The system logs the enquiry and assigns it to an available Housing Advisor. 2. Review Student Profile: The Housing Advisor accesses the student's record in the case management system, reviewing personal details, current accommodation status, previous housing interactions, and any flagged welfare notes. This provides context for the advice to be given. 3. Gather Market Data: The system automatically queries external databases for local accommodation listings, rental price trends, and the latest student housing survey results. This data is compiled into a market insights report for the advisor. 4. Assess Housing Needs: The Housing Advisor compares the student's requirements (budget, location, property type) with the market insights report. They identify suitable options, potential tenancy risks, and any welfare concerns that may need addressing. 5. Provide Tailored Advice: Based on the needs assessment, the Housing Advisor provides the student with tailored advice on accommodation options, tenancy rights, budgeting, and local resources. This may be delivered via phone, email, or in-person meeting. 6. Determine Referral Need: The Housing Advisor evaluates whether the student's circumstances indicate a need for specialist welfare support, such as financial hardship, mental health concerns, or safeguarding issues. This decision is based on the needs assessment and professional judgement. 7. Refer to Welfare Specialist: If a referral is needed, the Housing Advisor completes a referral form detailing the student's situation and the reason for referral. The case is transferred to the Welfare Specialist for further assessment and support. 8. Record Informed Choice: The student communicates their housing decision to the Housing Advisor, who records it in the system as an informed housing choice. This may include the property selected, tenancy signed, or decision to seek further advice. 9. Send Wellbeing Follow-Up: A few days after the housing decision is recorded, the system automatically sends a follow-up message to the student, asking about their wellbeing and housing satisfaction. The response is logged for review. 10. Close Housing Case: The Housing Advisor reviews the student's follow-up response. If wellbeing is confirmed and no further issues are raised, the case is closed in the system with a final wellbeing outcome recorded.
