Disability Support Mgt ()
## Purpose This process ensures that students with disabilities receive timely, appropriate, and legally compliant reasonable adjustments and specialised resources. It coordinates the intake of diagnostic evidence, the assessment of individual needs, the creation of a formal support plan, and its implementation across teaching and learning environments. ## Roles - Student: Submits disability notification and diagnostic evidence - Disability Support Officer: Validates documentation, assesses needs, drafts support plan - Disability Services Manager: Reviews and approves support plans - Academic Liaison: Receives and implements accommodations in learning environments - System: Stores records, sends notifications, manages workflow ## Upstream Inputs - Student Disability Notification - Diagnostic Assessment Reports - Individual Needs Data - Existing Support History (if applicable) ## Downstream Outputs - Customised Support Plan (approved) - Accessibility Accommodations List - Implementation Notification to Academic Staff - Student Support Record (updated) ## Key Decisions - Whether the submitted diagnostic documentation is valid and sufficient - What specific reasonable adjustments are appropriate for the student's needs - Whether the draft support plan meets all requirements and is ready for approval - Whether the plan requires revision after implementation feedback ## Exception Paths - Invalid or insufficient documentation: Student is notified and asked to resubmit - Plan rejected during review: Returned to Disability Support Officer for rework - Implementation issues reported: Plan is revised and re-approved ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Disability Notification: The student initiates the process by submitting a formal disability notification along with supporting diagnostic assessment reports and individual needs data through the student portal. The system logs the request and assigns it to a Disability Support Officer for initial processing. 2. Validate Documentation: The Disability Support Officer examines the submitted diagnostic assessments and needs data to ensure they are complete, current, and sufficient to determine reasonable adjustments. If documentation is invalid or insufficient, the officer initiates a request for resubmission. 3. Assess Individual Needs: Based on the validated documentation, the Disability Support Officer conducts a thorough assessment of the student's functional limitations in the learning environment. This assessment considers the nature of the disability, course requirements, and potential barriers to participation. 4. Determine Adjustments: Using the needs assessment, the officer determines the specific reasonable adjustments and specialised resources required. This may include extended exam time, accessible materials, note-taking support, or physical access accommodations. A draft list of accommodations is produced. 5. Draft Support Plan: The Disability Support Officer compiles a formal customised support plan that includes all determined adjustments, implementation timelines, responsible parties, and review dates. The plan is documented in the system and prepared for managerial review. 6. Review Support Plan: The Disability Services Manager examines the draft support plan to ensure it meets legal requirements, institutional policies, and adequately addresses the student's needs. The manager either approves the plan or identifies issues requiring revision. 7. Approve Support Plan: Upon satisfactory review, the Disability Services Manager formally approves the support plan. The system updates the student's record with the approved plan and generates notifications to relevant academic and support staff. 8. Implement Accommodations: The Academic Liaison receives the approved support plan and coordinates with teaching staff to implement the specified accommodations in the learning environment. Implementation is confirmed in the system, and the student is notified of the arrangements. 9. Resubmit Documentation: When documentation is found invalid or insufficient, the student is notified of the gaps and asked to resubmit the required documents. The student uploads corrected or additional diagnostic evidence through the portal. 10. Rework Support Plan: When the Disability Services Manager identifies issues during review, the plan is returned to the Disability Support Officer. The officer revises the plan to address the feedback and resubmits it for another review cycle.
