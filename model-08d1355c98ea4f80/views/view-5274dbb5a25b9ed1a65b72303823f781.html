Professional Learning (Staff) ()
## Purpose This process ensures academic staff have continuous access to professional learning opportunities that enhance their teaching skills, keep them current with pedagogical innovations, and align with the institution's strategic priorities. It transforms staff feedback, research insights, and institutional goals into structured learning activities that improve curriculum delivery and support career development. ## Roles - **Professional Development Coordinator**: Oversees the design, scheduling, and delivery of learning activities. - **Academic Staff**: Participate in learning activities and provide feedback on needs and outcomes. - **Head of Department**: Reviews and approves learning plans to ensure alignment with departmental priorities. - **System**: Manages registration, attendance tracking, and evaluation surveys. ## Upstream Inputs - Staff Feedback (surveys, performance reviews) - Educational Research (latest pedagogy and curriculum delivery studies) - Institutional Priorities (strategic goals, teaching excellence framework) - Departmental Training Needs Analysis ## Downstream Outputs - Professional Learning Calendar - Staff Training Records - Learning Activity Evaluations - Updated Teaching Practices - Career Progression Plans ## Key Decisions - Which learning topics are most critical based on feedback and research? - Should a learning activity be approved as designed or require revision? - Has a staff member met the learning outcomes to receive credit? ## Exception Paths - If a proposed learning activity does not align with institutional priorities, it is returned for revision. - If a staff member fails to meet learning outcomes, they are offered remedial support or alternative activities. - If participation is low, the activity may be rescheduled or cancelled. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Collect Development Needs**: The Professional Development Coordinator gathers staff feedback, reviews educational research, and analyses institutional priorities to compile a list of development needs. 2. **Design Learning Activities**: Based on the identified needs, the Coordinator designs specific learning activities, including workshops, seminars, and online modules. 3. **Review Learning Plan**: The Head of Department reviews the proposed learning plan to ensure alignment with departmental and institutional goals. 4. **Approve or Revise Plan**: The Head of Department either approves the plan or returns it with revision requests. 5. **Schedule and Promote Activities**: The Coordinator schedules the approved activities and promotes them to academic staff via email and the learning management system. 6. **Deliver Learning Sessions**: Academic staff attend the scheduled sessions, which are delivered by internal or external facilitators. 7. **Assess Participant Outcomes**: The Coordinator evaluates participant learning through assessments, surveys, or observed practice changes. 8. **Record and Report Results**: The System records attendance, assessment results, and evaluation data, generating reports for departmental review. Steps: 1. Collect Development Needs: The Professional Development Coordinator collects input from multiple sources: staff surveys and performance reviews, recent educational research publications, and the institution's strategic priorities. This data is compiled into a needs assessment document that highlights gaps and opportunities for professional growth. 2. Design Learning Activities: Using the needs assessment, the Coordinator designs a set of learning activities such as workshops, seminars, online courses, or peer coaching programs. Each activity includes learning objectives, content outline, delivery method, duration, and assessment criteria. 3. Review Learning Plan: The Head of Department examines the proposed learning plan to ensure it aligns with departmental teaching priorities, budget constraints, and the institution's strategic goals. They may request clarifications or modifications before making a decision. 4. Approve or Revise Plan: The Head of Department makes a formal decision. If the plan meets all criteria, it is approved. If issues are found, the plan is returned to the Coordinator with specific revision requests. 5. Revise Learning Plan: The Professional Development Coordinator updates the learning plan to address the revision requests from the Head of Department. The revised plan is then resubmitted for review. 6. Schedule and Promote Activities: Once the plan is approved, the Coordinator sets dates, times, and venues for each activity, and promotes them through email announcements, the learning management system, and departmental meetings. Registration is opened. 7. Deliver Learning Sessions: Internal or external facilitators deliver the learning sessions according to the schedule. Academic staff participate in the activities, which may include lectures, hands-on workshops, or online modules. Attendance is recorded. 8. Assess Participant Outcomes: The Coordinator administers assessments, surveys, or observes practice changes to determine if participants have met the learning outcomes. Results are compiled for each participant. 9. Record and Report Results: The System updates staff training records with attendance, assessment scores, and evaluation feedback. Reports are generated for departmental review, showing participation rates, outcomes, and areas for improvement.
