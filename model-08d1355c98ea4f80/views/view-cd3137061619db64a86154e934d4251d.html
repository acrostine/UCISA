## Purpose Treasury Management ensures the institution maintains optimal liquidity, maximises returns on idle cash, and secures cost-effective debt financing. It transforms cash forecasts, investment opportunities, and borrowing needs into cash management reports and funding strategies that support financial stability and risk mitigation. ## Roles - Treasury Analyst: Prepares cash forecasts and analyses investment opportunities - Treasury Manager: Reviews liquidity assessments and approves funding decisions - Chief Financial Officer (CFO): Authorises major debt financing and investment allocations - System: Automates data collection, report generation, and compliance checks ## Upstream Inputs - Cash Forecasts: Projected inflows and outflows from operational units - Investment Opportunities: Available short-term and long-term investment instruments - Borrowing Needs: Debt requirements from capital planning and operational budgets ## Downstream Outputs - Cash Management Reports: Daily/weekly liquidity positions and cash flow summaries - Funding Strategies: Approved plans for debt issuance, investment allocation, and risk hedging ## Key Decisions - Liquidity threshold: Minimum cash reserve level to maintain - Investment selection: Which instruments to invest in based on risk/return profile - Debt financing source: Whether to use bank loans, bonds, or internal funds - Approval authority: Which funding decisions require CFO sign-off ## Exception Paths - Insufficient liquidity: Triggers emergency borrowing or asset liquidation - Investment below threshold: Rejected and reworked with alternative options - Debt terms unfavourable: Escalated to CFO for renegotiation or alternative sourcing - Compliance breach: Investment or borrowing halted until issues resolved ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Cash Forecasts: The Treasury Analyst retrieves cash forecasts from operational departments and financial systems. Data includes expected receivables, payables, payroll, and capital expenditures. Forecasts are consolidated into a single view for analysis. 2. Assess Liquidity Position: The Treasury Analyst compares the consolidated forecast against current cash reserves. Liquidity gaps or surpluses are identified. A preliminary liquidity assessment is documented for review. 3. Review Investment Opportunities: The Treasury Analyst evaluates short-term and long-term investment options such as money market funds, bonds, and term deposits. Each option is assessed for yield, credit risk, and liquidity. A shortlist of suitable investments is prepared. 4. Evaluate Debt Financing Options: The Treasury Manager examines borrowing requirements from capital plans and operational budgets. Options include bank loans, bond issuance, and internal borrowing. Costs, terms, and covenants are compared to identify the most favourable source. 5. Approve Funding Strategy: The CFO receives the liquidity assessment, investment shortlist, and debt financing recommendation. A consolidated funding strategy is reviewed for alignment with institutional risk appetite and financial goals. Approval or revision request is issued. 6. Execute Investment Allocation: Based on the approved funding strategy, the system automatically places trades for selected investment instruments. Portfolio records are updated with new holdings. Confirmation receipts are generated. 7. Secure Debt Financing: The Treasury Manager executes the approved debt financing, signing agreements and coordinating with lenders. Funds are disbursed to operational accounts. Loan records are updated in the debt management system. 8. Generate Cash Management Report: The system aggregates data from executed investments, debt confirmations, and current cash balances. A standard cash management report is produced showing liquidity position, portfolio performance, and debt status. 9. Distribute Funding Strategy: The Treasury Manager distributes the approved funding strategy and cash management report to relevant stakeholders including the CFO, business units, and auditors. Documents are archived for compliance and audit trail.
