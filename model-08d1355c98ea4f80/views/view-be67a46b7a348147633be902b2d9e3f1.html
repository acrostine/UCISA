Assessment Marking & Feedback ()
## Purpose Assessment Marking & Feedback manages the end-to-end grading of submitted student work, from receipt of the submission through to the release of marks and feedback. It ensures that grading is fair, consistent, and aligned with published criteria, and that students receive actionable feedback to guide their learning. The process supports academic quality assurance through moderation and provides an auditable record of all assessment outcomes. ## Roles - **Marker**: Reviews submissions, assigns grades, and writes feedback. - **Moderator**: Reviews a sample of marked work to ensure consistency and fairness. - **System**: Automates grade recording, feedback distribution, and notification. - **Student**: Receives marks and feedback (external role, not modelled as a step). ## Upstream Inputs - Completed Assessment Submission - Marking Criteria / Rubric - Submission Register ## Downstream Outputs - Marked Script (with annotations) - Grade Record (in student information system) - Feedback Report (released to student) - Moderation Log ## Key Decisions - Does the marking meet the required standard? (Moderation decision) - Is the grade final and ready for release? (Marker confirmation) ## Exception Paths - If moderation identifies a significant discrepancy, the script is returned to the marker for re-marking. - If the submission is incomplete or illegible, it is flagged for review before marking proceeds. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Receive Submission**: The marker receives the completed assessment submission and confirms it is ready for marking. 2. **Review Against Criteria**: The marker reads the submission and evaluates it against the published marking criteria. 3. **Assign Grade**: The marker determines the grade and records the mark in the system. 4. **Prepare Feedback**: The marker writes constructive feedback comments aligned with the criteria and the student's performance. 5. **Submit for Moderation**: The marked script and feedback are submitted to the moderator for quality review. 6. **Moderate Marking**: The moderator reviews a sample of the marking to check consistency and fairness. 7. **Confirm Grade**: The marker confirms the final grade after moderation feedback is received. 8. **Release Results**: The system releases the grade and feedback report to the student. Steps: 1. Receive Submission: The marker accesses the submission queue in the assessment management system and retrieves the next submission. They verify the submission is complete and legible. If issues are found, the submission is flagged for administrative review. Otherwise, marking proceeds. 2. Review Against Criteria: The marker reads the submission thoroughly and compares each section against the marking criteria or rubric. They note strengths, weaknesses, and evidence of learning outcomes. This step forms the basis for the grade and feedback. 3. Assign Grade: Based on the evaluation, the marker selects the appropriate grade from the grading scale. The grade is entered into the assessment system along with any required justification. The system validates the grade against the criteria. 4. Prepare Feedback: The marker drafts feedback comments that explain the grade, highlight areas of strength, and suggest specific improvements. Feedback is aligned to the criteria and written in a constructive tone. The feedback is saved as a draft in the system. 5. Submit for Moderation: The marker flags the submission as ready for moderation. The system selects a sample of submissions for moderation based on predefined rules (e.g., first marking, borderline grades). The submission is queued for the moderator. 6. Moderate Marking: The moderator accesses the submission, grade, and feedback. They assess whether the grade is appropriate and the feedback is constructive. If the marking meets the standard, it is approved. If discrepancies are found, the submission is returned for re-marking. 7. Confirm Grade: If moderation was approved, the marker confirms the grade as final. If the submission was returned for re-marking, the marker adjusts the grade and feedback based on moderator comments, then resubmits. This step ensures the final grade is accurate. 8. Release Results: The system publishes the final grade to the student record and sends the feedback report to the student via the learning management system. A notification is sent to the student. The process ends with an auditable record of the outcome.
