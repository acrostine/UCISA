Gather Input Data: The System automatically retrieves performance data from HR systems, career aspirations from employee profiles, and competency frameworks from the organisational repository. This data forms the foundation for all subsequent talent assessments. 2. Assess High-Potential Candidates: The Talent Specialist reviews the consolidated data to identify employees who demonstrate high performance, leadership potential, and alignment with organisational needs. This step produces a list of high-potential candidates. 3. Flag for Development: Employees who do not meet the high-potential criteria are flagged in the system for development. They will be re-assessed in the next cycle after completing recommended training or growth activities. 4. Identify Succession Candidates: The Talent Specialist maps high-potential employees to critical positions based on skills, experience, and career aspirations. This step produces a draft succession plan. 5. Review Succession Plan: The HR Manager examines the draft succession plan for alignment with strategic workforce needs and leadership continuity. If approved, the plan moves to career path planning; if not, it is sent back for rework. 6. Rework Succession Plan: The Talent Specialist updates the draft succession plan to address the HR Manager's concerns, such as candidate suitability or role alignment. The revised plan is then resubmitted for review. 7. Plan Career Paths: The Line Manager, in consultation with the Talent Specialist, creates detailed career path plans for each succession candidate. These plans include development activities, timelines, and milestones aligned with organisational needs. 8. Approve Career Path Plans: The HR Manager reviews the draft career path plans to ensure they are realistic, aligned with competency frameworks, and support leadership continuity. If approved, the process finalises; if rejected, the plans are revised. 9. Revise Career Path Plans: The Line Manager revises the career path plans to incorporate the HR Manager's feedback, such as adjusting timelines or adding development activities. The updated plans are resubmitted for approval. 10. Finalise Talent Plan: The System compiles the high-potential list, approved succession plan, and approved career path plans into a single talent management plan. This is distributed to stakeholders and stored for future reference.
