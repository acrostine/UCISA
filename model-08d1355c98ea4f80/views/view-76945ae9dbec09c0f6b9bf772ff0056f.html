## Purpose This process ensures that staff competency gaps are systematically addressed through structured learning programs. It aligns training investments with institutional objectives, tracks certification outcomes, and uses evaluation feedback to continuously improve the learning portfolio. ## Roles - HR Training Coordinator - Department Manager - Training Provider (External) - System (Learning Management System) ## Upstream Inputs - Development Requests - Staff Competency Gap Reports - Institutional Objectives - External Training Resources Catalogue ## Downstream Outputs - Approved Training Plan - Training Schedule - Attendance Records - Assessment Results - Certification Records - Evaluation Feedback ## Key Decisions - Which competency gaps to prioritise based on institutional objectives - Whether to use internal or external training resources - Approval of the training plan by Department Manager - Whether a participant passes the assessment - Whether the program meets effectiveness criteria ## Exception Paths - If the training plan is rejected, it is reworked and resubmitted. - If a participant fails the assessment, a remediation plan is initiated. - If program effectiveness criteria are not met, the program is redesigned. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Development Requests: HR Training Coordinator gathers development requests from staff and managers, along with competency gap reports from performance reviews. All requests are logged into the Learning Management System for tracking. 2. Analyse Competency Gaps: HR Training Coordinator analyses the logged requests and gap reports, mapping them to institutional objectives. A prioritised list of training needs is produced, considering urgency and strategic alignment. 3. Design Learning Program: Based on prioritised needs, HR Training Coordinator designs the learning program, defining learning objectives, content outline, duration, and delivery method (e.g., workshop, e-learning, external course). External training resources are consulted as needed. 4. Review and Approve Plan: The draft training plan is submitted to the Department Manager for review. The manager assesses alignment with objectives, budget, and resource availability, then either approves or rejects the plan. 5. Rework Training Plan: If the plan is rejected, HR Training Coordinator revises the plan addressing the manager's concerns and resubmits for approval. 6. Schedule Training Sessions: HR Training Coordinator schedules the approved training, booking venues, arranging facilitators (internal or external), and notifying participants. The schedule is published in the LMS. 7. Deliver Training: The training provider (internal or external) delivers the session. Attendance is recorded in the LMS by the HR Training Coordinator or automatically via check-in. 8. Conduct Assessment: After training, participants take an assessment (quiz, practical, or project). Results are recorded in the LMS. The system determines pass/fail based on predefined criteria. 9. Initiate Remediation: For participants who fail, HR Training Coordinator initiates a remediation plan, which may include additional study materials, retraining, or a retake. 10. Evaluate Program Effectiveness: HR Training Coordinator reviews participant feedback surveys and overall pass rates. The program is assessed against effectiveness criteria (e.g., pass rate > 80%, satisfaction score > 4/5). 11. Redesign Program: If the program does not meet effectiveness criteria, HR Training Coordinator redesigns the program, addressing identified weaknesses, and loops back to the design step. 12. Update Certification Records: System updates each participant's certification record in the LMS, marking completion and expiry dates for those who passed. This step is terminal.
