Remuneration & Entitlements Mgt ()
## Purpose This process ensures that all employee remuneration and entitlements are calculated, approved, and distributed accurately and in compliance with organisational policies and legal regulations. It covers salary administration, benefits distribution, and entitlement tracking from data collection through to payment confirmation and record keeping. ## Roles - HR Officer: Collects and validates time reports and contractual data - Compensation Analyst: Calculates entitlements using business rules - HR Manager: Reviews and approves calculated entitlements - Payroll Specialist: Processes payroll and distributes payments - System: Automates rule-based calculations and sends notifications ## Upstream Inputs - Time Reporting Data - Employment Contracts - Compensation Policies - Regulatory Frameworks ## Downstream Outputs - Approved Payroll Run - Payment Confirmations - Archived Remuneration Records ## Key Decisions - Whether time reports and contracts are complete and valid - Whether calculated entitlements meet policy and regulatory thresholds - Whether to approve the payroll run for distribution - Whether payment discrepancies require rework ## Exception Paths - Incomplete or invalid time reports or contracts are returned for correction - Entitlement calculations that fail policy checks are flagged for rework - Payment discrepancies trigger a rework loop back to payroll processing ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Time & Contract Data: The HR Officer retrieves time reporting data from the time management system and employment contracts from the HR document repository. This data forms the basis for all subsequent entitlement calculations. The officer ensures all required records for the current pay period are available. 2. Validate Input Data: The HR Officer reviews the collected time reports and contracts for completeness, accuracy, and consistency with compensation policies. Missing or inconsistent records are flagged for correction. This step ensures only valid data proceeds to calculation. 3. Calculate Entitlements: The Compensation Analyst uses the validated data and applies business rules defined in the compensation policy to calculate gross pay, deductions, benefits, and other entitlements. The system performs automated checks against regulatory frameworks to ensure compliance. 4. Review Entitlements: The HR Manager examines the calculated entitlements to verify they align with contractual agreements and compensation policies. Any anomalies or policy violations are identified. This step serves as a quality gate before payroll approval. 5. Approve Payroll Run: The HR Manager provides final sign-off on the reviewed entitlements, authorising the payroll run for distribution. Approval confirms that all calculations are accurate and compliant. Rejected entitlements are sent back for correction. 6. Process Payroll Distribution: The Payroll Specialist processes the approved payroll run through the payroll system, generating payment instructions for salaries and benefits. Payments are distributed via direct deposit or other agreed methods. The system records all transactions. 7. Confirm Payment Receipt: The system checks payment status from banking partners and confirms successful delivery to employees. Any failed or discrepant payments are flagged for investigation. Confirmed payments update the remuneration records. 8. Archive Remuneration Records: The HR Officer archives all remuneration-related records including time data, contracts, calculations, approvals, and payment confirmations in the document management system. Archived records are retained per regulatory requirements. 9. Correct Input Data: The HR Officer corrects any issues identified during validation, such as missing time entries or contract discrepancies. Corrected data is resubmitted for validation. 10. Resolve Entitlement Issues: The Compensation Analyst investigates and resolves issues found during review, such as miscalculations or policy violations. Corrections are applied and entitlements are recalculated. 11. Resolve Payment Discrepancies: The Payroll Specialist investigates payment failures or discrepancies reported during confirmation. Corrections are made and payments are reprocessed.
