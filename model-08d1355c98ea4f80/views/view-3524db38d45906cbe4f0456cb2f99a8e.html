## Purpose Research Funds Management ensures that awarded research grants are administered within financial constraints and in full compliance with funder regulations. The process establishes budgets, monitors spending, and produces compliance reports to support transparent financial stewardship and maximise the impact of research funding on institutional objectives. ## Roles - Research Finance Officer - Principal Investigator - Compliance Manager - System ## Upstream Inputs - Awarded Grant Agreement - Financial Regulations - Institutional Budget Policies ## Downstream Outputs - Budget Allocation - Expenditure Tracking Report - Compliance Report - Corrective Action Plan ## Key Decisions - Is the budget allocation within approved limits? - Do expenditures match the budget allocation? - Are discrepancies within acceptable tolerance? ## Exception Paths - Budget allocation exceeds approved limits: rework budget with Principal Investigator - Expenditure discrepancy found: initiate corrective action and re-track - Discrepancy unresolved after corrective action: escalate to Compliance Manager ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Grant Agreement: The Research Finance Officer receives the formal awarded grant agreement from the funding body along with applicable financial regulations. These documents define the funding amount, allowable costs, reporting requirements, and compliance conditions. The officer verifies receipt and logs the documents into the financial management system. 2. Establish Budget Allocation: The Research Finance Officer analyses the grant agreement and institutional budget policies to create a detailed budget allocation. This includes categorising funds into personnel, equipment, travel, and indirect costs. The draft budget is prepared for compliance review. 3. Review Budget Compliance: The Compliance Manager reviews the draft budget allocation against financial regulations and funder requirements. They check for allowable costs, spending limits, and policy alignment. The review results in either approval or a request for revision. 4. Allocate Funds to Project: Upon compliance approval, the System automatically allocates the budgeted funds to the specific research project account. This creates a financial baseline for expenditure tracking and ensures funds are reserved for the project. 5. Track Expenditures: The System continuously records all financial transactions against the project account, including purchases, payroll, and travel costs. Expenditures are categorised and compared to the budget allocation in real time. 6. Review Expenditure Report: The Principal Investigator reviews the expenditure tracking report generated by the System. They verify that all charges are accurate, allowable, and within budget. If discrepancies are found, the report is flagged for resolution. 7. Resolve Discrepancies: The Research Finance Officer investigates flagged discrepancies, such as unauthorised spending or coding errors. They correct records, adjust allocations, or initiate reallocation as needed. The updated expenditure data is then re-tracked. 8. Generate Compliance Report: The System compiles all expenditure data, budget allocations, and compliance checks into a formal compliance report. The report includes financial summaries, variance analysis, and certification of adherence to funder requirements. 9. Submit Compliance Report: The Research Finance Officer reviews the generated compliance report for completeness and accuracy, then submits it to the funding body and institutional leadership. This step finalises the reporting cycle for the grant period.
