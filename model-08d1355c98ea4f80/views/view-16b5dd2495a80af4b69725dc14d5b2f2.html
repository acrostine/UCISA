Non Presence Based Teaching ()
## Purpose Non Presence Based Teaching delivers educational experiences through online, blended, and distance learning methods, producing accessible and flexible course delivery. It consumes digital learning materials, student data, and virtual communication tools to facilitate engagement. This process enhances inclusivity and supports diverse learner needs by removing geographical and time constraints, thereby increasing overall student participation and satisfaction. ## Roles - Course Instructor - Instructional Designer - System (LMS Platform) - Student ## Upstream Inputs - Digital Learning Materials - Student Data (enrolment, profiles) - Virtual Communication Tools (video conferencing, forums) - Course Schedule ## Downstream Outputs - Published Course Content - Engagement Records - Participation Assessments - Student Feedback ## Key Decisions - Is the virtual learning environment ready for content publication? - Is the published content complete and accurate? - Are students engaging adequately with the course? ## Exception Paths - If the virtual learning environment is not ready, the environment setup is reworked. - If published content has issues, content is reworked and re-published. - If student engagement is below threshold, targeted outreach is initiated. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Prepare Virtual Environment: The instructional designer sets up the LMS course structure, creates module folders, enables discussion forums, configures gradebook, and integrates video conferencing links. Review Environment Readiness: The instructor logs into the LMS, navigates through each module, verifies that all links work, checks that tools are enabled, and confirms the course schedule is correctly reflected. A readiness checklist is completed. 3. Rework Environment Setup: Based on the instructor's feedback, the instructional designer corrects configuration errors, adds missing tools, or adjusts module structure. The environment is then re-submitted for review. 4. Publish Course Content: The instructor uploads lecture videos, readings, slide decks, assignment briefs, and quiz questions into the appropriate LMS modules. Content is organised by week or topic and set to visible to students. 5. Validate Published Content: The instructional designer spot-checks uploaded materials for correct file formats, broken links, accurate metadata, and alignment with learning objectives. Any discrepancies are logged. 6. Rework Course Content: The instructor replaces incorrect files, fixes broken links, updates metadata, or re-uploads missing materials. Corrections are saved and content is re-published. 7. Facilitate Online Engagement: The instructor hosts weekly live video lectures, posts discussion prompts, responds to student queries in forums, and sends announcements. Engagement is monitored through participation metrics. 8. Assess Student Participation: The LMS analytics engine generates participation reports showing login counts, content views, forum posts, and assignment submission rates. These are compared against a predefined engagement threshold. 9. Initiate Engagement Outreach: The instructor sends personalised emails or LMS messages to students flagged as low-engagement, offering support, reminders, or additional resources to re-engage them in the course. 10. Collect Student Feedback: The LMS automatically sends a mid-course feedback survey to all enrolled students. Responses are aggregated into a feedback report for instructor review and continuous improvement.
