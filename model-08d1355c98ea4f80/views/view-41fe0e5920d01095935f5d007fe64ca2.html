Staff Absence Mgt ()
## Purpose This process governs the end-to-end management of employee absences, from initial notification through to return-to-work. It ensures that all absences are recorded accurately, approved in line with policy, and that operational capacity is maintained through proactive monitoring of attendance patterns. ## Roles - Employee: Submits absence requests and provides medical certificates - Manager: Reviews and approves/rejects absence requests, conducts return-to-work interviews - HR Administrator: Records approved absences, monitors attendance patterns - System: Validates requests against policy and schedule, sends notifications ## Upstream Inputs - Absence Requests - Medical Certificates - Workforce Schedules - Leave Policy Rules ## Downstream Outputs - Absence Records - Leave Approvals - Return-to-Work Plans - Attendance Pattern Reports ## Key Decisions - Whether the absence request meets policy criteria (entitlement, notice, documentation) - Whether to approve or reject the absence based on operational impact - Whether a return-to-work plan is required (medical absences only) - Whether attendance patterns indicate a need for intervention ## Exception Paths - Rejected absence: Employee is notified and may resubmit with additional information - Invalid medical certificate: Manager requests a valid certificate before proceeding - Non-compliance with return-to-work plan: Escalated to HR for corrective action ## Step Walkthrough 1. Monitor Attendance Patterns: HR Administrator reviews absence records to identify trends and flag potential issues. Steps: 1. Submit Absence Request: The employee initiates the process by submitting an absence request through the HR portal. The request includes the dates of absence, reason (e.g., annual leave, sick leave, personal leave), and optionally a medical certificate for sick leave. The system captures the request and notifies the manager. 2. Validate Request: The system automatically checks the absence request against leave policy rules, including entitlement balance, minimum notice periods, and workforce schedule constraints. If the request is valid, it proceeds to the manager for review. If invalid, the employee is notified with reasons. 3. Review Absence Request: The manager reviews the validated absence request, considering operational impact, team coverage, and any additional context. The manager can approve the request, reject it with a reason, or request further information from the employee. 4. Record Approved Absence: Upon approval, the HR Administrator enters the absence details into the HR system, updating the employee's leave balance and creating a permanent absence record. The system sends a confirmation to the employee and manager. 5. Create Return-to-Work Plan: If the absence was due to a medical reason, the manager and employee collaborate to create a return-to-work plan. The plan outlines phased duties, review dates, and any accommodations needed. The plan is recorded in the HR system. 6. Monitor Attendance Patterns: The HR Administrator periodically reviews absence records to identify patterns such as frequent short-term absences, high absence rates in certain teams, or seasonal trends. Reports are generated and escalated to management if intervention is needed.
