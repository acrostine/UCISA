## Purpose Strategic Plan Management ensures that the institution's strategic plan remains a living document by monitoring implementation progress, evaluating performance data, and incorporating stakeholder feedback. The process produces updated action plans and risk assessments to maintain alignment between operational activities and strategic aspirations, enabling timely adjustments when needed. ## Roles - Strategy Director: Oversees the process, approves major adjustments - Business Unit Lead: Provides progress reports and operational insights - Performance Analyst: Compiles and analyzes performance data - Risk Officer: Assesses strategic risks and updates risk register - System: Automated data aggregation and notification ## Upstream Inputs - Progress Reports: Quarterly updates from business units - Performance Data: KPI dashboards and metrics - Stakeholder Feedback: Survey results and meeting notes - Current Strategic Plan: Approved plan document ## Downstream Outputs - Updated Action Plans: Revised operational plans with new milestones - Risk Assessment: Updated strategic risk register with mitigation actions - Performance Dashboard: Refreshed KPI visualizations - Adjustment Recommendations: Formal proposals for plan changes ## Key Decisions - Whether performance gaps require plan adjustment or just operational correction - Which strategic risks have escalated to require immediate mitigation - Whether stakeholder feedback warrants a change in strategic priorities ## Exception Paths - Critical performance gap triggers an emergency review cycle bypassing regular cadence - Risk escalation to critical level requires immediate Strategy Director approval - Stakeholder feedback indicating major dissatisfaction initiates a special assessment ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Progress Data: The Performance Analyst collects quarterly progress reports from business unit leads and extracts performance data from KPI dashboards and operational systems. This step ensures all relevant data is available for performance evaluation. Data is validated for completeness and accuracy before proceeding. 2. Assess Performance Against Goals: The Performance Analyst analyzes consolidated data against strategic plan targets. Variances are calculated and categorized as on-track, at-risk, or off-track. A performance gap report is produced highlighting areas requiring attention. This step forms the basis for adjustment decisions. 3. Review Stakeholder Feedback: The Strategy Director reviews stakeholder feedback collected from surveys, town halls, and feedback channels. Key themes and concerns are identified and documented. This qualitative input is used to assess whether strategic priorities remain aligned with stakeholder expectations. 4. Evaluate Strategic Risks: The Risk Officer evaluates risks identified in the performance gap report and from ongoing risk monitoring. Each risk is assessed for likelihood and impact, and the risk register is updated. New risks are documented and existing risks are re-evaluated based on current conditions. 5. Determine Adjustment Needs: The Strategy Director reviews the performance gap report, feedback insights, and updated risk register. A decision is made on whether the strategic plan requires adjustments, operational corrections, or can continue as-is. This is the key decision gate in the process. 6. Update Action Plans: The Business Unit Lead revises operational action plans based on the adjustment decision. New milestones, resource allocations, and timelines are defined. Updated plans are documented and submitted for approval. 7. Update Risk Assessment: The Risk Officer finalizes the risk assessment by documenting mitigation actions for each strategic risk. Risk levels are updated based on the adjustment decision. The risk register is prepared for final approval. 8. Approve Updated Plans: The Strategy Director reviews and approves the updated action plans and final risk assessment. Approval authorizes implementation of the revised plans. The approved documents are distributed to all stakeholders.
