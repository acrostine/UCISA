Staff Recruitment ()
## Purpose This process manages the end-to-end recruitment of staff, from receiving a job requisition through to issuing an appointment offer. It ensures that all candidates are fairly evaluated against defined selection criteria, that interviews are efficiently coordinated, and that offers are made to the best-qualified candidates while meeting diversity and policy requirements. ## Roles - Hiring Manager: Initiates requisition, reviews shortlist, participates in interviews, approves offer - HR Recruiter: Screens applications, schedules interviews, coordinates offer process - Interview Panel: Conducts candidate interviews and provides evaluations - System: Automates screening, notification, and document generation tasks ## Upstream Inputs - Job Requisition: Formal request to fill a vacancy - Candidate Applications: Submitted resumes, cover letters, and supporting documents - Selection Criteria: Defined qualifications, skills, and experience requirements - Vacancy Data: Position details, department, budget code - Recruitment Policies: Institutional guidelines on hiring, diversity, and compliance ## Downstream Outputs - Candidate Shortlist: List of candidates advancing to interview stage - Interview Schedule: Timetable of interview slots with panel and candidate assignments - Appointment Offer: Formal offer letter sent to selected candidate - Rejection Notifications: Letters sent to unsuccessful candidates ## Key Decisions - Which candidates meet minimum selection criteria to be shortlisted? - Which candidate is the best fit after interview evaluation? - Does the offer meet budget and policy approval requirements? ## Exception Paths - No candidates meet minimum criteria: Requisition is returned to Hiring Manager for revision - Shortlisted candidate withdraws: HR Recruiter escalates to Hiring Manager to decide on replacement from remaining pool - Offer is rejected by candidate: HR Recruiter escalates to Hiring Manager to consider next candidate or re-advertise ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Job Requisition: The HR Recruiter receives the job requisition from the Hiring Manager. They check that the vacancy data (position title, department, budget code) is complete and that the requisition aligns with current recruitment policies. Any discrepancies are resolved before proceeding. 2. Screen Applications: The system automatically processes all candidate applications received for the position. It compares each application against the defined selection criteria, flagging candidates who meet the minimum requirements. Applications that do not meet criteria are marked as rejected. 3. Review Shortlist: The Hiring Manager accesses the system-generated shortlist of candidates who met the minimum criteria. They review each candidate's profile and confirm the final list of candidates to be invited for interview. If no candidates meet the criteria, the manager may revise the requisition. 4. Schedule Interviews: The HR Recruiter contacts the interview panel members and shortlisted candidates to find mutually available time slots. They create an interview schedule and distribute it to all participants via the system. 5. Conduct Interviews: The Interview Panel meets with each shortlisted candidate according to the schedule. Panel members ask questions, assess responses against criteria, and record individual scores and comments for each candidate. 6. Evaluate Candidates: The Hiring Manager collects all panel evaluations and reviews scores and comments. They compare candidate performance and select the best-qualified candidate for the position, considering diversity goals and policy fit. 7. Approve Offer: The Hiring Manager reviews the proposed offer details including salary, start date, and terms. They confirm budget availability and policy compliance, then formally approve the offer in the system. 8. Issue Offer: The system automatically generates the formal appointment offer letter using the approved details. It sends the offer to the candidate's email and records the offer in the recruitment record. 9. Notify Unsuccessful Candidates: After the offer is issued, the system automatically sends polite rejection notifications to all candidates who were interviewed but not selected. The notifications are logged for compliance and record-keeping.
