## Purpose This process ensures that every student's graduation eligibility is evaluated consistently and accurately against academic and program requirements. It supports timely progression through the student lifecycle and maintains academic standards by identifying qualifying graduates and flagging incomplete requirements for remediation. ## Roles - Academic Records Officer: Initiates the process and reviews assessment results - Program Coordinator: Provides program regulations and milestone definitions - System: Automates credit checks and milestone verification - Student: Receives notifications and submits remediation evidence ## Upstream Inputs - Student Academic Data: Transcripts, course completions, grades - Program Regulations: Credit requirements, milestone definitions, special conditions - Graduation Application: Student intent to graduate (if applicable) ## Downstream Outputs - Eligibility Confirmation: Official notification for qualifying students - Outstanding Requirements Notice: Detailed list of unmet conditions for non-qualifying students - Remediation Evidence: Student-submitted documentation to resolve gaps ## Key Decisions - Has the student completed all required credits? - Have all program milestones been fulfilled? - Are there any special conditions (e.g., residency, GPA minimum) that are unmet? - Does the student's remediation evidence resolve the identified gaps? ## Exception Paths - If credit requirements are incomplete, the student is notified and given a remediation window. - If program milestones are unmet, the student is notified with specific details. - If remediation evidence is insufficient, the student is asked to resubmit with corrections. - If the student fails to remediate within the allowed period, eligibility is denied. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Student Data: The Academic Records Officer retrieves student academic data including transcripts, course completions, and grades from the student information system. Any graduation applications submitted by students are also collected. This data forms the foundation for all subsequent eligibility checks. 2. Load Program Regulations: The System automatically loads the latest program regulations from the curriculum database, including credit requirements, milestone definitions (e.g., capstone, internship), and any special conditions such as minimum GPA or residency requirements. 3. Assess Credit Completion: The System compares the student's completed courses against the program's credit requirements. It calculates total earned credits and identifies any shortfalls. A credit status report is generated indicating whether the requirement is met or not. 4. Verify Milestone Fulfilment: The System checks whether the student has completed all program-specific milestones such as capstone projects, internships, or thesis submissions. Any incomplete milestones are flagged for review. 5. Review Special Conditions: The Program Coordinator manually reviews any special conditions attached to the program, such as minimum GPA thresholds, residency requirements, or disciplinary clearances. Each condition is marked as met or unmet. 6. Determine Eligibility: The Academic Records Officer reviews the credit status report, milestone status report, and special conditions assessment. Based on all three inputs, a final eligibility decision is made: eligible or not eligible. 7. Notify Eligible Students: The System automatically generates and sends an eligibility confirmation notification to students who have been determined eligible. This notification includes next steps for graduation processing. 8. Notify Outstanding Requirements: The System automatically generates and sends a detailed notice to students who are not yet eligible, listing all outstanding requirements such as missing credits, incomplete milestones, or unmet special conditions. 9. Submit Remediation Evidence: The student reviews the outstanding requirements notice and submits evidence to address each gap. This may include transcripts from other institutions, completion certificates, or formal requests for exceptions. 10. Review Remediation: The Academic Records Officer reviews the remediation evidence submitted by the student. If the evidence satisfactorily resolves the outstanding requirements, it is approved; otherwise, it is rejected with feedback. 11. Finalise Eligibility: The System updates the student's graduation status in the student information system. If remediation was approved, the status is set to eligible; if rejected, the status remains not eligible and the student is notified of the final decision.
