## Purpose This process ensures that the institution systematically identifies and curates external funding opportunities that align with its strategic research goals. By filtering calls through eligibility and priority criteria, it prevents wasted effort on unsuitable grants and maximises the chances of successful applications. ## Roles - Research Office Coordinator: Initiates the process and compiles the initial shortlist. - Research Office Manager: Reviews and validates the shortlist against strategic priorities. - System: Automates the ingestion of funding agency calls and eligibility rule checks. ## Upstream Inputs - Institutional Research Priorities - Funding Agency Calls - Eligibility Criteria ## Downstream Outputs - Curated Funding Opportunity List ## Key Decisions - Does the funding call align with institutional research priorities? - Does the researcher meet the eligibility criteria for the opportunity? - Should the opportunity be included in the final curated list? ## Exception Paths - If a funding call does not align with priorities, it is excluded from the shortlist. - If eligibility criteria are not met, the opportunity is flagged as unsuitable and not included. - If the Research Office Manager rejects the shortlist, it is sent back for rework and re-screening. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Gather Research Priorities: The Research Office Coordinator retrieves the latest institutional research priorities, including thematic focus areas and strategic goals. These priorities serve as the primary filter for identifying relevant funding opportunities. The coordinator ensures the list is current and approved by the research leadership. 2. Ingest Funding Calls: The system connects to external funding agency databases and ingests all open calls for proposals. It captures key metadata such as call title, deadline, funding amount, and thematic area. The system logs any new or updated calls since the last ingestion cycle. 3. Apply Eligibility Filters: The system applies eligibility rules such as researcher career stage, geographic restrictions, and institutional eligibility. Calls that fail any mandatory criterion are flagged as unsuitable. The system generates a filtered dataset of calls that meet all basic eligibility requirements. 4. Compile Initial Shortlist: The Research Office Coordinator reviews the filtered calls and compiles an initial shortlist. They add notes on alignment with research priorities and potential researcher matches. The shortlist is prepared for managerial review. 5. Review Shortlist: The Research Office Manager examines each opportunity on the shortlist against the institutional research priorities. They assess strategic fit, potential impact, and resource implications. The manager decides whether to approve the shortlist or send it back for revision. 6. Validate Final List: The Research Office Manager performs a final validation of the shortlist, confirming that all included opportunities meet priority and eligibility criteria. They formally approve the list for dissemination to academic staff. 7. Publish Curated Opportunities: The system publishes the validated funding list to the institutional research portal, making it accessible to all academic staff. Notifications are sent to relevant researchers based on their research profiles. The list is timestamped and archived for audit purposes. 8. Rework Shortlist: The Research Office Coordinator revisits the shortlist to address the manager's concerns. They may add or remove opportunities, update alignment notes, or re-apply eligibility filters. The revised shortlist is resubmitted for review.
