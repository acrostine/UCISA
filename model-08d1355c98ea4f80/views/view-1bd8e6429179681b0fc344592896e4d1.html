The output is a comprehensive campaign plan ready for review. 3. Review Campaign Design: The Marketing Manager evaluates whether the campaign plan aligns with strategic objectives and stays within budget. If criteria are met, the design proceeds; otherwise, it is sent back for rework. 4. Rework Campaign Design: The Marketing Manager addresses the issues identified during review, adjusting channels, timeline, or messaging as needed. The revised plan is then resubmitted for re-review. 5. Develop Creative Content: The Creative Designer creates all required creative assets including headlines, images, videos, and landing page mockups. Content is developed to match the campaign's tone, audience, and channel specifications. 6. Approve Creative Content: The Marketing Manager reviews the creative assets for brand compliance, messaging accuracy, and visual quality. If approved, the content moves to launch; if rejected, it is sent back for revision. 7. Revise Creative Content: The Creative Designer incorporates the Marketing Manager's feedback into the creative assets. The revised content is then resubmitted through the approval workflow. 8. Launch Campaign: The System schedules and publishes the approved creative content across all selected channels. Launch notifications are sent to stakeholders and tracking tags are activated. 9. Monitor Campaign Performance: The Campaign Analyst monitors impressions, clicks, conversions, and spend. Performance is compared against predefined KPIs. If metrics fall below threshold, an escalation is triggered. 10. Escalate Underperformance: The Campaign Analyst sends an escalation alert to the Marketing Manager detailing the underperformance metrics. The Marketing Manager reviews and decides on corrective actions such as pausing or adjusting the campaign. 11. Generate Performance Analytics: The Campaign Analyst aggregates all campaign metrics, compares them to objectives, and produces a final analytics report. The report includes insights and recommendations for future campaigns.
