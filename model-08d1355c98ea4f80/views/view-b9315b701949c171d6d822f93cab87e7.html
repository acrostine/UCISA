## Purpose This process governs the handling of student requests to transfer between academic programmes within the institution. It ensures that transfers are assessed fairly based on academic eligibility, approved by the appropriate authorities, and recorded accurately to maintain academic continuity and support institutional reporting on student mobility. ## Roles - Student (initiator) - Student Administration Officer (logger and processor) - Academic Advisor (eligibility reviewer) - Programme Director (approver) - Registry Officer (record updater) - System (automated notifications and record updates) ## Upstream Inputs - Transfer Application (from student) - Academic Transcript (from student record) - Programme Requirements (from programme catalogue) ## Downstream Outputs - Transfer Decision Notification (to student) - Updated Student Record (in student information system) - Programme Transfer Report (to institutional reporting) ## Key Decisions - Does the student meet the minimum academic requirements for the target programme? - Does the transfer align with programme capacity and progression rules? - Should the transfer be approved or rejected? ## Exception Paths - If the student does not meet eligibility criteria, the application is rejected and the student is notified with reasons. - If the Programme Director is unavailable, the application is escalated to the Dean for decision. - If record update fails, the Registry Officer is alerted to perform a manual correction. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Transfer Application: The Student Administration Officer receives the formal transfer application submitted by the student. The application details, including student ID, current programme, and target programme, are entered into the student administration system. A case record is created to track the request through the process. 2. Review Academic Transcript: The Academic Advisor accesses the student's academic transcript from the student record system. They review the student's grades, credits completed, and any academic standing issues to understand the student's current academic status. 3. Check Programme Requirements: The Academic Advisor retrieves the entry requirements for the target programme from the programme catalogue. They compare the student's academic transcript against these requirements, noting any prerequisites, minimum GPA, or credit thresholds. 4. Assess Transfer Eligibility: The Academic Advisor synthesises the transcript review and requirements check to make an eligibility determination. They consider academic performance, programme capacity, and progression rules. This step produces a recommendation for approval or rejection. 5. Approve Transfer Request: The Programme Director receives the eligibility assessment and makes the final decision on the transfer. They consider academic fit, programme capacity, and any exceptional circumstances. The decision is recorded in the system. 6. Update Student Record: The Registry Officer updates the student's record to reflect the new programme. This includes changing the programme code, updating the academic plan, and adjusting any associated module enrolments. The system validates the changes for consistency. 7. Notify Student of Outcome: The system automatically generates and sends a notification to the student's registered email address. The notification includes the transfer decision, effective date, and any further instructions. A copy is also sent to relevant academic departments.
