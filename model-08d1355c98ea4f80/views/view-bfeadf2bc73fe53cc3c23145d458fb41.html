Non-Commercial Research Impact Mgt ()
## Purpose This process ensures that research findings with non-commercial potential are systematically translated into societal benefits. It covers the full lifecycle from assessing impact potential through to producing case studies and policy briefs, engaging communities, and reporting outcomes. The process supports the institution's knowledge exchange mission and enhances its reputation for public-good research. ## Roles - Impact Manager: Leads strategy development, coordinates activities, and oversees documentation. - Research Lead: Provides subject matter expertise and validates research outputs. - Stakeholder Liaison: Manages relationships with community and policy stakeholders. - Approver (Head of Research Impact): Authorises the impact strategy and final case studies. - System (Research Impact Platform): Automated notifications, document storage, and reporting. ## Upstream Inputs - Validated Research Outputs (from Research Output Validation process) - Stakeholder Feedback (from Stakeholder Engagement process) - Institutional Impact Priorities ## Downstream Outputs - Impact Case Study (to Research Reporting and REF submission) - Policy Brief (to external policy stakeholders) - Community Engagement Activity Report (to Stakeholder Engagement process) - Impact Monitoring Report (to Research Governance) ## Key Decisions - Whether the research output has sufficient non-commercial impact potential to proceed - Which dissemination channels and engagement methods are most appropriate - Whether the impact strategy meets institutional quality and ethical standards - Whether the final case study is ready for publication and submission ## Exception Paths - If impact potential is assessed as low, the process terminates with a notification to the Research Lead. - If the impact strategy is rejected by the Approver, it is returned to the Impact Manager for revision. - If stakeholder engagement yields negative feedback or low interest, the strategy is adjusted via a dedicated remediation step. - If the case study fails quality review, it is sent back for rework before final approval. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Research Outputs: The Impact Manager retrieves the latest validated research outputs from the Research Output Validation process and any relevant stakeholder feedback from the Stakeholder Engagement process. These inputs form the foundation for assessing non-commercial impact potential. The data is logged into the Research Impact Platform for tracking. 2. Assess Impact Potential: The Impact Manager reviews the research outputs against institutional impact criteria, considering factors such as policy relevance, community need, and alignment with societal challenges. A preliminary impact rating is assigned. If potential is low, the process terminates; otherwise, it proceeds to strategy development. 3. Develop Impact Strategy: Based on the assessment, the Impact Manager creates a detailed impact strategy outlining target audiences (e.g., policymakers, community groups), dissemination channels (e.g., policy briefs, public reports), engagement methods (e.g., workshops, consultations), and success metrics. The strategy is documented in the platform. 4. Review and Approve Strategy: The Approver (Head of Research Impact) reviews the draft strategy for alignment with institutional mission, ethical standards, and resource availability. If approved, the strategy moves to execution. If rejected, it is returned with feedback for revision. 5. Revise Impact Strategy: The Impact Manager reviews the feedback from the Approver and updates the strategy accordingly. The revised strategy is resubmitted for approval. This step ensures that only high-quality, aligned strategies proceed. 6. Execute Dissemination Activities: The Impact Manager works with the Research Lead to produce and distribute policy briefs, public-facing reports, and media releases. These materials are tailored to non-commercial audiences and designed to maximise societal reach. All outputs are logged in the platform. 7. Conduct Community Engagement: The Stakeholder Liaison organises and facilitates engagement activities such as community workshops, policy roundtables, or public consultations. Feedback and participation data are collected and recorded. This step strengthens knowledge exchange and builds relationships. 8. Document Impact Case Study: The Impact Manager compiles evidence from dissemination and engagement activities into a structured impact case study. The case study includes narrative, quantitative indicators, stakeholder testimonials, and links to research outputs. It is stored in the platform for review. 9. Review and Finalise Case Study: The Research Lead and Approver jointly review the draft case study. They verify factual accuracy, assess the strength of the impact narrative, and ensure alignment with institutional standards. If approved, the case study is finalised. If issues are found, it is returned for revision. 10. Rework Case Study: The Impact Manager addresses the feedback from the review, updating the case study narrative, evidence, or presentation as needed. The revised version is resubmitted for final approval. 11. Report and Monitor Outcomes: The system automatically compiles an impact monitoring report summarising all activities, outputs, and outcomes from this process instance. The report is stored in the institutional repository and made available for governance and future REF submissions.
