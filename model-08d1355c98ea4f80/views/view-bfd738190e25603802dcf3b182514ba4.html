## Purpose The Legal Advisory process ensures that the institution receives expert legal counsel on a wide range of matters including academic policies, research collaborations, commercial contracts, and governance decisions. By systematically analysing case facts, institutional strategies, and regulatory frameworks, the process produces risk-assessed recommendations that empower leadership to make informed decisions and strengthen the institution's legal risk posture. ## Roles - Legal Advisor: Receives request, analyses case facts, assesses risks, formulates recommendations, and prepares advisory opinion - Senior Legal Counsel: Reviews advisory opinion for quality and completeness - Requesting Stakeholder: Submits the legal inquiry and receives the final advisory opinion ## Upstream Inputs - Legal Counsel Request - Case Facts - Institutional Strategies - Regulatory Frameworks ## Downstream Outputs - Legal Advisory Opinion - Risk Assessment Report - Recommendation Summary ## Key Decisions - Whether the request contains sufficient information to proceed with analysis - Whether the advisory opinion meets quality standards before delivery - Whether additional legal research or consultation is required ## Exception Paths - If the request lacks sufficient information, the Legal Advisor requests clarification from the stakeholder - If the advisory opinion fails internal review, it is revised and re-submitted for approval - If the matter requires specialised expertise, external legal counsel may be engaged ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Legal Request: The Legal Advisor receives the legal counsel request via the institutional case management system. The request is logged with a unique identifier, and the stakeholder is acknowledged. The advisor reviews the initial submission to capture case facts, the nature of the inquiry, and any attached documentation. 2. Review Case Information: The Legal Advisor conducts a thorough review of all submitted case facts, relevant institutional strategies (e.g., research strategy, commercialisation policy), and applicable regulatory frameworks (e.g., legislation, codes of practice). This step ensures a comprehensive understanding of the legal context before proceeding with risk analysis. 3. Assess Legal Risks: Using the reviewed case information, the Legal Advisor identifies potential legal risks including compliance gaps, liability exposure, contractual issues, and reputational risks. Each risk is assessed for likelihood and impact, and documented in a risk assessment report. 4. Formulate Recommendations: The Legal Advisor synthesises the risk assessment findings into clear, actionable recommendations. Recommendations address risk mitigation, compliance actions, strategic options, and any required policy changes. A recommendation summary is prepared for inclusion in the advisory opinion. 5. Prepare Advisory Opinion: The Legal Advisor compiles the risk assessment and recommendations into a formal advisory opinion. The document includes an executive summary, detailed legal analysis, risk findings, and recommended actions. The opinion is formatted according to institutional standards and saved for review. 6. Review Advisory Opinion: The Senior Legal Counsel examines the draft advisory opinion to ensure legal accuracy, clarity, completeness, and alignment with institutional policies. If the opinion meets standards, it is approved. If issues are found, the opinion is returned with feedback for revision. 7. Revise Advisory Opinion: The Legal Advisor receives the review feedback from the Senior Legal Counsel and makes the necessary revisions to the draft advisory opinion. The revised document is then resubmitted for a second review cycle. 8. Deliver Advisory Opinion: The Legal Advisor transmits the final approved advisory opinion to the requesting stakeholder via the case management system. A delivery confirmation is recorded, and the case is closed. The stakeholder is invited to ask follow-up questions if needed.
