## Purpose Event Mgt organises and coordinates marketing-related events such as open days, webinars, and alumni gatherings. It consumes event objectives, logistical requirements, and promotional materials to deliver seamless experiences. The process produces event schedules, attendee communications, and post-event reports, fostering community engagement and institutional visibility. ## Roles - Event Coordinator: Plans and oversees event logistics - Marketing Manager: Approves promotional strategy and budget - Communications Officer: Manages attendee communications - Operations Team: Handles venue, catering, and technical setup - System: Automated scheduling and notification tools ## Upstream Inputs - Event Objectives - Logistical Requirements - Promotional Materials - Budget Allocation ## Downstream Outputs - Event Schedule - Attendee Communications - Post-Event Report - Lessons Learned Document ## Key Decisions - Resource allocation: Determining budget and staff for each event - Risk assessment: Evaluating potential issues and mitigation strategies - Promotional approval: Sign-off on marketing materials and channels - Post-event evaluation: Deciding on success metrics and improvement areas ## Exception Paths - Budget overrun: Escalation to Marketing Manager for reallocation or scope reduction - Low registration: Trigger additional promotional push or event cancellation review - Venue cancellation: Activate backup venue or reschedule event - Technical failure during event: Initiate contingency plan and post-event incident report ## Step Walkthrough 1. Allocate Resources: The Marketing Manager reviews the resource assessment and approves the budget, staff assignments, and venue booking. If resources are insufficient, the manager may adjust scope or request additional funding. 4. Develop Event Plan: The Event Coordinator produces a comprehensive event plan including timelines, task assignments, communication schedules, and contingency measures. This plan serves as the blueprint for all execution activities. 5. Review and Approve Plan: The Marketing Manager examines the event plan for completeness, alignment with objectives, and feasibility. If the plan meets criteria, it is approved; otherwise, it is returned with feedback for revision. 6. Execute Promotional Campaign: The Communications Officer sends invitations, manages registrations, and coordinates promotional content across channels. This step ensures target audiences are informed and engaged ahead of the event. 7. Coordinate Logistics: The Operations Team handles all physical and technical arrangements including room layout, AV equipment, catering orders, signage, and staffing schedules. This step ensures the venue is ready for the event day. 8. Deliver Event: The Event Coordinator leads the on-the-day execution, monitoring schedules, handling unexpected issues, and ensuring a positive attendee experience. All activities are tracked against the event plan. 9. Conduct Post-Event Review: The Event Coordinator collects attendee surveys, attendance numbers, budget actuals, and team feedback. This data is analysed to evaluate success against objectives and identify improvement areas. 10. Archive and Distribute Report: The Communications Officer sends the final report to all stakeholders, updates the event knowledge base, and archives promotional materials and logistics records. This step closes the event lifecycle.
