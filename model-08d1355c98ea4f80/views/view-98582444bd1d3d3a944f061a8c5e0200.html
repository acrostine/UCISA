Receive Content: The Media Relations Officer collects newsworthy content and institutional messaging from various internal departments such as academic affairs, student services, and executive leadership. This input forms the basis for all subsequent media activities. 2. Review Content: The Media Relations Officer examines the content to ensure it is factually accurate, relevant to current institutional priorities, and aligned with the approved messaging framework. Any discrepancies are noted for correction. 3. Draft Press Release: Using the reviewed content, the Media Relations Officer writes a press release or outlines a media campaign. The draft follows institutional templates and style guides to ensure consistency. 4. Approve Draft: The Communications Director reviews the draft press release for messaging alignment, tone, and quality. A decision is made to approve for distribution or reject with feedback for revision. 5. Rework Draft: If the draft is rejected, the Media Relations Officer incorporates the feedback from the Communications Director and revises the press release. The revised draft is then resubmitted for approval. 6. Distribute Press Release: The System automatically distributes the approved press release to a predefined list of media outlets, including newspapers, TV stations, and online platforms. Distribution is logged for tracking. 7. Monitor Coverage: The System tracks media mentions and coverage resulting from the press release. A coverage report is generated summarizing reach, sentiment, and key metrics for review by the Media Relations Officer.
