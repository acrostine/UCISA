## Purpose This process ensures that all problems arising during commercial engagements are systematically identified, assessed, and resolved. It provides a structured approach to prioritising issues based on contractual terms and business impact, enabling timely and transparent handling that protects institutional relationships and operational continuity. ## Roles - Issue Manager: Receives, logs, and triages incoming issues; coordinates resolution activities. - Commercial Lead: Assesses contractual impact and approves resolution plans. - Stakeholder: Provides feedback and receives status updates. - System: Automated notifications and log updates. ## Upstream Inputs - Issue Report - Stakeholder Feedback - Contractual Terms ## Downstream Outputs - Issue Log - Resolution Plan - Communication Update - Closed Issue Record ## Key Decisions - Determine issue severity (Critical, Major, Minor) - Assess whether issue breaches contractual obligations - Approve or reject proposed resolution plan - Decide if issue requires escalation to senior management ## Exception Paths - If issue severity is Critical, immediate escalation to Commercial Lead occurs. - If resolution plan is rejected, the plan must be reworked and resubmitted. - If issue cannot be resolved within agreed timeline, stakeholder is notified and timeline is renegotiated. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Issue Report: The Issue Manager receives the issue report from internal or external sources, along with any accompanying stakeholder feedback. This step initiates the process and captures all initial information needed for logging. 2. Log Issue: The Issue Manager enters the issue details into the issue management system, assigning a unique ID, date stamp, and initial classification (e.g., type, source). The issue log is updated and becomes the central record. 3. Assess Issue Severity: The Issue Manager assesses the issue's severity (Critical, Major, Minor) based on predefined criteria such as financial impact, operational disruption, and contractual breach potential. This determines the priority and escalation path. 4. Review Contractual Impact: The Commercial Lead examines the issue in the context of the relevant contract terms, assessing whether obligations have been breached, what remedies apply, and the overall business impact. This step informs the resolution approach. 5. Develop Resolution Plan: Based on the severity and contractual impact, the Issue Manager develops a detailed resolution plan including specific actions, assigned resources, expected timeline, and success criteria. The plan is documented in the issue log. 6. Approve Resolution Plan: The Commercial Lead reviews the proposed resolution plan for feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with contractual terms. The plan is either approved for execution or rejected with feedback for rework. 7. Rework Resolution Plan: If the resolution plan is rejected, the Issue Manager revises the plan addressing the Commercial Lead's feedback. The updated plan is resubmitted for approval. 8. Execute Resolution Plan: The Issue Manager coordinates the execution of the approved resolution plan, assigning tasks to relevant team members, tracking progress, and updating the issue log with status changes and completed actions. 9. Communicate Status Update: The Issue Manager prepares and distributes a status update to stakeholders, summarising the issue, actions taken, current status, and expected resolution timeline. This ensures transparency and maintains trust. 10. Close Issue: The Issue Manager confirms that all resolution actions are complete, updates the issue log with final status, documents lessons learned, and archives the record. The issue is formally closed.
