## Purpose Research Programme Performance Management ensures that research initiatives are systematically monitored against their stated objectives and funding requirements. It provides a structured mechanism to evaluate progress, identify issues, and drive continuous improvement through formal reviews and action plans. ## Roles - Research Programme Manager - Principal Investigator - Quality Assurance Officer - System ## Upstream Inputs - Performance Data - Project Reports - Stakeholder Feedback - Funding Criteria ## Downstream Outputs - Performance Review - Improvement Plan - Escalation Notice ## Key Decisions - Does the programme meet performance criteria? - Is escalation required for underperformance? - What corrective actions are needed? ## Exception Paths - If performance criteria are not met, a remediation step is triggered to analyse root causes and propose corrective actions. - If issues persist after remediation, an escalation notice is sent to senior management. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Performance Data: The system gathers performance metrics, project reports, and stakeholder feedback from multiple sources. 2. Review Programme Obligations: The Research Programme Manager checks that all funding and reporting obligations are being met. 3. Assess Performance Against Criteria: The Quality Assurance Officer evaluates programme performance against defined KPIs and funding criteria. 4. Analyse Underperformance Issues: If criteria are not met, the Principal Investigator analyses root causes and documents findings. 5. Develop Improvement Plan: Based on the analysis, the Research Programme Manager creates a formal improvement plan with corrective actions. 6. Approve Improvement Plan: The Quality Assurance Officer reviews and approves the improvement plan, or requests revisions. 7. Generate Performance Review: The system compiles the final performance review document, incorporating the improvement plan if applicable. Steps: 1. Collect Performance Data: The system automatically collects quantitative performance data from research management systems, project reports submitted by principal investigators, and qualitative feedback from stakeholders. This data forms the basis for all subsequent evaluation activities. 2. Review Programme Obligations: The Research Programme Manager reviews the consolidated data against the programme's funding agreement and reporting schedule. They verify that all contractual obligations, milestone deliverables, and compliance requirements are satisfied. 3. Assess Performance Against Criteria: The Quality Assurance Officer uses the obligations assessment and performance data to evaluate whether the programme meets its stated objectives and KPIs. This step produces a pass/fail determination that drives the next decision point. 4. Analyse Underperformance Issues: The Principal Investigator conducts a root cause analysis to understand why the programme underperformed. They examine data trends, resource constraints, and operational challenges, documenting findings for the improvement planning step. 5. Develop Improvement Plan: The Research Programme Manager uses the root cause analysis to design a detailed improvement plan. The plan includes specific corrective actions, responsible parties, timelines, and success metrics to address the identified issues. 6. Approve Improvement Plan: The Quality Assurance Officer reviews the draft improvement plan for feasibility, completeness, and alignment with programme objectives. They either approve the plan or send it back for revision with specific feedback. 7. Generate Performance Review: The system automatically generates the final performance review document, incorporating the performance evaluation result and, if applicable, the approved improvement plan.
