## Purpose The Internal Communications process ensures that organisational announcements, policy updates, and event information are disseminated efficiently and effectively across the university. It coordinates the review, approval, channel selection, and distribution of messages to enhance collaboration, alignment, and engagement among all departments and personnel. ## Roles - Communications Officer: Initiates and triages incoming communications. - Content Reviewer: Reviews and approves message content for accuracy and policy alignment. - Channel Manager: Selects appropriate distribution channels and schedules the release. - System: Automates distribution and feedback collection. ## Upstream Inputs - Organisational Announcements - Policy Updates - Event Information ## Downstream Outputs - Targeted Messaging - Distribution Confirmation - Feedback Report ## Key Decisions - Whether the communication content meets quality and policy standards. - Which distribution channels are most appropriate for the target audience. - Whether feedback indicates a need for corrective action. ## Exception Paths - If content is rejected during review, it is sent back for rework with specific guidance. - If feedback reveals significant issues (e.g., miscommunication), a corrective action loop is triggered. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Select Channels: The Channel Manager evaluates the target audience and message type to select the most effective channels, such as email, intranet, digital signage, or printed bulletins. 7. Schedule Distribution: The Channel Manager determines the optimal date and time for distribution, considering audience availability and any time-sensitive requirements. 8. Distribute Message: The System executes the distribution plan, sending the communication via all selected channels simultaneously or according to the schedule. 9. Collect Feedback: The System automatically collects feedback through surveys, read receipts, click-through rates, and other engagement metrics for a predefined period after distribution. 10. Assess Feedback: The Communications Officer analyses the feedback report to determine if the communication achieved its objectives and identifies any areas for improvement. 11. Address Issues: If the assessment reveals significant issues such as miscommunication or low engagement, the Communications Officer develops and implements corrective actions, which may include follow-up communications or process adjustments.
