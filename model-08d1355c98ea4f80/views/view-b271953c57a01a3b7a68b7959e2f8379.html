PGR Student Skills Development ()
## Purpose This process ensures that postgraduate research (PGR) students receive structured, targeted skills development to enhance their research capabilities, academic performance, and professional readiness. It aligns training with institutional goals and individual student needs, improving employability and research quality. ## Roles - **Skills Development Coordinator**: Oversees the process, analyses inputs, and designs the training plan. - **Supervisor**: Provides guidance on student development needs and approves the personalised plan. - **Trainer/Facilitator**: Delivers training sessions and resources. - **Student**: Participates in training and provides feedback. - **System**: Automates data collection, scheduling, and reporting. ## Upstream Inputs - Curriculum frameworks - Supervisory guidance - Student feedback - Student enrolment data ## Downstream Outputs - Skilled researchers - Training completion records - Evaluation reports - Updated development plans ## Key Decisions - Which skill gaps are prioritised for each student? - Is the personalised development plan approved by the supervisor? - Does the student meet the required participation threshold? - Is the training effective based on evaluation criteria? ## Exception Paths - If the development plan is rejected by the supervisor, it is reworked and resubmitted. - If a student fails to meet participation requirements, a remediation step is triggered. - If training evaluation shows poor effectiveness, the training content is revised. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Collect Input Data**: The Skills Development Coordinator gathers data from curriculum frameworks, supervisory guidance, and student feedback to understand development needs. 2. **Assess Skill Gaps**: The coordinator analyses the collected data to identify specific skill gaps for each student, producing a gap analysis report. 3. **Design Development Plan**: Based on the gap analysis, a personalised development plan is created, outlining training sessions and resources. 4. **Review Development Plan**: The supervisor reviews the proposed plan and decides whether to approve it or request changes. 5. **Rework Development Plan**: If the plan is rejected, the coordinator revises it based on supervisor feedback and resubmits for approval. 6. **Deliver Training Sessions**: Once approved, trainers deliver the scheduled training sessions and provide resources to students. 7. **Monitor Student Progress**: The system tracks student participation and completion of training activities, flagging any issues. 8. **Evaluate Training Effectiveness**: The coordinator assesses the impact of training using student feedback and performance metrics, producing an evaluation report. 9. **Update Development Plan**: Based on evaluation results, the plan is updated for continuous improvement, and the cycle may repeat for new skill gaps. Steps: 1. Collect Input Data: The Skills Development Coordinator collects all relevant input data, including curriculum frameworks that define required competencies, supervisory guidance on individual student needs, and direct student feedback from surveys or meetings. This data is compiled into a single repository for analysis. 2. Assess Skill Gaps: Using the compiled input data, the coordinator performs a gap analysis to determine where each student's current skills fall short of required competencies. This assessment prioritises gaps based on urgency and relevance to the student's research. 3. Design Development Plan: Based on the gap analysis report, the coordinator designs a tailored development plan that includes specific training sessions, workshops, online resources, and milestones. The plan is documented and prepared for supervisor review. 4. Review Development Plan: The supervisor examines the personalised development plan to ensure it aligns with the student's research goals and institutional requirements. The supervisor either approves the plan or provides feedback for revision. 5. Rework Development Plan: If the supervisor rejects the plan, the coordinator revises it according to the feedback provided. The updated plan is then resubmitted for supervisor review. 6. Deliver Training Sessions: Trainers deliver the scheduled training sessions, which may include workshops, seminars, or online modules. Resources such as guides and tools are made available to the student to support their development. 7. Monitor Student Progress: The system automatically monitors the student's engagement with training sessions and resources, recording attendance, completion rates, and assessment scores. Any deviations from the plan are flagged for coordinator attention. 8. Evaluate Training Effectiveness: The coordinator collects student feedback and reviews performance data to evaluate whether the training met its objectives. An evaluation report is produced, highlighting successes and areas for improvement. 9. Update Development Plan: Using the evaluation report, the coordinator updates the development plan to address any remaining gaps or new needs. This updated plan may feed back into the process for continuous improvement.
