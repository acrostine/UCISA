## Purpose Collaborative Opportunity Management enables the institution to proactively identify, evaluate, and formalise research partnerships with external organisations. By systematically screening potential collaborators against funding calls and strategic priorities, the process ensures that only high-value, aligned opportunities proceed to formal agreement, thereby increasing the institution's competitiveness for collaborative funding. ## Roles - Research Development Manager: Leads opportunity identification, screening, and partner engagement. - Head of Research Strategy: Approves strategic alignment and partnership decisions. - Principal Investigator (PI): Provides technical input and co-develops proposal plans. - Legal Counsel: Reviews and finalises partnership agreements. - System: Automated notifications and document repository. ## Upstream Inputs - Potential Collaborator Profiles - Funding Call Announcements - Institutional Research Priorities - Past Collaboration Records ## Downstream Outputs - Joint Proposal Plan - Partnership Agreement - Collaboration Decision Record - Partner Engagement Log ## Key Decisions - Does the opportunity align with institutional research priorities? - Does the potential partner bring complementary expertise and resources? - Are the terms of collaboration acceptable to both parties? - Should the partnership proceed to a formal agreement? ## Exception Paths - If strategic alignment is insufficient, the opportunity is rejected and logged. - If partner due diligence reveals risks, the opportunity is escalated for review or terminated. - If negotiation fails to reach acceptable terms, the collaboration is abandoned and documented. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Identify Potential Collaborators: The Research Development Manager collects data from internal and external sources including partner databases, funding agency announcements, and the institutional research strategy document. A longlist of potential collaborative opportunities is compiled for initial screening. 2. Screen Opportunities: The Research Development Manager evaluates each longlist entry using predefined criteria such as alignment with research strengths, partner reputation, and funding call relevance. Opportunities that fail the screening are rejected and logged with reasons. 3. Assess Strategic Fit: The Head of Research Strategy reviews the shortlisted opportunities against the institution's strategic research priorities. Approved opportunities proceed to partner engagement; rejected ones are returned with feedback for re-evaluation or final rejection. 4. Engage Partner: The Research Development Manager reaches out to the identified partner organisation, shares a preliminary proposal concept, and schedules a meeting to discuss mutual interests and collaboration scope. 5. Evaluate Collaboration Viability: The PI and Research Development Manager jointly review the partner's technical expertise, available resources, and potential risks including IP conflicts and funding constraints. Viable opportunities proceed to negotiation; non-viable ones are documented and closed. 6. Negotiate Terms: The Research Development Manager and Legal Counsel engage with the partner to agree on key terms including intellectual property ownership, budget allocation, publication rights, and project deliverables. If terms cannot be agreed, the negotiation may be re-attempted or the collaboration abandoned. 7. Formalise Agreement: Legal Counsel drafts the formal partnership agreement incorporating all negotiated terms. Both parties review and sign the document, which is then stored in the institutional repository. 8. Produce Joint Proposal Plan: The PI and Research Development Manager work with the partner to create a comprehensive proposal plan including research objectives, methodology, work packages, budget, and timeline. This plan serves as the basis for the funding application. 9. Archive Collaboration Record: The system automatically archives the partnership agreement, joint proposal plan, viability assessment, and all decision logs in a structured repository for audit and reuse purposes.
