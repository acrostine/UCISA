## Purpose Research Quality Management ensures that all research activities within the institution meet the highest standards of rigor, integrity, and compliance. By systematically auditing and reviewing research outputs, the process identifies areas for improvement, mitigates reputational risk, and drives a culture of continuous quality enhancement. ## Roles - Quality Manager: Oversees the quality management process, reviews audit findings, and approves recommendations. - Quality Analyst: Conducts detailed reviews of research materials, identifies compliance gaps, and drafts assessment reports. - Research Lead: Provides research documentation and receives quality recommendations for implementation. - System: Automated notifications and document storage. ## Upstream Inputs - Quality Audit Request: Formal request to initiate a quality review of a research activity. - Peer Review Reports: Completed peer review assessments from internal or external reviewers. - Compliance Reports: Documentation of regulatory compliance status for research projects. - Institutional Standards: Current quality and compliance standards applicable to research. ## Downstream Outputs - Quality Assessment Report: Detailed findings from the quality review process. - Quality Recommendations: Approved, actionable recommendations for improving research quality. - Quality Assurance Insights: Summary of key insights for institutional learning and policy updates. ## Key Decisions - Does the submitted documentation meet the minimum quality threshold for review? - Are there significant compliance gaps that require immediate remediation? - Should the quality recommendations be approved as drafted or revised? ## Exception Paths - If submitted documentation is incomplete or insufficient, the request is returned to the Research Lead for resubmission. - If significant compliance gaps are found, a dedicated remediation step is triggered before the assessment can proceed. - If recommendations are rejected by the Quality Manager, they are sent back for revision. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Quality Request: The process begins when a Research Lead submits a quality audit request along with relevant research documentation, peer review reports, and compliance records. The Quality Analyst acknowledges receipt and logs the request into the quality management system. 2. Review Submitted Materials: The Quality Analyst reviews the submitted materials against a checklist of required documentation and minimum quality thresholds. This step determines whether the request can proceed to full compliance assessment or needs to be returned for additional information. 3. Return for Resubmission: If the submitted documentation is incomplete or does not meet the minimum quality threshold, the Quality Analyst returns the request to the Research Lead. A clear explanation of missing items or deficiencies is provided, and the Research Lead is asked to resubmit the corrected materials. 4. Assess Research Compliance: The Quality Analyst conducts a detailed assessment of the research activities against institutional quality standards and external regulatory requirements. This involves reviewing methodology, data handling, ethical approvals, and reporting practices to identify any gaps or non-compliance issues. 5. Address Compliance Gaps: When compliance gaps are identified, the Research Lead is notified and must take corrective action to address the issues. This may involve updating documentation, obtaining missing approvals, or revising research protocols. Once completed, the materials are resubmitted for re-assessment. 6. Compile Quality Assessment: Based on the compliance assessment findings, the Quality Analyst compiles a detailed quality assessment report. The report includes an overview of the review, identified strengths and weaknesses, compliance status, and preliminary recommendations for improvement. 7. Review and Approve Recommendations: The Quality Manager examines the quality assessment report and the proposed recommendations. If the recommendations are sound and aligned with institutional goals, they are approved. If not, the report is returned to the Quality Analyst with feedback for revision. 8. Revise Recommendations: If the Quality Manager rejects the recommendations, the Quality Analyst revises them according to the feedback provided. The revised recommendations are then resubmitted to the Quality Manager for approval. 9. Disseminate Quality Insights: Once recommendations are approved, the Quality Analyst disseminates the final quality assessment report and recommendations to relevant stakeholders, including the Research Lead and institutional leadership. The insights are also archived in the quality management system for future reference and continuous improvement.
