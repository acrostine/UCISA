## Purpose Supplier Management ensures that the organization maintains effective oversight of its vendor and service provider relationships. By systematically reviewing performance data, contract compliance, and risk indicators, the process drives accountability, identifies improvement opportunities, and mitigates potential disruptions to supply or service delivery. ## Roles - Supplier Manager: Owns the overall supplier relationship and coordinates review activities - Procurement Analyst: Gathers performance data, metrics, and payment history - Risk Analyst: Assesses supplier risk levels and flags issues - Supplier Representative: Participates in review meetings and provides input - System: Automates data collection, scoring, and report generation ## Upstream Inputs - Contract Terms and SLAs - Performance Metrics (quality, delivery, cost) - Payment History Records - Supplier Incident Reports - Market Intelligence Data ## Downstream Outputs - Supplier Evaluation Report - Supplier Risk Assessment - Corrective Action Plan (if issues found) - Updated Supplier Scorecard - Supplier Relationship Action Log ## Key Decisions - Whether supplier performance meets contractual thresholds - Whether a supplier risk level requires escalation or remediation - Whether to continue, renegotiate, or terminate a supplier relationship - What corrective actions are appropriate for underperforming suppliers ## Exception Paths - Supplier fails performance review: triggers a dedicated remediation step with a rework loop back to the evaluation step - High-risk supplier identified: escalates to a separate risk mitigation sub-process - Supplier disputes evaluation findings: initiates a dispute resolution path with a review meeting ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Supplier Data: The Procurement Analyst retrieves the latest performance data from operational systems, including quality scores, on-time delivery rates, cost variance, and payment history. Review Performance Metrics: The Supplier Manager reviews the collected data package, comparing actual performance against agreed service levels and key performance indicators. A preliminary performance score is calculated, and any notable variances or trends are documented for discussion. 3. Assess Supplier Risk: The Risk Analyst evaluates the supplier's financial stability, market position, geopolitical exposure, and compliance history. A risk rating (low, medium, high) is assigned, and any red flags are flagged for escalation. 4. Determine Evaluation Outcome: The Supplier Manager combines the preliminary performance score and the risk rating to produce a final evaluation outcome. If both metrics are within acceptable ranges, the outcome is positive. If either metric falls below threshold, the outcome is negative, triggering remediation. 5. Initiate Remediation: When the evaluation outcome is negative, the Supplier Manager works with the Supplier Representative to develop a corrective action plan. The plan includes specific improvement targets, timelines, and review milestones. Once created, the process loops back to step 2 for re-evaluation after the remediation period. 6. Update Supplier Record: The Procurement Analyst enters the final evaluation outcome, risk rating, and any corrective action items into the supplier master record. This ensures the supplier profile is current and auditable for future reviews. 7. Generate Final Reports: The System automatically generates the formal Supplier Evaluation Report and Supplier Risk Assessment document based on the updated record. These reports are distributed to relevant stakeholders, including procurement leadership and business unit owners.
