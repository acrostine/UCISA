Researcher Training & Development ()
## Purpose This process ensures that academic and research staff receive targeted, high-quality professional development that aligns with institutional strategic goals, enhances research capabilities, and improves success in securing competitive funding. It covers the full lifecycle from needs identification through program design, delivery, evaluation, and continuous improvement. ## Roles - Research Training Manager: Oversees program design, scheduling, and quality assurance - Subject Matter Expert (SME): Provides specialist content for methodological and ethical training - Researcher (Participant): Attends training and provides feedback - System: Automated scheduling, registration, and survey distribution ## Upstream Inputs - Strategic Research Priorities: Institutional focus areas for research growth - Staff Development Needs Assessment: Survey results and gap analysis from researchers - External Funding Criteria: Requirements from major funders (e.g., UKRI, Horizon Europe) ## Downstream Outputs - Trained Research Workforce: Researchers with enhanced skills in methodology, ethics, and career progression - Training Completion Records: Certificates and attendance logs - Program Evaluation Report: Analysis of training effectiveness and recommendations ## Key Decisions - Which training topics are most critical to address current skill gaps? - Does the proposed training content meet external funder expectations? - Did participants demonstrate sufficient competency to pass the training? ## Exception Paths - If training content does not meet funding criteria, the program design is reworked before delivery. - If a participant fails the competency assessment, they are offered remedial coaching and a reassessment opportunity. - If evaluation feedback indicates systemic issues, the entire program design may be revised. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Collect Development Needs**: The Research Training Manager gathers strategic priorities and staff survey results to identify skill gaps. 2. **Design Training Program**: The SME and Training Manager create curriculum and materials addressing identified needs. 3. **Review Against Criteria**: The Training Manager checks the program against external funding requirements and ethical standards. 4. **Rework Program Design**: If criteria are not met, the SME revises content until compliance is achieved. 5. **Schedule and Register Participants**: The System publishes training dates and manages participant enrollment. 6. **Deliver Training Sessions**: The SME conducts the training, covering methodological, ethical, and career topics. 7. **Assess Participant Competency**: Participants complete an assessment; results determine if they pass or need remediation. 8. **Provide Remedial Coaching**: Failing participants receive targeted coaching to address knowledge gaps. 9. **Collect and Analyze Feedback**: The System distributes surveys; the Training Manager reviews results for improvement insights. 10. **Update Training Portfolio**: Based on evaluation data, the Training Manager refines future program offerings. Steps: 1. Collect Development Needs: The Research Training Manager reviews institutional strategic research priorities and compiles results from staff development needs surveys. This step identifies critical skill gaps in methodology, ethics, and career progression areas. Inputs include documented priorities and survey data. Output is a consolidated needs analysis report. 2. Design Training Program: The Subject Matter Expert and Research Training Manager collaborate to design training modules covering methodological, ethical, and career progression topics. They develop session plans, slide decks, exercises, and reference materials. The design aligns with the consolidated needs analysis. 3. Review Against Criteria: The Research Training Manager evaluates the draft program against external funding criteria and institutional ethical guidelines. This step ensures the training will support researchers in meeting funder expectations. If compliant, the program moves to scheduling; if not, it is sent for rework. 4. Rework Program Design: The Subject Matter Expert revises the training content to address gaps identified during the compliance review. Changes may include adding funder-specific modules or updating ethical case studies. The revised program is resubmitted for review. 5. Schedule and Register Participants: The System automatically schedules training sessions based on calendar availability and sends invitations to eligible researchers. Participants register through the training portal. The system confirms seats and sends pre-reading materials. 6. Deliver Training Sessions: The Subject Matter Expert facilitates the training sessions, covering methodological techniques, ethical considerations, and career progression strategies. Interactive exercises and Q&A sessions are included. Attendance is recorded in the system. 7. Assess Participant Competency: Participants complete a competency assessment (quiz or practical exercise) to demonstrate understanding of the training content. The System automatically scores the assessment. A passing score moves the participant to completion; a failing score triggers remediation. 8. Provide Remedial Coaching: The Subject Matter Expert provides one-on-one or small group coaching to participants who failed the assessment. Coaching addresses specific knowledge gaps. After coaching, the participant retakes the assessment. 9. Collect and Analyze Feedback: The System distributes post-training surveys to all participants. The Research Training Manager analyzes survey data, assessment pass rates, and attendance metrics to evaluate program effectiveness. Insights are documented in an evaluation report. 10. Update Training Portfolio: The Research Training Manager uses the evaluation report to update the training portfolio, adding new topics, retiring outdated modules, and adjusting delivery methods. This ensures continuous improvement and alignment with evolving research priorities.
