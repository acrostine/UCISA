Child Care Mgt ()
## Purpose This process coordinates the provision of on-campus childcare services, ensuring a safe, supportive, and accessible environment for students, staff, and faculty with childcare needs. It consumes regulatory standards, staffing resources, and facility inputs to deliver accessible childcare options, enhancing institutional inclusivity and supporting retention. ## Roles - Parent/Guardian: Submits childcare request and provides required documentation - Childcare Coordinator: Reviews requests, manages enrolment, and oversees operations - Compliance Officer: Conducts regulatory checks and audits - System: Automates notifications and record updates ## Upstream Inputs - Childcare Service Request - Regulatory Standards - Staffing Resources - Facility Availability Data ## Downstream Outputs - Enrolment Confirmation - Orientation Schedule - Compliance Report - Care Delivery Record ## Key Decisions - Eligibility check: Does the applicant meet institutional and regulatory criteria? - Capacity check: Is there available space in the requested age group? - Compliance check: Are all safety and regulatory standards being met? ## Exception Paths - If eligibility criteria are not met, the request is rejected with a notification to the parent/guardian. - If capacity is full, the applicant is placed on a waitlist and notified. - If compliance issues are found during audit, a corrective action plan is initiated and the care delivery is paused until resolved. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Request: The parent or guardian submits a childcare service request via the online portal. The system validates the submission for completeness and logs the request for review. Required documentation such as child's age, medical records, and emergency contacts are attached. The request is timestamped and assigned a unique identifier. 2. Review Eligibility: The Childcare Coordinator reviews the validated request against eligibility criteria including institutional affiliation (student/staff/faculty), age requirements, and regulatory standards. The coordinator verifies submitted documentation and may request additional information if needed. A decision is recorded in the system. 3. Check Capacity: The Childcare Coordinator checks the current occupancy levels for the requested age group against facility capacity limits. If space is available, a provisional slot is reserved. If the group is full, the applicant is placed on a waitlist and notified. 4. Enrol Child: The Childcare Coordinator enters the child's details into the enrolment system, assigns a care group, and generates an enrolment confirmation. The confirmation is sent to the parent/guardian via email and the system updates the occupancy records. 5. Conduct Orientation: The Childcare Coordinator contacts the parent/guardian to schedule an orientation session. During orientation, the family is introduced to the facility, staff, daily routines, and safety procedures. The parent/guardian signs consent forms and receives the care schedule. 6. Deliver Care: Childcare staff execute the daily care plan including educational activities, meals, rest periods, and outdoor play. Attendance is recorded, and any incidents or observations are logged in the system. The parent/guardian receives a daily summary. 7. Perform Compliance Audit: The Compliance Officer conducts a scheduled or random audit of the childcare facility, reviewing safety equipment, staff-to-child ratios, hygiene practices, and documentation. Findings are recorded in an audit report. If issues are found, a corrective action plan is initiated. 8. Resolve Issues: The Childcare Coordinator reviews the compliance report and implements corrective actions for any identified issues. This may include staff retraining, facility repairs, or process updates. Once resolved, the coordinator updates the system and notifies the Compliance Officer for re-audit.
