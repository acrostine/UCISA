Review Report Quality: The Quality Reviewer checks the draft report against stakeholder requirements, data accuracy, formatting standards, and governance guidelines. A pass/fail decision is made to either proceed or return for rework. 4. Rework Report: If the quality review identifies issues, the Reporting Analyst revises the draft report and presentation materials to correct errors, fill gaps, or improve alignment with requirements. The revised draft is then resubmitted for another quality review. 5. Approve Report: The Approver reviews the final draft report and presentation materials. A decision is made to either approve for distribution or reject with feedback for further revision. 6. Revise Report: If the report is rejected at approval, the Reporting Analyst incorporates the feedback, revises the content, and resubmits the updated draft for a new approval cycle. 7. Distribute Report: The System automatically distributes the approved report and presentation materials to the designated internal and external stakeholders via email or a secure portal.
