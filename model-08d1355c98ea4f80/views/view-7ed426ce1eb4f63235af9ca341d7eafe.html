Career & Employability Engagement Mgt ()
## Purpose This process coordinates the end-to-end management of student career and employability engagement, from initial request through to tailored guidance, skills training, and feedback collection. It ensures that each student receives personalised support aligned with labour market trends and their own career aspirations, ultimately improving graduate outcomes. ## Roles - Student: Initiates the process and participates in training - Career Advisor: Assesses student needs, provides guidance, and schedules training - System: Automates labour market data retrieval and feedback analysis ## Upstream Inputs - Student Career Engagement Request - Labour Market Data Feed - Student Career Interest Profile ## Downstream Outputs - Tailored Career Guidance Report - Employability Skills Training Schedule - Student Feedback Record - Refined Career Offering Recommendations ## Key Decisions - Whether the student's career interests align with available labour market opportunities - Which employability skills training modules are most relevant for the student - Whether the training was effective based on student feedback ## Exception Paths - If labour market data is unavailable, the career advisor proceeds with a manual review of known trends - If the student's interests do not match any current opportunities, a general career exploration session is offered instead - If the student fails to complete training, a follow-up reminder is sent; after two reminders, the case is escalated to a senior advisor ## Step Walkthrough 1. Analyse Feedback: The system aggregates feedback from multiple students and performs sentiment analysis and trend identification. It generates a report highlighting strengths, weaknesses, and recommended changes to the career offerings catalogue. 9. Update Career Offerings: The career advisor reviews the feedback analysis report and decides which changes to implement.
