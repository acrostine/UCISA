Accommodation Incident & Emergency Response ()
## Purpose This process ensures that all incidents and emergencies within student accommodation are identified, reported, and resolved in a structured and timely manner. It prioritises occupant safety, minimises operational disruption, and supports regulatory compliance through accurate logging and preventive action planning. ## Roles - **Resident Advisor**: First responder, reports incidents, provides initial assessment - **Accommodation Manager**: Oversees response, approves escalation, reviews incident logs - **Security Officer**: Manages physical security response and site access - **Facilities Team**: Handles property damage and infrastructure issues - **System**: Automated notification and logging system ## Upstream Inputs - Incident Report (from resident, staff, or automated sensor) - Emergency Protocol Documents - Communication Channel Alerts (fire alarm, security system) - Occupant Contact Information ## Downstream Outputs - Incident Log Entry - Emergency Response Report - Preventive Action Plan - Updated Safety Procedures - Occupant Safety Notification ## Key Decisions - Incident severity classification (low/medium/high/critical) - Whether to activate emergency protocols (fire, medical, security threat) - Whether to escalate to external emergency services - Root cause determination for preventive action - Approval of preventive action plan ## Exception Paths - False alarm: incident is logged as a drill/false alarm, no further action required - Critical emergency requiring external services: immediate escalation to police/fire/ambulance, bypasses internal review - Unresolved root cause: incident escalated to senior management for further investigation - Non-compliance with safety regulations: mandatory reporting to regulatory body ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Receive Incident Report**: The system or Resident Advisor receives an incident report from a resident, staff member, or automated detection system, capturing initial details. 2. **Assess Incident Severity**: The Resident Advisor evaluates the incident against predefined criteria (type, location, number of people affected) and classifies it as low, medium, high, or critical. 3. **Activate Emergency Protocol**: If severity is high or critical, the Accommodation Manager activates the appropriate emergency protocol (fire, medical, security) and notifies all relevant responders. 4. **Coordinate Response**: The Security Officer and Facilities Team execute the response actions per the protocol, while the Resident Advisor manages occupant communication and evacuation if needed. 5. **Resolve Immediate Situation**: Responders contain and resolve the incident, ensuring the area is safe and occupants are accounted for. 6. **Log Incident Details**: The system records all incident information, actions taken, and outcomes into the incident log for compliance and analysis. 7. **Review Root Cause**: The Accommodation Manager leads a review to identify the underlying cause of the incident, using evidence and witness statements. 8. **Determine Preventive Actions**: Based on the root cause, the Accommodation Manager proposes preventive actions (e.g., repairs, training, policy updates) and assigns responsibility. 9. **Approve Preventive Plan**: The Accommodation Manager reviews and approves the preventive action plan, or sends it back for revision if incomplete. 10. **Implement Preventive Actions**: The Facilities Team or relevant staff execute the approved preventive actions and update safety procedures as needed. 11. **Close Incident Record**: The system closes the incident record, archives all documentation, and sends a final summary to stakeholders. Steps: 1. Receive Incident Report: The incident report is received via phone, online form, in-person report, or automated system alert. The Resident Advisor or system records the time, location, type of incident, and reporter details. This step ensures all incidents are formally registered for tracking. 2. Assess Incident Severity: The Resident Advisor evaluates the incident against severity criteria including threat to life, property damage, number of people affected, and regulatory impact. The classification determines the response level: low (minor issue), medium (requires coordination), high (immediate danger), or critical (life-threatening). 3. Activate Emergency Protocol: For high or critical severity incidents, the Accommodation Manager selects and activates the correct emergency protocol (fire evacuation, medical response, security lockdown, etc.). All responders are notified via the communication system, and external services are contacted if required. 4. Coordinate Response: The Security Officer and Facilities Team execute the protocol actions. The Resident Advisor manages occupant communication, evacuation routes, and assembly points. All actions are coordinated via radio or messaging to ensure a unified response. 5. Resolve Immediate Situation: Responders work to contain the incident, provide first aid if needed, secure the area, and ensure all occupants are safe. Once the situation is under control, a preliminary all-clear is given. This step ends the active emergency phase. 6. Log Incident Details: The system prompts the Resident Advisor to enter full incident details: timeline, actions taken, injuries, property damage, and witness statements. All data is saved to the incident log for compliance, analysis, and reporting. 7. Review Root Cause: The Accommodation Manager leads a post-incident review with relevant staff. They examine evidence, interview witnesses, and identify contributing factors such as equipment failure, human error, or procedural gaps. The root cause is documented for preventive action planning. 8. Determine Preventive Actions: Based on the root cause, the Accommodation Manager identifies specific preventive actions such as repairs, staff training, policy updates, or equipment upgrades. Each action is assigned an owner and a target completion date. 9. Approve Preventive Plan: The Accommodation Manager reviews the draft plan for feasibility, cost, and impact. If satisfactory, the plan is approved and moves to implementation. If revisions are needed, it is returned to the previous step with feedback. 10. Implement Preventive Actions: The assigned owners (Facilities Team, training coordinator, etc.) carry out the approved actions. This may include physical repairs, updating safety procedures, conducting training sessions, or installing new equipment. Completion is documented in the system. 11. Close Incident Record: The system closes the incident record, attaches all reports and logs, and sends a final summary to the Accommodation Manager and relevant stakeholders. The record is archived for future reference and regulatory audits.
