Approve Programme: The Research Director makes the final decision to approve or reject the programme proposal based on the review outcome and budget validation result. Approval authorizes the programme to proceed. 6. Finalize Management Framework: The Research Programme Manager creates the management framework including governance structure, reporting cadence, risk register, and performance indicators. This framework guides programme execution and oversight. 7. Rework Proposal: The Research Programme Manager addresses issues identified during the review or budget validation, updating the scope, objectives, or resource plan as needed before resubmitting. 8. Notify Rejection: The system sends a notification to the Research Programme Manager and relevant stakeholders that the programme proposal has been rejected, with reasons provided.
