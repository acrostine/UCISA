## Purpose This process governs the lifecycle of academic publications from initial submission through to final archiving. It ensures that every publication is reviewed for compliance and quality before being formally accepted and stored in the institutional repository, supporting audit readiness and research dissemination. ## Roles - Author: Submits manuscript and peer reviews; receives notifications and reworks submissions if needed. - Publication Officer: Logs submissions, checks compliance, and manages the workflow. - Quality Reviewer: Assesses manuscript quality and makes approval recommendations. - System: Automates notifications and archival storage. ## Upstream Inputs - Manuscript - Peer Reviews - Compliance Guidelines ## Downstream Outputs - Archived Publication Record - Publication Notification - Audit Trail Entry ## Key Decisions - Does the submission meet compliance guidelines? - Does the manuscript pass quality review? ## Exception Paths - If compliance check fails, the submission is returned to the author for revision. - If quality review fails, the submission is returned to the author for revision. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Submit Manuscript: Author submits the manuscript and peer reviews to the Publication Officer. 2. Log Submission: Publication Officer logs the submission details into the system, creating an initial record. 3. Check Compliance: Publication Officer reviews the submission against compliance guidelines; if compliant, proceeds; if not, returns to author. 4. Review Quality: Quality Reviewer assesses the manuscript for quality standards; if passed, approves; if failed, returns to author. 5. Approve Publication: Quality Reviewer formally approves the publication for archiving. 6. Archive Publication: System archives the approved publication and creates an audit trail entry. 7. Notify Author: System sends a publication notification to the author. Steps: 1. Submit Manuscript: The author prepares the manuscript and any accompanying peer review documents, then submits them through the institutional submission portal or directly to the Publication Officer. This step initiates the publication workflow. 2. Log Submission: The Publication Officer enters the manuscript metadata, author information, and submission date into the publication management system. A unique tracking ID is generated for the submission. 3. Check Compliance: The Publication Officer compares the submission against institutional compliance guidelines, checking for policy adherence, formatting requirements, and ethical approvals. A decision is made to either proceed or return for revision. 4. Rework Submission: The author receives feedback on compliance or quality issues and makes necessary revisions to the manuscript or supporting documents. The revised submission is resubmitted for re-evaluation. 5. Review Quality: The Quality Reviewer evaluates the manuscript for academic rigor, clarity, originality, and adherence to publication standards. A pass or fail decision is made, with detailed feedback provided if failed. 6. Approve Publication: Upon successful quality review, the Quality Reviewer grants final approval, authorizing the publication to be archived and disseminated. This step updates the submission status to 'Approved'. 7. Archive Publication: The system stores the final approved manuscript in the institutional repository, generates a permanent identifier (e.g., DOI), and logs the event in the audit trail for compliance and reporting purposes. 8. Notify Author: The system automatically generates and sends a notification email to the author confirming the publication has been archived, including the permanent link and any relevant details.
