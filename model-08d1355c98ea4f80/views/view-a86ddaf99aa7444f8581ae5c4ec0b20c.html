Research Opportunity Analysis ()
## Purpose This process enables the research organization to systematically scan the external environment for emerging research themes, funding opportunities, and market demands, and to evaluate them against internal capabilities and strategic priorities. The output is a set of actionable, prioritized opportunity reports and risk assessments that inform which research projects to pursue. ## Roles - Research Strategist: Leads the analysis, performs strategic alignment assessment - Funding Analyst: Gathers and interprets funding landscape data - Capability Lead: Provides internal capability assessment data - Research Director: Reviews and approves final opportunity report - System: Automated data gathering and scoring ## Upstream Inputs - Funding Agency Data: Calls for proposals, funding trends, budget cycles - Academic Trend Data: Publication analytics, conference themes, citation networks - Internal Capability Assessment: Researcher expertise, lab capacity, existing projects - Strategic Goals Document: Institutional research priorities and objectives ## Downstream Outputs - Opportunity Report: Prioritized list of research opportunities with descriptions - Risk Assessment: Risk scores and mitigation notes for each opportunity - Approved Opportunity List: Final list of opportunities selected for further development ## Key Decisions - Which emerging themes are most aligned with institutional strategy? - Which opportunities have the highest funding viability? - What is the acceptable risk threshold for pursuing an opportunity? - Which opportunities should be prioritized for detailed project planning? ## Exception Paths - If no viable opportunities are identified, the process loops back to broaden the external scan. - If an opportunity fails strategic alignment, it is rejected and removed from consideration. - If risk assessment exceeds threshold, the opportunity is flagged for review and may be rejected or sent for mitigation planning. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Gather External Intelligence: The system connects to funding agency APIs, academic databases, and market research feeds to pull the latest data on calls for proposals, publication trends, and industry demands. This data is aggregated and normalized for analysis. 2. Compile Internal Capabilities: The Capability Lead collects and submits data on current research projects, researcher expertise, lab equipment, and available budget. This information is compiled into an internal capability profile. 3. Identify Potential Opportunities: The Research Strategist reviews the external intelligence dataset and internal capability profile to identify areas where external opportunities align with internal strengths. A list of candidate research opportunities is created. 4. Screen Opportunities: The Research Strategist applies a set of exclusion criteria (e.g., no funding source, outside strategic scope, insufficient capability) to quickly remove opportunities that are not worth further analysis. The remaining opportunities proceed. 5. Assess Strategic Alignment: The Research Strategist maps each opportunity to the institution's strategic research priorities and scores its alignment. Opportunities with low alignment are flagged for rejection. 6. Evaluate Funding Viability: The Funding Analyst reviews current funding landscape data, including active calls, success rates, and budget cycles, to estimate the probability of funding for each opportunity. A viability score is assigned. 7. Assess Risk: The Research Strategist evaluates each opportunity for potential risks, including technical feasibility, budget constraints, timeline risks, and resource availability. A risk score and mitigation notes are recorded. 8. Compile Opportunity Report: The system aggregates strategic alignment scores, funding viability scores, and risk assessments into a single prioritized opportunity report. The report includes a summary, detailed analysis for each opportunity, and a risk heat map. 9. Review and Approve Report: The Research Director examines the draft opportunity report, focusing on the prioritization logic, risk levels, and alignment with strategic goals. The director can approve the report for distribution or send it back with revision requests. 10. Distribute Final Report: The system sends the final approved opportunity report and accompanying risk assessment to all relevant stakeholders, including research leads, department heads, and executive leadership.
