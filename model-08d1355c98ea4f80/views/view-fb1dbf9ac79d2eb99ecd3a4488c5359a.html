Presence Based Teaching ()
## Purpose Presence Based Teaching is the core instructional process for delivering face-to-face sessions on campus. It transforms prepared teaching content and scheduled timetables into immersive learning experiences through direct student-teacher interaction. The process ensures that real-time feedback is captured, student understanding is assessed, and any learning gaps are remediated, ultimately contributing to deeper engagement and academic success. ## Roles - Instructor: Delivers the session, facilitates interaction, assesses understanding, and provides remediation. - Student: Participates in the session, engages in activities, and provides feedback. - System: Manages timetable scheduling, attendance capture, and session outcome recording. ## Upstream Inputs - Scheduled Timetable - Prepared Teaching Content - Classroom Facilities Availability - Student Roster ## Downstream Outputs - Session Attendance Record - Real-time Feedback Log - Student Understanding Assessment - Remediation Actions - Session Outcome Report ## Key Decisions - Does student understanding meet the required threshold? - Is remediation required for specific students or topics? - Should the session be adjusted in real-time based on feedback? ## Exception Paths - If student understanding is below threshold, remediation actions are initiated and the student is re-assessed. - If classroom facilities are unavailable, the session is rescheduled via an escalation path. - If real-time feedback indicates major confusion, the instructor may pause and re-teach key concepts. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Prepare Classroom Facilities: The instructor or support staff ensures the classroom is ready for the session. This includes checking audio-visual equipment, arranging seating for planned activities, and verifying that all necessary materials are available. Any facility issues are reported for resolution before the session begins. 2. Review Teaching Content: The instructor goes through the prepared teaching content, ensuring it matches the scheduled timetable and session objectives. Any last-minute updates or adjustments are made to the materials. The instructor also identifies key discussion points and activities for the session. 3. Deliver Instructional Session: The instructor delivers the instructional session according to the planned content and schedule. This involves lecturing, facilitating discussions, and guiding activities. The session is conducted in the prepared classroom with all students present. Direct interaction is encouraged throughout. 4. Facilitate Real-time Interaction: During the session, the instructor actively facilitates real-time interaction by posing questions, encouraging student participation, and responding to queries. This creates a dynamic learning environment where immediate feedback is exchanged. The instructor notes key feedback points for later assessment. 5. Assess Student Understanding: The instructor assesses student understanding using methods such as quick quizzes, show-of-hands polls, or verbal checks. This step determines whether the class has grasped the key concepts. The assessment results are recorded for analysis. 6. Determine Remediation Need: The instructor reviews the assessment results to determine if any students or topics require additional attention. If understanding is below the required threshold, remediation is triggered. If understanding is satisfactory, the process moves to attendance capture. 7. Provide Remediation: The instructor provides targeted remediation to students or on topics where understanding was insufficient. This may involve re-explaining concepts, offering additional examples, or conducting a brief small-group session. The goal is to bring all students to the required level of understanding. 8. Capture Attendance: The instructor or system records attendance for all students who participated in the session. This is typically done through a digital attendance system or manual roll call. The attendance record is stored for reporting and compliance purposes. 9. Record Session Outcomes: The instructor finalizes the session by recording key outcomes, a summary of real-time feedback, and any follow-up actions required. This includes noting successful teaching strategies and areas for improvement. The session outcome report is stored in the learning management system for future reference.
