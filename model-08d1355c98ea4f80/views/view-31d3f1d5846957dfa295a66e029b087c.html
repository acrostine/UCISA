Employee Support ()
## Purpose This process ensures that employees receive comprehensive support services — including advisory, wellness, and conflict resolution — tailored to their individual needs. By systematically triaging requests, applying relevant policies, and collecting feedback, the organisation maintains a motivated and resilient workforce critical to institutional success. ## Roles - Employee: Initiates the support request and provides feedback - HR Support Specialist: Triages requests, reviews policies, and delivers advisory/wellness/conflict resolution services - System: Automates request logging, policy retrieval, and feedback collection ## Upstream Inputs - Employee Support Request - Personal and Professional Needs Data - Organisational Policies and HR Guidelines - Staff Feedback Data ## Downstream Outputs - Support Case Record - Service Delivery Confirmation - Employee Satisfaction Score - Wellness Program Participation Data ## Key Decisions - Determine the appropriate service path (advisory, wellness, or conflict resolution) - Assess whether the request requires escalation to senior HR or legal - Evaluate employee satisfaction to determine if follow-up is needed ## Exception Paths - If the request is incomplete, the employee is asked to resubmit with required information - If the issue requires escalation (e.g., legal or senior management), the case is transferred to the appropriate authority - If the employee is dissatisfied with the service, a follow-up remediation step is initiated ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Support Request: The employee accesses the HR portal and fills out a support request form detailing their personal or professional needs. The system logs the request as a new case and assigns a unique case ID. An automatic notification is sent to the HR Support Specialist queue. 2. Triage Request: The HR Support Specialist reviews the case details and categorises the request into one of three paths: advisory services, wellness programs, or conflict resolution. If the request is incomplete or unclear, the specialist may request additional information from the employee. 3. Review Policies: The HR Support Specialist accesses the policy repository to retrieve the relevant organisational policies and HR guidelines that apply to the triaged request. This ensures that the service delivered is compliant and tailored to the employee's situation. 4. Deliver Service: The HR Support Specialist delivers the appropriate service to the employee. This may involve a one-on-one advisory session, enrolment in a wellness program, or facilitation of a conflict resolution meeting. The specialist documents the interaction and outcomes in the case record. 5. Collect Feedback: After service delivery, the system automatically triggers a satisfaction survey email to the employee. The employee completes the survey, rating their experience and providing optional comments. The response is recorded in the case record. 6. Assess Satisfaction: The HR Support Specialist reviews the employee's satisfaction score. If the score meets or exceeds the threshold, the case is closed. If the score is below threshold, a remediation step is initiated to address the employee's concerns. 7. Remediate Issues: The HR Support Specialist contacts the employee to understand their dissatisfaction, addresses specific concerns, and provides additional support or alternative solutions. The remediation is documented, and the case is reassessed for satisfaction. 8. Close Case: The HR Support Specialist updates the case status to 'Closed' and archives the case record. A closure notification is sent to the employee. The case data is stored for reporting and trend analysis.
