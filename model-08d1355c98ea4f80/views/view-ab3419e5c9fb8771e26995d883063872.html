## Purpose This process ensures that every student's enrolment status is accurately determined, recorded, and maintained throughout their academic lifecycle. It consumes application submissions, registration data, and academic progress information to evaluate and update enrolment conditions, enabling compliant service access and resource planning. ## Roles - Student Administration Officer - Academic Registrar (approval authority) - System (automated checks and updates) ## Upstream Inputs - Student Application Data - Registration Data - Academic Progress Information - Institutional Policy Rules ## Downstream Outputs - Updated Enrolment Status Record - Enrolment Status Notification - Escalation Case File - Compliance Audit Trail ## Key Decisions - Does the student meet all enrolment conditions based on policy rules? - Is manual intervention required due to exceptions or incomplete data? - Should the enrolment status be approved or rejected? ## Exception Paths - If automated checks find policy violations or missing data, the case is escalated to a Student Administration Officer for manual review. - If the manual review identifies unresolved issues, the student is notified and the status remains pending until remediation is completed. - If the Academic Registrar rejects the status update, the case is returned to the officer for rework. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Enrolment Event: The system listens for incoming application submissions or registration updates from the student portal or integration layer. When an event is detected, it creates a case record and initiates the enrolment status workflow. This step ensures no status change is missed and all triggers are logged for audit. 2. Retrieve Student Data: Using the student identifier from the enrolment event, the system queries the student database to gather all relevant records. This includes the original application form, current registration details, and the latest academic progress report. The data is assembled into a single assessment package for policy evaluation. 3. Evaluate Policy Conditions: The system applies a set of institutional policy rules to the student assessment package. Rules cover prerequisites, academic standing, registration deadlines, and regulatory compliance. Each rule produces a pass or fail result, and any failures are recorded with the specific reason for escalation. 4. Determine Enrolment Status: If all policy conditions pass, the system assigns a provisional status of 'Active' or 'Conditional' depending on any minor flags. If failures are detected, the status is set to 'Pending Review' and the case is flagged for manual intervention. The provisional status is recorded but not yet published. 5. Review Exceptions Manually: A Student Administration Officer accesses the case queue and examines each flagged record. They verify the policy violation details, check for missing documentation, and may contact the student for clarification. The officer documents their findings and makes a recommendation to approve, reject, or request further information. 6. Approve Status Update: The Academic Registrar receives the recommendation from the officer along with the supporting evidence. They perform a final compliance check and either approve the status update or reject it with comments. Approval is recorded with a digital signature for audit purposes. 7. Update Enrolment Record: Upon receiving an approved decision, the system updates the student's enrolment status field in the core database. The previous status is archived in the audit log, and the change is timestamped. This step ensures the record is authoritative and current. 8. Notify Stakeholders: The system generates a notification email or portal alert to inform the student of their updated enrolment status. Copies are sent to academic advising, finance, and housing departments as needed. The notification includes the effective date and any conditions attached to the status.
