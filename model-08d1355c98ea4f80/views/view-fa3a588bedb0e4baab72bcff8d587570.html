Student Mobility ()
## Purpose This process manages the end-to-end administration of student mobility, including exchanges and study abroad programmes. It ensures that applications are processed efficiently, placements are coordinated with partner institutions, and academic outcomes are validated and recorded accurately. ## Roles - Student - Mobility Coordinator - Academic Advisor - Registrar - System ## Upstream Inputs - Student Application Details - Partner Institution Agreements - Credit Transfer Information ## Downstream Outputs - Mobility Placement Confirmation - Validated Academic Outcomes - Updated Student Academic Record ## Key Decisions - Whether the student meets eligibility criteria for the mobility programme - Whether the proposed placement aligns with partner institution agreements - Whether the credit transfer proposal is academically sound - Whether the completed mobility outcomes are valid for credit recognition ## Exception Paths - Application rejected due to ineligibility: student is notified and process ends - Placement not available or agreement expired: student is offered alternative or process ends - Credit transfer proposal rejected: student revises and resubmits - Outcomes do not match expectations: academic advisor reviews and may reject or adjust credit award ## Step Walkthrough 1. Update Academic Record: Registrar records validated outcomes and credits on the student's academic record. Steps: 1. Submit Mobility Application: The student completes and submits a mobility application form, including preferred destinations, programme choices, and supporting documents such as transcripts and motivation letters. The application is entered into the student administration system for processing. 2. Review Eligibility: The Mobility Coordinator reviews the application to confirm the student meets academic, language, and other eligibility requirements. Partner institution agreements are consulted to ensure alignment. A decision is made to proceed or reject. 3. Assess Placement Options: Based on eligibility and preferences, the Mobility Coordinator assesses available placements at partner institutions. Capacity, programme fit, and timing are considered. A suitable placement is identified or alternatives are proposed. 4. Validate Credit Transfer: The Academic Advisor examines the student's proposed credit transfer plan, comparing partner institution courses against home institution curriculum. Equivalencies are assessed and approval is granted or revisions are requested. 5. Confirm Placement: Once eligibility, placement, and credit transfer are confirmed, the Mobility Coordinator finalises the placement. A confirmation letter is sent to the student and partner institution. The student's record is updated with mobility status. 6. Complete Mobility Programme: The student attends the partner institution and completes the agreed programme of study. This step represents the period of mobility and is not directly managed by the home institution, but progress is monitored via periodic check-ins. 7. Submit Outcomes: Upon return, the student submits official transcripts, completion certificates, and any other evidence of academic performance from the partner institution. These documents are uploaded to the student administration system. 8. Validate Outcomes: The Academic Advisor reviews the submitted outcomes against the original learning agreement and credit transfer approval. Equivalencies are confirmed, and any discrepancies are noted for resolution. 9. Update Academic Record: The Registrar enters the validated outcomes into the student's academic record, applying credit transfer as approved. The record is updated to reflect the mobility experience and any grades or credits awarded.
