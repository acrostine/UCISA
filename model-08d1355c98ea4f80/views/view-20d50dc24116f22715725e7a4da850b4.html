## Purpose This process administers the allocation and promotion of financial support schemes to prospective and current students. It consumes funding availability data, eligibility criteria, and application submissions to assess and award scholarships and bursaries. The process produces equitable distribution of financial aid, increased access for underrepresented groups, and incentivized student recruitment. Determine Award Amount: Based on the assessment, the Financial Aid Officer calculates the award amount considering available budget, applicant profile, and scheme guidelines. The amount is documented with justification for committee review. 6. Approve Award: The Awards Committee convenes to review the assessment and award amount proposal. They consider budget constraints, equity goals, and institutional priorities before making a final decision to approve or reject the award. 7. Notify Applicant: The system generates and sends a notification to the applicant via email and portal message. The notification includes the award decision, amount (if approved), payment schedule, and any conditions or next steps.
