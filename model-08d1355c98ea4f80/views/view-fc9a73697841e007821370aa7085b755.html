Prospective Student Engagement ()
## Purpose This process manages all structured interactions with prospective students from the moment an enquiry is received through to the point of application or disengagement. It ensures that every potential student receives timely, relevant, and personalized support that clarifies academic offerings, campus life, and application procedures, thereby maximizing conversion and building a strong applicant pipeline. ## Roles - Engagement Coordinator: Manages enquiry triage, advisor assignment, and event coordination. - Student Advisor: Provides personalized support, answers queries, and nurtures the relationship. - Marketing Specialist: Supplies marketing content and campaign materials for digital communications. - System: Automates email campaigns, event registration, and CRM updates. ## Upstream Inputs - Enquiry Data: Inbound enquiries from website, fairs, calls, or referrals. - Marketing Content: Brochures, program details, event calendars, and digital assets. - Feedback Mechanisms: Survey templates and feedback collection tools. ## Downstream Outputs - Enhanced Student Experience: Positive engagement touchpoints recorded in CRM. - Higher Conversion Rates: Increased number of enquiries progressing to application. - Well-Informed Applicant Pool: Applicants with clear understanding of programs and support. - Recruitment Efficiency Metrics: Data on response times, engagement rates, and conversion funnel. ## Key Decisions - Enquiry Priority: Determine if the enquiry is high priority (e.g., specific program interest, imminent deadlines) or standard. - Engagement Path: Decide on the appropriate mix of events, digital campaigns, and one-on-one support. - Interest Level: Assess whether the prospect is actively interested, undecided, or disengaged. - Conversion Readiness: Determine if the prospect is ready to apply or requires further nurturing. ## Exception Paths - Disengaged Prospect: If a prospect does not respond after multiple touchpoints, they are moved to a dormant list and flagged for future re-engagement campaigns. - Negative Feedback: If a prospect reports dissatisfaction or confusion, an escalation to a senior advisor is triggered for immediate resolution. - Application Issues: If a prospect attempts to apply but encounters technical or procedural problems, a support ticket is raised and the advisor intervenes. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Provide Targeted Support: For active and undecided prospects, the advisor offers one-on-one consultations, campus tour bookings, or application guidance. 8. Update CRM Record: All interactions, decisions, and feedback are recorded in the CRM for reporting and future re-engagement. Steps: 1. Receive Enquiry: The system automatically logs the enquiry with source (web form, email, phone, event), timestamp, and basic prospect details. A unique CRM record is created for tracking all future interactions. 2. Assess Enquiry Priority: The coordinator examines the enquiry for program specificity, urgency (e.g., application deadlines), and geographic region. Based on these factors, the enquiry is classified as high priority or standard priority to guide resource allocation. 3. Assign Student Advisor: The coordinator manually assigns a Student Advisor who specializes in the prospect's area of interest. High priority enquiries are assigned within 2 hours; standard within 24 hours. The assignment is recorded in the CRM. 4. Send Welcome Communication: An automated email is triggered containing the advisor's name and contact details, a brief program overview, key dates, and links to further resources. The email is personalized using the prospect's name and program interest. 5. Deliver Personalized Content: The advisor selects and sends relevant brochures, event invitations, scholarship information, and program-specific details. Content is curated from the marketing content library and sent via email or portal. 6. Review Interest Level: The advisor reviews email open rates, click-throughs, event attendance, and any direct replies. Based on this data, the prospect is classified as actively interested, undecided, or disengaged. 7. Provide Targeted Support: For active and undecided prospects, the advisor schedules a consultation call, arranges a campus tour (virtual or in-person), or provides step-by-step application assistance. This step deepens the relationship and addresses specific questions. 8. Confirm Application Decision: The advisor verifies the prospect's application status in the CRM. If an application has been submitted, the process moves to feedback capture. If the prospect has explicitly declined, the reason is noted. If still undecided, the advisor may loop back to provide additional support. 9. Capture Feedback: An automated survey is sent via email asking about the prospect's experience with the engagement process, clarity of information, and overall satisfaction. Responses are stored in the CRM for analysis. 10. Update CRM Record: The system finalizes the prospect record with a complete interaction history, final disposition (applied, disengaged, dormant), and feedback scores. This data feeds into recruitment analytics and future re-engagement campaigns.
