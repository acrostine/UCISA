International Student Recruitment ()
## Purpose This process governs the end-to-end recruitment of international students, from market analysis and campaign execution through to application processing and offer acceptance. It ensures that the institution attracts high-calibre candidates from diverse global markets while adhering to regulatory requirements and strategic enrolment targets. ## Roles - International Recruitment Manager: Oversees strategy, partnerships, and campaign approval. - Marketing Coordinator: Executes digital and print marketing campaigns. - Admissions Officer: Processes applications, verifies documents, and issues offers. - Compliance Officer: Reviews visa documentation and regulatory adherence. - System: Automates campaign distribution, document verification, and offer generation. ## Upstream Inputs - Market Intelligence Reports - Visa Regulation Updates - Cultural Outreach Initiatives - Partnership Agreements - Enrolment Targets ## Downstream Outputs - Qualified Applications - Offer Letters Issued - Accepted Enrolments - International Student Cohort Data - Compliance Reports ## Key Decisions - Which target markets to prioritise based on intelligence and enrolment gaps. - Whether a marketing campaign meets brand and budget criteria. - Whether an applicant meets academic and English language requirements. - Whether visa documentation is complete and compliant. - Whether to issue a conditional or unconditional offer. ## Exception Paths - Campaign rejected: Marketing Coordinator reworks creative assets and resubmits for approval. - Application incomplete: Admissions Officer requests missing documents; applicant must resubmit. - Visa non-compliance: Compliance Officer initiates corrective action with applicant; if unresolved, application is rejected. - Offer declined: No further action; applicant is archived for future cycle analysis. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Analyse Market Intelligence: The International Recruitment Manager analyses current market intelligence reports, visa policy updates, and cultural outreach feedback. This step determines which countries and regions offer the highest potential for qualified applicants. The output is a prioritised list of target markets and enrolment targets. 2. Develop Marketing Campaign: The Marketing Coordinator designs campaign assets including digital ads, brochures, and partner communications. Materials are customised to reflect cultural sensitivities and highlight relevant programmes. The campaign package is prepared for review. 3. Approve Campaign: The International Recruitment Manager evaluates the campaign package against brand guidelines, budget constraints, and strategic fit. If approved, the campaign proceeds to distribution. If rejected, it is returned with feedback for revision. 4. Distribute Campaign Materials: The system automatically distributes the approved campaign materials via email, social media, partner portals, and the institution's website. Distribution is scheduled according to market time zones and cultural calendars. 5. Collect Applications: The system captures applications submitted through the online portal, agent networks, and direct enquiries. Each application is logged with a unique ID and routed for document verification. Incomplete submissions trigger an automated acknowledgement. 6. Verify Application Documents: The Admissions Officer reviews academic transcripts, English language test scores, passport copies, and any additional supporting documents. Missing or suspicious documents are flagged for follow-up. Verified documents are passed to eligibility assessment. 7. Assess Applicant Eligibility: The Admissions Officer compares the applicant's academic background, grades, and test scores against programme-specific entry criteria. A determination is made whether the applicant meets the standard, requires a conditional offer, or is ineligible. 8. Review Visa Compliance: The Compliance Officer checks visa application forms, financial statements, and health insurance coverage against current immigration regulations. Any discrepancies or missing items are flagged. A compliance pass is required before an offer can be issued. 9. Issue Offer Letter: The system automatically generates an unconditional or conditional offer letter based on the eligibility and compliance outcomes. The offer is sent via email with instructions for acceptance and deposit payment. A copy is stored in the applicant record. 10. Confirm Enrolment: The system processes the applicant's acceptance, deposit payment, and any outstanding conditions. The student is enrolled into the international cohort, and data is updated in the student information system. A confirmation notification is sent to the student and relevant departments.
