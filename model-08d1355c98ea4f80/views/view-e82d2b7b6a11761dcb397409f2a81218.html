## Purpose Insurance Management administers institutional risk coverage through policy procurement, claims processing, and compliance monitoring. It consumes risk assessments and asset registers to produce insurance schedules and claims reports, mitigating financial exposure and safeguarding institutional assets and operations against unforeseen events. ## Roles - Risk Manager: Initiates coverage assessment and reviews policy adequacy - Procurement Officer: Procures insurance policies from providers - Claims Officer: Processes and reviews insurance claims - Compliance Officer: Monitors policy compliance and renewals - System: Automates notifications and report generation ## Upstream Inputs - Risk Assessment Report - Asset Register - Incident Reports - Policy Renewal Notices ## Downstream Outputs - Insurance Schedule - Claims Report - Compliance Monitoring Report - Policy Renewal Alerts ## Key Decisions - Whether coverage gaps exist requiring new policies - Whether a claim meets policy criteria for approval - Whether compliance issues require escalation ## Exception Paths - Claim Rejected: Claims Officer rejects claim; sent to Rework Claim step - Compliance Issue Found: Compliance Officer identifies non-compliance; sent to Resolve Compliance Issue step - Coverage Gap Identified: Risk Manager finds coverage gap; looped back to Assess Coverage Needs ## Step Walkthrough 1. Assess Coverage Needs: Risk Manager reviews risk assessments and asset registers to identify required insurance coverage. 2. Procure Policies: Procurement Officer obtains insurance policies from providers based on identified needs. 3. Process Claim: Claims Officer receives and logs an insurance claim from an incident report. 4. Review Claim: Claims Officer evaluates the claim against policy terms and decides to approve or reject. 5. Approve Claim: Claims Officer finalises the approved claim and initiates payment. 6. Rework Claim: Claims Officer corrects or supplements the claim documentation after rejection. 7. Monitor Compliance: Compliance Officer reviews policy compliance and renewal status, identifying issues if present. 8. Resolve Compliance Issue: Compliance Officer addresses any compliance gaps or non-renewal risks. 9. Generate Reports: System compiles insurance schedule and claims report for distribution. Steps: 1. Assess Coverage Needs: The Risk Manager examines the incoming Risk Assessment Report and Asset Register to identify gaps in current coverage. This step establishes the scope of policies needed for the upcoming period. Output is a coverage requirements document. 2. Procure Policies: The Procurement Officer contacts insurance providers, obtains quotes, and selects policies that meet the coverage requirements. Policies are purchased and recorded in the system. Output is a set of active insurance policies. 3. Process Claim: The Claims Officer receives an incident report and creates a claim record in the system. Basic details such as date, description, and estimated value are captured. Output is a logged claim ready for review. 4. Review Claim: The Claims Officer checks the logged claim against the relevant insurance policy terms, coverage limits, and exclusions. A decision is made to approve or reject the claim. Output is a claim review decision. 5. Approve Claim: The Claims Officer confirms the claim approval and triggers payment processing. The claim status is updated to 'Approved' and the payment is scheduled. Output is a finalised claim record. 6. Rework Claim: The Claims Officer reviews the rejection reason and gathers additional information or corrects errors in the claim. The reworked claim is resubmitted for review. Output is a revised claim. 7. Monitor Compliance: The Compliance Officer checks all active policies for compliance with institutional requirements and monitors upcoming renewals. If issues are found, they are flagged for resolution. Output is a compliance status report. 8. Resolve Compliance Issue: The Compliance Officer investigates flagged compliance issues, coordinates with relevant parties to resolve them, and updates policy records. Output is a resolved compliance record. 9. Generate Reports: The system automatically generates the Insurance Schedule listing all active policies and the Claims Report summarising all processed claims. These are distributed to stakeholders. Output is the final reports.
