Religious Support ()
## Purpose This process ensures that the campus provides comprehensive spiritual care, facilities, and activities that respect and support the diverse faith communities present. It aims to foster an inclusive environment that enriches cultural diversity and supports the holistic wellbeing of all students and staff. ## Roles - Religious Support Coordinator: Leads the assessment, planning, and coordination of religious activities. - Facilities Manager: Allocates and prepares physical spaces for religious use. - Spiritual Care Provider: Delivers direct spiritual care and counselling. - System: Handles automated notifications and scheduling updates. ## Upstream Inputs - Community Needs Assessments: Reports on the spiritual and religious requirements of the campus population. - Religious Resources: Inventory of available texts, artefacts, and materials. - Event Schedules: Existing calendar of campus events to avoid conflicts. ## Downstream Outputs - Activity Calendar: Published schedule of religious events and services. - Facility Bookings: Confirmed allocations of spaces for faith practices. - Spiritual Care Records: Logs of counselling and support interactions. - Engagement Reports: Metrics on participation and satisfaction. ## Key Decisions - Which activities to prioritise based on community needs and resource availability. - How to allocate limited facilities among competing faith groups. - Whether an individual requires immediate spiritual care or referral to external support. ## Exception Paths - If a facility is unavailable, the coordinator must find an alternative space or reschedule the activity. - If a spiritual care request is urgent and the provider is unavailable, an automated escalation to an on-call provider is triggered. - If community feedback indicates low engagement, a review and adjustment of the activity plan is initiated. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Gather Community Needs: The Religious Support Coordinator retrieves the latest community needs assessments from the campus wellbeing office and reviews the inventory of available religious resources. This step establishes the baseline understanding of spiritual requirements across the campus population. 2. Assess Requirements: The coordinator analyses the consolidated needs profile to determine which faith communities have the highest demand, what types of activities are requested, and any urgent spiritual care requirements. This assessment drives the planning and resource allocation decisions. 3. Plan Activity Calendar: Based on the priority requirements, the coordinator creates a draft calendar of activities, including regular services, special events, and spiritual care sessions. The calendar is checked against the existing campus event schedule to avoid conflicts. 4. Allocate Facilities: The Facilities Manager reviews the draft activity calendar and assigns suitable rooms or outdoor spaces for each event, considering capacity, accessibility, and religious requirements. Bookings are confirmed in the campus facility management system. 5. Facilitate Activities: The Spiritual Care Provider and coordinator execute the planned activities, including leading services, facilitating group discussions, and offering one-on-one spiritual counselling. Attendance and participation are recorded. 6. Monitor Engagement: The coordinator reviews participation logs and distributes feedback surveys to attendees. This data is compiled into an engagement report that highlights satisfaction levels and areas for improvement. 7. Review and Adjust: The coordinator examines the engagement report to identify trends, low participation, or unmet needs. Adjustments are made to the next activity calendar, resource allocations, or outreach strategies to improve inclusivity and wellbeing outcomes.
