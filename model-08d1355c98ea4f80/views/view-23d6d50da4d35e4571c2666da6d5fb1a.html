Commercial Opportunity Assessment ()
## Purpose This process systematically examines identified commercial opportunities to determine their feasibility, risk profile, and alignment with institutional capabilities and strategic goals. It consumes data from market analysis, internal resource evaluations, and stakeholder input, producing a prioritized opportunity portfolio with recommended actions. The output supports informed investment decisions, ensuring resources focus on commercially viable and strategically beneficial initiatives. ## Roles - Commercial Analyst: Conducts feasibility and risk assessments - Strategy Manager: Evaluates strategic alignment and prioritization - Portfolio Manager: Reviews and approves the final prioritized portfolio - System: Automated data retrieval and scoring ## Upstream Inputs - Identified Opportunities from Market Analysis - Internal Resource Capability Data - Stakeholder Input and Requirements - Strategic Objectives and Criteria ## Downstream Outputs - Prioritized Opportunity Portfolio - Recommended Actions for Each Opportunity - Risk Assessment Reports - Feasibility Analysis Results ## Key Decisions - Feasibility threshold: Is the opportunity technically and operationally viable? - Risk acceptance: Are the identified risks within acceptable limits? - Strategic alignment: Does the opportunity support institutional strategic objectives? - Prioritization ranking: Which opportunities offer the best value-risk balance? ## Exception Paths - If feasibility assessment fails, the opportunity is flagged as non-viable and returned for rework or rejection. - If risk assessment identifies critical risks, the opportunity is escalated for mitigation planning before re-evaluation. - If strategic alignment is insufficient, the opportunity is deprioritized or rejected. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Identified Opportunities: The process begins by gathering identified opportunities from market analysis, internal resource evaluations, and stakeholder input. These opportunities are compiled into a single list for assessment. The Commercial Analyst ensures all required data is complete and ready for evaluation. 2. Assess Feasibility: The Commercial Analyst reviews each opportunity against feasibility criteria including technical requirements, operational capacity, and available resources. A feasibility score is calculated for each opportunity. Opportunities that do not meet the minimum threshold are flagged for rework or rejection. 3. Evaluate Risks: For opportunities that pass feasibility, the Commercial Analyst conducts a risk assessment covering financial exposure, market volatility, and operational challenges. Each risk is rated for likelihood and impact. Opportunities with critical risks are escalated for mitigation planning. 4. Review Strategic Alignment: The Strategy Manager reviews each opportunity against strategic objectives and institutional capabilities. Opportunities that strongly align are prioritized; those with weak alignment are deprioritized or rejected. This step ensures resources focus on strategically beneficial initiatives. 5. Prioritize Opportunities: The Strategy Manager combines feasibility scores, risk ratings, and strategic alignment scores to rank all opportunities. A weighted scoring model is used to produce a prioritized opportunity portfolio. Recommended actions are assigned to each opportunity (e.g., pursue, monitor, reject). 6. Approve Portfolio: The Portfolio Manager reviews the prioritized portfolio and recommended actions. If satisfied, the portfolio is approved and sent downstream for investment decision-making. If issues are found, the portfolio is returned for rework with specific feedback. 7. Rework Non-Viable Opportunities: Opportunities that failed feasibility or strategic alignment are reviewed for potential rework. The Commercial Analyst or Strategy Manager identifies corrective actions such as resource adjustments or scope changes. Reworked opportunities are re-submitted for assessment. 8. Mitigate Critical Risks: For opportunities escalated due to critical risks, the Commercial Analyst develops risk mitigation plans. These plans are reviewed and, if adequate, the opportunity is re-evaluated. If mitigation is not feasible, the opportunity is rejected.
