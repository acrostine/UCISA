Academic Year Scheduling ()
## Purpose This process establishes the academic calendar by defining term dates, examination periods, and enrolment deadlines. It ensures that the schedule aligns with institutional priorities, faculty availability, resource constraints, and external accreditation timelines, enabling efficient resource planning and supporting student progression. ## Roles - Academic Registrar: Oversees the scheduling process and ensures alignment with institutional strategy. - Faculty Coordinator: Provides faculty availability data and reviews schedule feasibility. - Resource Manager: Supplies resource constraint information and validates capacity. - Quality Assurance Officer: Ensures compliance with regulatory and accreditation requirements. - System: Automates data collection, validation, and notification tasks. ## Upstream Inputs - Faculty Availability Data - Resource Constraint Reports - External Accreditation Timelines - Institutional Strategic Priorities - Regulatory Requirements ## Downstream Outputs - Approved Academic Calendar - Term Dates Schedule - Examination Period Schedule - Enrolment Deadlines - Resource Allocation Plan ## Key Decisions - Approval of proposed term dates and examination periods - Validation of schedule against resource constraints - Confirmation of compliance with accreditation timelines - Resolution of conflicts between faculty availability and schedule ## Exception Paths - If resource constraints are not met, the schedule is revised and re-submitted for validation. - If accreditation compliance fails, the schedule is adjusted and re-reviewed. - If faculty availability conflicts arise, the schedule is reworked and re-validated. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Review Inputs: The Academic Registrar and Quality Assurance Officer review the compiled input data to ensure it is complete, accurate, and aligned with regulatory requirements and institutional priorities. Any discrepancies or missing data are flagged for correction. 3. Draft Schedule: Using the validated input data, the Academic Registrar drafts a proposed academic calendar. This includes defining term start and end dates, examination periods, and enrolment deadlines. The draft is created in the Academic Scheduling System and made available for validation. 4. Validate Schedule: The Resource Manager and Quality Assurance Officer validate the draft schedule against resource constraints and external accreditation timelines. They check for capacity conflicts, feasibility issues, and compliance with regulatory requirements. If issues are found, the schedule is flagged for revision. 5. Approve Schedule: The Academic Registrar reviews the validated schedule and provides formal approval. This step ensures that the schedule aligns with institutional strategic goals and is ready for publication. Approval is recorded in the system. 6. Publish Calendar: The approved academic calendar is published through institutional systems, making it accessible to faculty, students, and administrative units. The calendar includes term dates, examination periods, and enrolment deadlines. 7. Notify Stakeholders: Automated notifications are sent to all stakeholders including faculty, students, and administrative units, informing them of the published academic calendar. This ensures timely awareness and enables downstream planning activities. 8. Revise Schedule: If validation issues are identified, the Academic Registrar revises the draft schedule to address resource constraints, accreditation compliance, or faculty availability conflicts. The revised schedule is then re-submitted for validation.
