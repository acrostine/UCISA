Student Financial Advice ()
## Purpose This process provides confidential, one-on-one financial advice to students, helping them manage their finances, access funding, and navigate hardship. It ensures students receive consistent, regulation-compliant guidance tailored to their individual circumstances, reducing financial stress and supporting retention. ## Roles - Student (initiator) - Financial Advisor (primary executor) - System (automated checks and record-keeping) ## Upstream Inputs - Student Financial Status (from student records and self-declaration) - Funding Regulations (from policy database) - Student Advice Request (submitted via portal) ## Downstream Outputs - Personalised Financial Plan (delivered to student) - Support Service Referral (sent to student support team) - Advice Record (stored in case management system) ## Key Decisions - Does the student require a detailed financial review due to hardship indicators? - Is the student eligible for specific hardship funds or emergency loans? - Has the student accepted the proposed financial plan? ## Exception Paths - If the student does not respond to the initial advice, a follow-up reminder is sent after 5 working days. - If the student is ineligible for all funding options, they are referred to external debt counselling. - If the student rejects the financial plan, the advisor reworks the plan with revised options. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Receive Advice Request**: The student submits a request for financial advice via the student portal; the system logs the request and assigns it to an available advisor. 2. **Review Student Status**: The advisor accesses the student's financial status and checks funding regulations to understand the student's context and eligibility. 3. **Assess Financial Situation**: The advisor evaluates the student's income, expenses, debts, and any hardship indicators to determine the level of support needed. 4. **Determine Advice Path**: Based on the assessment, the advisor decides whether to provide standard budgeting advice or proceed to a detailed financial review for complex cases. 5. **Provide Standard Advice**: For straightforward cases, the advisor delivers budgeting tips and signposts to standard funding options; the system generates a summary document. 6. **Conduct Detailed Review**: For hardship cases, the advisor performs an in-depth analysis of the student's finances, exploring emergency loans and hardship funds. 7. **Develop Personalised Plan**: The advisor creates a tailored financial plan, including recommended actions, funding applications, and budget adjustments. 8. **Present Plan to Student**: The advisor presents the plan to the student, explains the options, and answers questions; the student may accept or request changes. 9. **Finalise and Refer**: Upon acceptance, the advisor finalises the plan, refers the student to relevant support services, and updates the case record. Steps: 1. Receive Advice Request: The student initiates the process by completing an online request form, providing basic details about their financial concern. The system automatically assigns the request to an available financial advisor and creates a case record. 2. Review Student Status: The financial advisor accesses the student's financial status data from the student information system and reviews current funding regulations from the policy database. This step ensures the advisor has a complete picture of the student's circumstances and eligibility. 3. Assess Financial Situation: The advisor analyses the student's financial data, including income sources, regular expenses, outstanding debts, and any hardship indicators such as unexpected costs or loss of income. This assessment determines the complexity of the advice required. 4. Determine Advice Path: Based on the assessment, the advisor determines the appropriate advice path. If the student has straightforward budgeting needs, standard advice is provided. If hardship indicators are present or the situation is complex, a detailed financial review is initiated. 5. Provide Standard Advice: For straightforward cases, the advisor provides the student with practical budgeting advice, cost-saving tips, and information on standard funding options such as bursaries or scholarships. The system generates a summary document for the student. 6. Conduct Detailed Review: For complex or hardship cases, the advisor conducts a thorough review of the student's finances, exploring eligibility for emergency loans, hardship funds, and payment plans. This step may involve consultation with the student support team. 7. Develop Personalised Plan: The advisor synthesises the findings from the detailed review into a personalised financial plan. The plan includes recommended actions, specific funding applications, budget adjustments, and timelines. It is formatted as a formal document. 8. Present Plan to Student: The advisor meets with the student (in person or virtually) to present the financial plan, explain each recommendation, and answer questions. The student can accept the plan as presented or request modifications. 9. Finalise and Refer: Upon acceptance, the advisor finalises the plan in the system, sends a copy to the student, and makes referrals to relevant support services (e.g., mental health, academic support). The case record is updated to 'Completed'.
