Strategic Plan Development ()
## Purpose This process integrates institutional goals, stakeholder inputs, and environmental analysis to formulate a coherent multi-year strategic plan. It consumes insights from horizon scanning, performance data, and stakeholder consultations to produce a documented strategic framework. The process aligns resources and priorities, guiding the institution’s long-term direction and ensuring responsiveness to sector challenges and opportunities. ## Roles - **Strategic Planning Lead**: Facilitates the process, synthesizes inputs, and manages the drafting and revision cycle. - **Executive Sponsor**: Provides high-level direction and approves the final strategic plan. - **Stakeholder Representative**: Provides input during consultations and reviews draft plans. - **System**: Automates data aggregation, document version control, and notification workflows. ## Upstream Inputs - Horizon Scanning Reports - Institutional Performance Data - Stakeholder Consultation Feedback - Current Strategic Plan (for reference) ## Downstream Outputs - Approved Strategic Plan Document - Strategic Framework Summary - Implementation Roadmap (high-level) ## Key Decisions - Which strategic themes and priorities to include in the plan. - How to allocate resources across competing initiatives. - Whether the draft plan meets quality and alignment criteria for approval. ## Exception Paths - If stakeholder feedback reveals major conflicts, the draft is returned for rework and additional consultation. - If the executive sponsor rejects the plan, it is sent back for revision with specific guidance. - If data inputs are incomplete, the process pauses until missing data is provided. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Collect Inputs**: The Strategic Planning Lead gathers horizon scanning reports, performance data, and stakeholder feedback from upstream sources. 2. **Analyze Environment**: The team reviews the collected inputs to identify strategic opportunities, threats, and performance gaps. 3. **Draft Strategic Plan**: Based on the analysis, a draft multi-year strategic plan is created, outlining goals, priorities, and resource allocations. 4. **Review Draft Plan**: The draft is reviewed by stakeholder representatives and subject matter experts for alignment and completeness. 5. **Revise Plan**: If issues are found during review, the draft is revised to address feedback and improve clarity. 6. **Approve Strategic Plan**: The revised plan is submitted to the executive sponsor for final approval. 7. **Publish Final Plan**: Once approved, the final strategic plan is published and distributed to the organization. Steps: 1. Collect Inputs: The Strategic Planning Lead collects horizon scanning reports, institutional performance data, and stakeholder consultation feedback. These inputs are compiled into a central repository for analysis. The step ensures all relevant information is available before proceeding. 2. Analyze Environment: The team analyzes the compiled inputs to identify key opportunities, threats, and performance gaps. This analysis informs the strategic themes and priorities for the plan. A summary of findings is produced to guide the drafting phase. 3. Draft Strategic Plan: Based on the environmental analysis, the Strategic Planning Lead drafts the strategic plan, including goals, priorities, resource allocations, and a high-level implementation roadmap. The draft is structured according to the institution's template. 4. Review Draft Plan: The draft plan is shared with stakeholder representatives and subject matter experts for review. They assess whether the plan aligns with institutional goals, addresses key issues, and is complete. Feedback is collected for revision. 5. Revise Plan: The Strategic Planning Lead incorporates the feedback from the review into the draft plan. Revisions may include clarifying goals, adjusting priorities, or adding missing elements. The revised draft is prepared for approval. 6. Approve Strategic Plan: The revised strategic plan is submitted to the executive sponsor for final approval. The sponsor evaluates the plan against institutional strategy and resource constraints. A decision is made to approve or reject the plan. 7. Publish Final Plan: Once approved, the final strategic plan is published on the institution's intranet and communicated via email to all staff. The plan is archived for future reference and performance tracking.
