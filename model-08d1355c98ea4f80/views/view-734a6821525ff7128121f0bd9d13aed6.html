## Purpose This process ensures that the institution's use of copyrighted materials and licensed content is fully compliant with applicable laws and agreements. It provides a structured workflow for reviewing usage requests, identifying potential infringements, and taking corrective action, thereby safeguarding the institution against legal penalties and reputational damage while supporting educational access. ## Roles - Copyright Officer: Reviews usage requests and assesses compliance - Content Requester: Submits requests for content usage - Legal Advisor: Provides guidance on complex copyright issues - System: Automates license checks and record-keeping ## Upstream Inputs - Copyright Usage Request: Submitted by Content Requester - License Database: Contains current licensing agreements - Copyright Materials Registry: Inventory of owned or licensed content ## Downstream Outputs - Approved Usage Record: Log of compliant content usage - Compliance Report: Summary of copyright activities and decisions - Remediation Action: Corrective steps taken for non-compliant requests ## Key Decisions - Does the requested usage fall within existing license terms? - Is the content covered by a fair use or educational exception? - Does the usage require escalation to Legal Advisor? ## Exception Paths - If usage is not covered by any license, the request is rejected and the requester is notified - If usage is partially compliant, remediation steps are initiated to adjust the request - If legal ambiguity exists, the request is escalated to Legal Advisor for review ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Usage Request: The Copyright Officer receives a formal copyright usage request submitted by a Content Requester. The request includes details about the content to be used, the intended purpose, and the user group. The officer logs the request into the tracking system, assigning a unique identifier for future reference. 2. Check License Coverage: The Copyright Management System queries the License Database using the content identifier from the request. It checks for active licenses that cover the specific work, format, and usage type. The system returns a coverage status indicating whether the content is fully covered, partially covered, or not covered. 3. Review Request Compliance: The Copyright Officer examines the license coverage status and the details of the usage request. They compare the intended use against the terms of any applicable licenses, as well as institutional copyright policies and fair use guidelines. The officer determines if the request is compliant, partially compliant, or non-compliant. 4. Assess Legal Risk: For requests that are partially compliant or borderline, the Copyright Officer performs a detailed legal risk assessment. They consider factors such as the amount of content used, the purpose (educational vs. commercial), and any precedents. The officer assigns a risk level: low, medium, or high. 5. Approve or Reject Request: The Copyright Officer makes a final decision on the usage request. If the request is fully compliant and low risk, it is approved. If it is non-compliant or high risk, it is rejected with a clear explanation. The decision is recorded in the system along with any conditions for approval. 6. Log Usage Record: For approved requests, the Copyright Management System automatically creates a usage record in the Copyright Materials Registry. The record includes the content identifier, requester details, usage dates, and any conditions. This log serves as an audit trail for compliance monitoring. 7. Notify Requester of Outcome: The Copyright Management System generates and sends an automated notification to the Content Requester. The notification includes the decision (approved or rejected), any conditions for use, and instructions for next steps. For rejected requests, it provides guidance on alternative options or how to resubmit. 8. Initiate Remediation: When a request is partially compliant or assessed as high risk, the Copyright Officer initiates a remediation workflow. This may involve contacting the requester to modify the usage scope, seeking additional permissions, or consulting with the Legal Advisor. The remediation steps are tracked in the system until resolved.
