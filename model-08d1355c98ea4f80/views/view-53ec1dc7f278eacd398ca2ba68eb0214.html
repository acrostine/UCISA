Commercial Contract Performance Mgt ()
## Purpose This process ensures that commercial contracts are actively monitored and evaluated against agreed terms, timelines, and deliverables. By conducting regular performance reviews and compliance checks, the organization can identify deviations early, address issues through corrective actions, and continuously improve contract value realization. The process relies on contract documentation, performance metrics, and stakeholder input to drive accountability and strengthen partner relationships. ## Roles - Contract Manager: Leads performance reviews, compliance checks, and stakeholder engagement - Commercial Analyst: Prepares performance metrics and compliance reports - Stakeholder (Internal): Provides input on contract performance and deliverables - System: Automates data collection, metric calculation, and report generation ## Upstream Inputs - Contract Documentation - Performance Metrics Data - Stakeholder Input - Deliverable Status Reports ## Downstream Outputs - Performance Review Report - Compliance Assessment - Corrective Action Plan - Performance Rating ## Key Decisions - Whether performance meets contractual thresholds - Whether compliance issues require formal escalation - Which corrective actions are appropriate for identified gaps - Final performance rating assignment ## Exception Paths - If compliance issues are found, a dedicated remediation step is triggered before re-review - If performance gaps are identified, corrective actions are planned and re-assessed - If stakeholder input reveals unresolved concerns, additional review cycle is initiated ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Contract Data: The Contract Manager initiates the process by retrieving the master contract document, amendments, and any relevant schedules. The Commercial Analyst extracts performance metrics from the contract management system, including delivery timelines, quality indicators, and financial data. All data is compiled into a working file for review. 2. Review Deliverables & Timelines: The Contract Manager reviews the compiled data against the contract's deliverable schedule. Each deliverable is checked for timeliness, completeness, and adherence to agreed specifications. Any discrepancies or delays are noted for further evaluation. 3. Assess Compliance: The Commercial Analyst performs a systematic compliance check against all contractual clauses, including reporting obligations, service levels, and regulatory requirements. A compliance checklist is completed, and any deviations are flagged. The output is a formal compliance assessment document. 4. Gather Stakeholder Input: The Contract Manager reaches out to key internal stakeholders who interact with the contract, such as operations, finance, and end-users. Feedback is collected via structured surveys or meetings, focusing on satisfaction, issues encountered, and improvement suggestions. 5. Evaluate Performance: The Contract Manager and Commercial Analyst jointly review the deliverable review notes, compliance assessment, and stakeholder feedback. Performance is measured against key performance indicators (KPIs) defined in the contract. A preliminary performance score is calculated. 6. Identify Gaps & Issues: Based on the performance evaluation, the team identifies specific gaps such as missed deadlines, quality issues, or non-compliance. Each gap is documented with evidence and categorized by severity. This step produces a clear list of issues requiring action. 7. Initiate Corrective Action: For each identified gap, the Contract Manager defines a corrective action including responsible parties, timelines, and expected outcomes. The plan is documented in the contract management system and communicated to relevant stakeholders. This step ensures accountability for resolution. 8. Approve Performance Rating: The Contract Manager consolidates all findings and proposes a final performance rating (e.g., Excellent, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement). The rating is reviewed against the contract's performance framework and approved. This step formalizes the performance outcome. 9. Generate Performance Report: The Commercial Analyst compiles all data, evaluations, and the approved rating into a formal Performance Review Report. The report includes an executive summary, detailed findings, compliance status, corrective actions, and the final rating. It is distributed to stakeholders and archived.
