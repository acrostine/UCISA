## Purpose This process systematically engages the public to build and maintain community trust, social license, and support for institutional initiatives. Collect Community Feedback: Automated systems and manual collection methods capture feedback from multiple public channels. Surveys are distributed online, social media mentions are monitored, and public forum notes are recorded. All data is aggregated into a central repository for analysis. 2. Identify Outreach Opportunities: The Community Engagement Officer reviews upcoming public events, partnership invitations, and speaking requests. Opportunities are filtered by alignment with institutional initiatives and strategic priorities. A shortlist of viable opportunities is prepared. 3. Assess Feedback and Opportunities: The Community Engagement Officer reviews aggregated feedback and shortlisted opportunities. Each item is assessed for urgency, alignment with institutional goals, and potential to build trust. Items are classified as high priority, standard, or low priority. 4. Plan Engagement Activities: Using the assessed inputs, the Communications Specialist drafts communication materials (press releases, newsletters, social media posts) and the Community Engagement Officer plans event logistics (venue, agenda, speakers). A draft engagement plan is produced. 5. Review Engagement Plan: The Public Relations Manager examines the draft engagement plan for consistency with institutional messaging, risk factors, and resource allocation. The plan is either approved for execution or returned with revision requests. 6. Execute Communication Campaign: The Communications Specialist publishes press releases, sends newsletters, and posts social media content according to the approved plan. Distribution is tracked for reach and engagement metrics. 7. Conduct Public Event: The Community Engagement Officer facilitates the public event, managing logistics, welcoming attendees, and collecting on-site feedback. Attendance numbers and real-time sentiment are recorded. 8. Generate Engagement Report: The system aggregates data from the communication campaign and public event, including reach, attendance, feedback themes, and sentiment scores. A comprehensive engagement report is generated for stakeholders.
