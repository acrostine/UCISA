## Purpose This process establishes a structured framework for creating, reviewing, and maintaining academic policies and regulations. It ensures that all policies are compliant with external accreditation bodies and internal governance structures, supporting consistent academic quality and integrity. The process enables transparent decision-making and proactive risk mitigation by formalizing stakeholder input and approval gates. ## Roles - Policy Analyst: Drafts and revises policy documents based on research and stakeholder input. - Academic Committee: Reviews and approves policy drafts, ensuring alignment with governance standards. - Legal & Compliance Officer: Validates policy compliance with external regulations and accreditation requirements. - System: Manages document storage, version control, and notification workflows. ## Upstream Inputs - Regulatory Framework Updates: Changes from external accreditation bodies or government regulations. - Committee Recommendations: Policy change requests or new policy proposals from academic committees. - Stakeholder Consultation Feedback: Input from faculty, staff, and students on policy needs or issues. ## Downstream Outputs - Formalized Policy Document: The approved, published policy text. - Regulatory Guidelines: Supporting implementation guidelines for the policy. - Communication Notification: Announcement sent to all relevant stakeholders. ## Key Decisions - Approval Decision: Does the draft policy meet all governance and compliance requirements? - Compliance Validation: Is the policy fully compliant with external accreditation standards? - Review Cycle Determination: When should the policy be next reviewed for relevance? ## Exception Paths - Policy Draft Rejected: If the Academic Committee rejects the draft, it is sent back for revision with specific feedback. - Compliance Issues Found: If the Legal & Compliance Officer identifies non-compliance, the draft is returned for rework with documented issues. - Stakeholder Objections: If significant objections arise during consultation, the draft may be returned for further revision before committee review. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Identify Policy Need: The Policy Analyst reviews incoming regulatory framework updates, committee recommendations, and stakeholder consultation feedback. This step identifies gaps or required changes in existing policies or the need for a new policy. The output is a clear scope statement for the policy development effort. 2. Draft Policy Document: Based on the defined scope, the Policy Analyst writes a comprehensive draft policy document. This includes the policy's purpose, applicability, definitions, procedures, and responsibilities. The draft is stored in the policy management system for review. 3. Review Draft by Experts: The draft is circulated to relevant subject matter experts (e.g., faculty leads, department heads) who provide feedback on technical content, feasibility, and alignment with academic standards. Comments are collected and consolidated. 4. Revise Draft: The Policy Analyst reviews all expert feedback and revises the draft accordingly. This step ensures the policy is technically sound and addresses any concerns raised during review. The revised draft is then prepared for formal submission. 5. Submit to Academic Committee: The Policy Analyst submits the revised policy draft along with a summary of changes and expert feedback to the Academic Committee. The submission includes a request for agenda placement at the next committee meeting. 6. Approve Policy: The Academic Committee reviews the submission package, discusses the policy's alignment with governance and strategic objectives, and votes on approval. If approved, the policy moves to compliance validation. If rejected, it is returned with specific reasons. 7. Validate Compliance: The Legal & Compliance Officer reviews the approved policy against all relevant external accreditation standards and internal regulations. If compliant, the policy is cleared for publication. If issues are found, the policy is returned with a compliance report. 8. Publish and Communicate: The System publishes the final policy document to the official policy repository and sends a communication notification to all relevant stakeholders (faculty, staff, students). The policy is now active and enforceable. 9. Schedule Review Cycle: The Policy Analyst schedules a periodic review date in the policy management system, typically 1-3 years out, to ensure the policy remains current and compliant. This step closes the current process cycle. 10. Address Rejection Feedback: The Policy Analyst reviews the rejection feedback from the Academic Committee and revises the policy draft to address all concerns. The revised draft is then resubmitted for committee review. 11. Resolve Compliance Issues: The Policy Analyst works with the Legal & Compliance Officer to resolve all compliance issues identified in the report. The draft is revised and resubmitted for compliance validation.
