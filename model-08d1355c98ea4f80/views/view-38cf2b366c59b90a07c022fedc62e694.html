## Purpose This process manages the end-to-end lifecycle of course offers, from initial allocation based on applicant merit and institutional capacity, through applicant acceptance decisions, to final confirmation and quota monitoring. It ensures that student intake aligns with strategic enrolment targets and regulatory requirements while optimising resource utilisation. ## Roles - Admissions Officer: Generates and issues offers, monitors acceptance responses, and updates quota records. - Registrar: Reviews and approves quota adjustments and exception cases. - System: Automates offer generation, sends notifications, and updates quota utilisation dashboards. - Applicant: Receives offer and submits acceptance or rejection decision. ## Upstream Inputs - Applicant Data: Ranked applicant list with scores and preferences. - Institutional Capacity Information: Course quotas, faculty availability, and facility limits. - Strategic Enrolment Targets: Planned intake numbers per course and programme. ## Downstream Outputs - Confirmed Offer Lists: Final list of accepted applicants per course. - Quota Utilisation Reports: Updated reports showing filled and remaining places per course. - Offer Notifications: Formal offer letters sent to applicants. - Acceptance Records: Documented acceptance or rejection decisions from applicants. ## Key Decisions - Offer Allocation: Which applicants receive offers based on merit and quota availability. - Acceptance Validation: Whether an acceptance is valid and within the offer expiry period. - Quota Adjustment: Whether to adjust quotas when over- or under-subscription occurs. - Exception Handling: Whether to approve late acceptances or quota overrides. ## Exception Paths - Offer Expired: Applicant accepts after offer expiry date — requires manual review and possible quota override approval. - Quota Full: All places filled before all acceptances processed — remaining acceptances waitlisted or rejected. - Invalid Acceptance: Applicant accepts with incorrect details — returned for correction and re-submission. - System Failure: Automated offer generation fails — manual fallback process initiated. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Applicant Data: The Admissions Officer retrieves the ranked applicant list from the admissions system along with current course quotas, faculty availability, and facility limits. This data forms the basis for offer allocation decisions. 2. Determine Offer Allocation: The Admissions Officer reviews each applicant's score and preferences against available course quotas. They allocate offers to the highest-ranked applicants while ensuring no course exceeds its capacity. This step involves a key decision point for borderline cases. 3. Generate Offer Notifications: The system processes the allocation decisions and generates formal offer letters for each selected applicant. Offers include course details, acceptance deadline, and instructions. Notifications are sent via email and the applicant portal. 4. Receive Acceptance Decision: Applicants respond through the online portal by accepting or rejecting the offer. The system records the decision and timestamp. If no response is received by the deadline, the system marks the offer as expired. 5. Validate Acceptance: The Admissions Officer reviews each acceptance to ensure it was submitted before the offer expiry date and that applicant details match records. Valid acceptances proceed to confirmation; invalid ones are flagged for correction. 6. Confirm Enrolment: For each validated acceptance, the system updates the course roster with the applicant's details and confirms their enrolment status. A confirmation notification is sent to the applicant. 7. Update Quota Utilisation: The system automatically updates the quota utilisation dashboard, decrementing available places for each course as enrolments are confirmed. Real-time data is available for monitoring. 8. Review Quota Balance: The Admissions Officer examines the updated quota dashboard to identify courses that are over-subscribed or under-subscribed. This review informs whether quota adjustments are needed. 9. Adjust Quota Allocation: If quota imbalances are identified, the Registrar reviews the situation and approves adjustments such as increasing a course quota or reallocating places from under-subscribed courses. Adjustments are recorded in the system. 10. Produce Final Reports: The system compiles final confirmed offer lists per course and generates quota utilisation reports showing filled and remaining places. These reports are distributed to planning and compliance teams.
