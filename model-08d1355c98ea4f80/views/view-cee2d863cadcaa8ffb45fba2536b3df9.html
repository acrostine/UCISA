## Purpose This process ensures the university maintains a safe and compliant environment by systematically managing risk assessments, incident reports, safety training, and compliance monitoring. It transforms raw incident data and regulatory requirements into actionable safety protocols and mitigation measures, minimising risks and promoting wellbeing across campus. ## Roles - Safety Officer: Reviews incidents, conducts risk assessments, and proposes mitigation measures. - Health & Safety Manager: Approves mitigation plans and oversees compliance. - Training Coordinator: Schedules and delivers safety training sessions. - System: Automates notifications, record-keeping, and compliance checks. ## Upstream Inputs - Incident Report - Risk Assessment Trigger (scheduled or event-based) - Regulatory Requirement Update - Staff/Student Feedback ## Downstream Outputs - Approved Mitigation Plan - Safety Protocol Update - Training Completion Record - Compliance Report ## Key Decisions - Does the incident or trigger require a formal risk assessment? - Are the proposed mitigation measures adequate and cost-effective? - Has the training been completed by all required personnel? - Are compliance metrics within acceptable thresholds? ## Exception Paths - If a risk assessment identifies critical hazards, the plan is escalated for immediate approval. - If training completion rates are low, a follow-up notification and re-scheduling loop is triggered. - If compliance monitoring reveals a breach, an incident report is re-opened for investigation. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Incident Report: The process starts when an incident report is filed by a staff member, student, or automated system, or when a scheduled risk assessment trigger activates. The Safety Officer logs the report and reviews initial details to determine urgency. 2. Assess Risk: Using a standard risk matrix, the Safety Officer assesses the reported hazard. The assessment determines whether a formal risk assessment is required and prioritises the response based on risk level. 3. Develop Mitigation Plan: If risks are confirmed, the Safety Officer develops a detailed mitigation plan. The plan includes specific actions, responsible parties, deadlines, and resource requirements to reduce the identified risks. 4. Approve Mitigation Plan: The Health & Safety Manager reviews the draft mitigation plan for adequacy, feasibility, and regulatory compliance. The plan is either approved for implementation or returned to the Safety Officer with revision comments. 5. Schedule Safety Training: Based on the approved mitigation plan and any regulatory training requirements, the Training Coordinator schedules appropriate safety training sessions. Dates, venues, and participant lists are created in the training system. 6. Deliver Training: The scheduled training sessions are delivered to the identified participants. Attendance is recorded in the system, and completion certificates are generated for attendees. 7. Monitor Compliance: The system automatically monitors training completion rates, incident trends, and other compliance metrics against predefined thresholds. A compliance report is generated for review by the Health & Safety Manager. 8. Collect Feedback: Feedback on safety protocols, training effectiveness, and overall safety culture is collected via surveys and direct submissions. The feedback is analysed to identify improvement opportunities and update safety protocols.
