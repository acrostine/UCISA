## Purpose Income Management ensures that all institutional revenues from tuition fees, grants, and donations are received, validated, recorded, and reconciled in a timely and accurate manner. This process supports cash flow stability, financial reporting accuracy, and regulatory compliance. ## Roles - Finance Officer: Initiates payment receipt, validates payments, and reconciles accounts. - Finance Manager: Reviews and approves reconciliations and income statements. - System: Automates payment matching, ledger posting, and statement generation. ## Upstream Inputs - Payment Data: Incoming payment records from bank feeds, payment gateways, and donor systems. - Contractual Agreements: Tuition fee schedules, grant award letters, and donation pledges. ## Downstream Outputs - Income Statements: Summarised revenue reports for financial planning and reporting. - Reconciled Accounts: General ledger accounts with matched and cleared transactions. - Audit Trail: Archived records of all income transactions and reconciliation actions. ## Key Decisions - Does the payment match the contractual obligation? (Validation decision) - Are discrepancies within tolerance for automatic resolution? (Exception handling decision) - Is the reconciliation complete and accurate for approval? (Approval gate) ## Exception Paths - Payment mismatch: Payment does not match any obligation — flagged for investigation. - Unresolved discrepancy: Investigation fails to resolve within tolerance — escalated to Finance Manager. - Reconciliation rejection: Finance Manager rejects reconciliation due to errors — returned for rework. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Payment Data: The Finance Officer retrieves payment data from bank feeds, online payment gateways, and donor management systems. This data includes amounts, dates, references, and payer information. All records are logged into the income processing queue for validation. 2. Validate Payment Against Obligation: The system automatically matches each payment against tuition fee schedules, grant award letters, or donation pledges. It checks amount, reference number, and payer identity. Payments that match exactly proceed; mismatches are flagged for investigation. 3. Investigate Payment Mismatch: The Finance Officer examines each flagged mismatch, contacting the payer or reviewing supporting documentation. If the discrepancy is minor and can be corrected, the payment is adjusted. If unresolved, the case is escalated to the Finance Manager. 4. Record Income in Ledger: The system automatically posts all validated and resolved payments to the appropriate general ledger accounts. Revenue codes are assigned based on the income type — tuition, grant, or donation. A journal entry is created for each transaction. 5. Reconcile Accounts: The Finance Officer runs a reconciliation process comparing posted ledger entries with bank statements and obligation records. Any unmatched or incorrectly posted items are identified. A reconciliation report is prepared for review. 6. Review Reconciliation: The Finance Manager examines the reconciliation report for completeness, accuracy, and proper documentation. If all items are matched and discrepancies resolved, the report is approved. If errors are found, it is rejected with comments for rework. 7. Rework Reconciliation: The Finance Officer addresses the issues noted in the rejected reconciliation report. This may involve correcting ledger entries, re-matching transactions, or obtaining additional documentation. The updated report is resubmitted for review. 8. Generate Income Statement: The system aggregates all reconciled ledger transactions for the reporting period into a formal income statement. The statement shows revenue by category — tuition, grants, donations — and provides totals for financial planning. 9. Archive Records: The system archives all transaction records, reconciliation reports, and income statements in a secure, immutable audit repository. Records are indexed by date and income type for future retrieval.
