Collect Requirements: The Recreation Manager collects participant registration data, community feedback surveys, and the funding allocation report. This step establishes the baseline demand and resource envelope for the upcoming program cycle. All inputs are logged into the recreation management system. 2. Assess Feasibility: The Recreation Manager reviews the requirements summary against facility availability, budget limits, and staffing capacity. This step determines which programs are viable and identifies potential conflicts. A feasibility report is produced for each proposed program. 3. Design Program Plan: Based on the feasibility report, the Recreation Manager designs a comprehensive program plan including session timings, required equipment, staffing assignments, and a preliminary risk assessment. The plan is documented and prepared for review. 4. Review Safety Compliance: The Safety Officer examines the program plan for potential safety hazards, emergency procedures, and compliance with relevant regulations. A safety assessment is completed, identifying any required mitigations. This is a critical evaluation step. 5. Approve Budget: The Finance Officer reviews the program plan's cost estimates against the available funding allocation. The budget is either approved, allowing the program to proceed, or rejected if costs exceed limits or justification is insufficient. 6. Schedule Facilities: The Facility Coordinator reserves the required athletic facilities, fields, courts, and equipment based on the approved program plan. Conflicts are resolved by adjusting timings or swapping venues. A confirmed schedule is published. 7. Execute Program: The Recreation Manager and support staff deliver the program according to the schedule. Real-time monitoring ensures participant safety, equipment availability, and adherence to the plan. Minor adjustments are made as needed during execution. 8. Collect Feedback: Post-event, the Recreation Manager distributes satisfaction surveys to participants and collects any incident reports from staff. This data is compiled for analysis and reporting. 9. Generate Report: The Recreation Manager consolidates attendance records, actual budget spend, safety incidents, and satisfaction scores into a final program report. This report is archived and shared with stakeholders for continuous improvement.
