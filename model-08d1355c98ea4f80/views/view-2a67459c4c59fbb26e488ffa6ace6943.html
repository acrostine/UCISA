Internal Stakeholder Relationship Mgt ()
## Purpose This process ensures that internal departments, faculties, and administrative units communicate effectively and collaborate on shared goals. It transforms internal feedback and institutional objectives into actionable coordinated plans and resource-sharing frameworks, promoting institutional cohesion and goal alignment. ## Roles - Internal Stakeholder Lead: Initiates and facilitates the relationship management process - Department Representative: Provides departmental feedback and objectives - Planning Committee: Reviews and approves coordinated action plans - Resource Manager: Develops shared resource frameworks - System: Automates notifications and document routing ## Upstream Inputs - Internal Feedback: Input from departments, faculties, and administrative units - Institutional Objectives: Strategic goals and priorities from leadership - Existing Collaboration Records: Historical data on past cross-unit initiatives ## Downstream Outputs - Coordinated Action Plan: Approved plan outlining joint activities and timelines - Shared Resource Framework: Document detailing resource allocation and sharing agreements - Stakeholder Engagement Report: Summary of collaboration outcomes and feedback ## Key Decisions - Whether departmental feedback aligns with institutional objectives - Whether the coordinated action plan meets stakeholder requirements - Whether the shared resource framework is equitable and feasible ## Exception Paths - If feedback alignment assessment identifies significant gaps, the process loops back to gather additional input from stakeholders - If the action plan is rejected, the planning committee must revise and resubmit - If resource framework conflicts arise, a mediation step is triggered before re-approval ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Internal Feedback: The Internal Stakeholder Lead initiates a feedback collection process across all departments, faculties, and administrative units. This involves surveys, meetings, and direct submissions to capture current collaboration challenges, resource needs, and improvement suggestions. The collected feedback forms the foundation for alignment analysis. 2. Review Institutional Objectives: The Internal Stakeholder Lead reviews the latest institutional objectives provided by senior leadership. This step ensures that all subsequent planning aligns with the institution's strategic direction. Key priorities, performance targets, and cross-cutting initiatives are documented for reference. 3. Assess Alignment Gaps: The Internal Stakeholder Lead systematically compares the collected internal feedback with the institutional objectives. Gaps, overlaps, and areas of potential conflict are identified. This assessment determines whether additional stakeholder engagement is needed before proceeding to planning. 4. Address Feedback Gaps: If significant gaps are found, the Internal Stakeholder Lead conducts targeted follow-up sessions with affected departments to clarify needs and adjust expectations. This remediation step ensures that feedback is refined before proceeding to planning. 5. Develop Coordinated Action Plan: Using the aligned feedback and objectives, the Internal Stakeholder Lead drafts a coordinated action plan. This plan specifies joint activities, responsible parties, timelines, and success metrics. The draft is prepared for review by the Planning Committee. 6. Review Action Plan: The Planning Committee reviews the draft coordinated action plan, assessing its alignment with institutional objectives, resource availability, and stakeholder commitments. Feedback and revision requests are documented for the plan author. 7. Approve Action Plan: The Planning Committee makes a final decision on the coordinated action plan. If approved, the plan moves to resource framework development. If rejected, it returns for revision with documented reasons. 8. Revise Action Plan: The Internal Stakeholder Lead revises the coordinated action plan to address the Planning Committee's feedback. Changes are documented and the updated plan is resubmitted for re-review. 9. Develop Shared Resource Framework: The Resource Manager develops a shared resource framework that details how budgets, personnel, equipment, and facilities will be allocated and shared among participating units. The framework ensures equitable access and efficient utilization. 10. Review Resource Framework: Department representatives and the Resource Manager review the draft shared resource framework. They assess whether allocations are fair, feasible, and aligned with the action plan. Feedback and proposed adjustments are collected. 11. Approve Resource Framework: The Planning Committee provides final approval on the shared resource framework. If approved, it becomes operational. If rejected, the framework is returned for revision with specific guidance. 12. Distribute Final Outputs: The Internal Stakeholder Lead distributes the approved coordinated action plan and shared resource framework to all internal stakeholders. Notifications are sent, and documents are published on the stakeholder portal for ongoing reference.
