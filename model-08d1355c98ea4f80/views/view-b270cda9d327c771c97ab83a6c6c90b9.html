It analyzes operational risks, market conditions, and contractual obligations to ensure the institution can maintain revenue and reputation during unforeseen events. ## Roles - Commercial Risk Manager: Leads risk identification and strategy development - Business Continuity Officer: Provides continuity framework inputs and validates alignment - Commercial Director: Approves final contingency plans - System: Automates risk data aggregation and plan documentation ## Upstream Inputs - Risk Assessment Report - Business Continuity Framework - Market Conditions Data - Contractual Obligations Register ## Downstream Outputs - Contingency Plan Document - Approved Response Strategies - Risk Mitigation Action Items ## Key Decisions - Which risks require contingency planning? - What is the appropriate response strategy for each risk? - Does the plan align with business continuity standards? - Should the plan be approved for implementation? ## Exception Paths - If risk assessment data is incomplete, the process loops back to data gathering - If the plan fails alignment review, it is reworked and resubmitted - If the Commercial Director rejects the plan, it is revised and re-presented ## Step Walkthrough 1. Identify Commercial Risks: The Commercial Risk Manager reviews the Risk Assessment Report and Market Conditions Data to identify all potential disruptions to commercial activities. This includes operational, financial, and contractual risks. The output is a comprehensive risk register for further evaluation. 2. Assess Risk Impact: The Commercial Risk Manager analyzes each risk in the register, scoring its probability and potential impact on revenue, operations, and reputation. This step prioritizes risks requiring contingency planning. High-priority risks are flagged for strategy development. 3. Develop Response Strategies: For each high-priority risk, the Commercial Risk Manager designs specific response strategies, including preventive measures, contingency actions, and escalation procedures. The draft Contingency Plan Document is created, incorporating contractual obligations and resource requirements. 4. Validate Plan Alignment: The Business Continuity Officer reviews the draft Contingency Plan to ensure it aligns with the Business Continuity Framework. They verify that response strategies meet organizational standards and that recovery timelines are realistic. If gaps are found, the plan is sent back for revision. 5. Approve Contingency Plan: The Commercial Director reviews the validated plan, considering strategic alignment, resource commitments, and overall risk posture. They either approve the plan for implementation or reject it with feedback for revision. 6. Document and Distribute Plan: The System automatically generates the final Contingency Plan Document, archives it in the document repository, and distributes it to all relevant stakeholders via email and the intranet. This ensures all parties are prepared to execute the plan if needed. 7. Rework Response Strategies: The Commercial Risk Manager revises the draft Contingency Plan to address gaps identified during the alignment validation. This may involve adjusting response strategies, timelines, or resource allocations to meet business continuity standards. 8. Revise and Resubmit Plan: The Commercial Risk Manager incorporates the Commercial Director's feedback to revise the validated plan. Changes may include strategic adjustments, cost re-evaluations, or scope modifications. The revised plan is then resubmitted for approval.
