Staff Engagement ()
## Purpose This process ensures that employee feedback is systematically gathered, analysed, and acted upon to foster a positive and productive work environment. It transforms raw engagement data into strategic insights and concrete action plans that drive institutional improvement. ## Roles - HR Business Partner: Oversees the engagement cycle and approves action plans - Engagement Analyst: Collects and analyses survey data and qualitative feedback - Department Manager: Reviews and implements action plans within their teams - System: Automates survey distribution, data aggregation, and report generation ## Upstream Inputs - Engagement Metrics: Quantitative scores from previous surveys and pulse checks - Qualitative Data: Open-ended comments, focus group transcripts, and communication sentiment - Organisational Culture Assessments: Culture audit results and climate survey data ## Downstream Outputs - Engagement Survey Results: Aggregated scores and response rates - Feedback Reports: Detailed analysis with trend comparisons and demographic breakdowns - Action Plans: Prioritised initiatives with owners, timelines, and success metrics ## Key Decisions - Determine which engagement dimensions require immediate intervention - Decide whether survey response rates are statistically significant for analysis - Approve or reject proposed action plans based on resource availability and impact ## Exception Paths - Low response rate: If survey participation falls below 30%, the process triggers a re-survey with enhanced communication and incentives - Critical disengagement flag: If scores drop below a threshold, an escalation path initiates an emergency leadership review - Action plan rejection: If a plan is rejected, it is returned for revision with specific feedback from the approver ## Step Walkthrough 1. Distribute Engagement Survey: The HR system automatically distributes the standardised engagement survey via email and the internal employee portal. The survey includes both Likert-scale questions and open-ended comment fields. Automated reminders are scheduled at 3, 7, and 10 days after initial distribution to maximise response rates. 2. Collect Survey Responses: The system captures all submitted responses, storing them in a secure database. Incomplete submissions are flagged for follow-up. The system tracks response rates by department, tenure, and location to ensure representative sampling. 3. Analyse Engagement Data: The Engagement Analyst examines the aggregated data, calculating mean scores for each engagement dimension. They perform sentiment analysis on open-ended comments and identify key themes. Outliers and significant changes from previous cycles are highlighted for management attention. 4. Assess Response Validity: The Engagement Analyst evaluates whether the overall response rate exceeds the 30% minimum threshold. If the rate is below this level, the data may not be statistically representative. The analyst also checks for demographic representation to avoid skewed results. 5. Generate Feedback Report: The system generates a detailed feedback report including executive summaries, score breakdowns by department and demographic, trend comparisons with previous cycles, and word clouds from qualitative comments. The report is formatted for both digital viewing and print. 6. Review Report with Managers: The HR Business Partner schedules and conducts review meetings with each Department Manager. They walk through the report, highlight areas of strength and concern, and discuss potential root causes. Managers are encouraged to ask questions and provide contextual insights. 7. Draft Action Plan: Using the reviewed insights, the Department Manager drafts a concrete action plan. The plan includes specific initiatives (e.g., town halls, recognition programs), assigns owners, sets timelines, and defines success metrics. The draft is prepared in a standard template. 8. Approve Action Plan: The HR Business Partner evaluates the draft action plan for feasibility, alignment with organisational goals, and resource availability. If the plan is sound, it is approved. If not, it is rejected with specific feedback for revision. 9. Communicate Action Plan: The Department Manager communicates the approved action plan to their team via a team meeting and written summary. They explain the rationale, expected outcomes, and how team members can contribute. A Q&A session is held to address concerns. 10. Monitor Implementation: The Engagement Analyst monitors the implementation of action plan initiatives, tracking completion of milestones and collecting interim feedback. Progress is documented and reported to the HR Business Partner for the next engagement cycle review. 11. Resolve Low Response Rate: When the response rate is below 30%, the Engagement Analyst triggers a re-survey process. This includes sending personalised invitations, extending the deadline, and offering small incentives. The goal is to achieve a statistically valid sample. 12. Revise Action Plan: The Department Manager reviews the rejection feedback from the HR Business Partner and revises the action plan accordingly. Changes may include adjusting timelines, reallocating resources, or modifying initiatives. The revised plan is resubmitted for approval.
