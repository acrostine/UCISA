## Purpose This process ensures that all personnel records—including qualifications, contact details, employment history, and training records—are accurately captured, validated, and maintained. It consumes data from recruitment, training, and general HR updates to create a single source of truth for staff information, enabling effective decision-making and regulatory compliance. ## Roles - HR Administrator: Initiates record creation and updates, performs data entry - HR Manager: Reviews and approves significant record changes - System: Automates data validation, duplicate checks, and archival - Employee: Provides personal data updates and verifies records ## Upstream Inputs - Recruitment Onboarding Data: New hire information from recruitment process - Training Completion Records: Course completions and certifications - HR Update Requests: Change of address, contact, or employment status - Employee Self-Service Submissions: Personal data changes initiated by staff ## Downstream Outputs - Updated Staff Profile: Consolidated personnel record in HRIS - Compliance Report: Audit trail of record changes - HR Analytics Feed: Aggregated staff data for workforce planning - Notification to Employee: Confirmation of record update ## Key Decisions - Is the incoming data complete and valid? (Data Validation) - Does the change require managerial approval? (Change Classification) - Are there duplicate records that need merging? (Duplicate Check) - Is the record accurate after periodic audit? (Record Audit) ## Exception Paths - Incomplete or invalid data: Sent back to source for correction - Unauthorized change request: Rejected and logged for security review - Duplicate record found: Merged with existing record, original archived - Audit discrepancy: Flagged for HR Manager investigation and correction ## Step Walkthrough 1. Incomplete or malformed records are flagged for correction. 3. Classify Change Type: The system evaluates the type of change—routine updates (e.g., address change) vs. significant changes (e.g., job title, salary). Approve Significant Change: The HR Manager reviews the proposed change, checks supporting documentation, and either approves or rejects the update. This step ensures proper oversight for sensitive data modifications. 5. Check for Duplicates: The system compares the incoming data against existing staff profiles using matching algorithms (name, date of birth, national ID). If a potential duplicate is found, it flags the records for merging. 6. Merge Duplicate Records: The system automatically merges the duplicate records, preserving the most complete data from each source. The original duplicate record is archived for audit purposes. 7. Update Staff Profile: The system applies the validated and approved changes to the employee's master record in the HRIS. This step commits the data and updates all dependent fields. 8. Notify Employee: The system generates and sends an automated notification to the employee confirming the record update. This provides transparency and allows the employee to verify the change. 9. Audit Record Accuracy: The HR Administrator selects a sample of staff profiles and compares them against source documents and employee verifications. Discrepancies are logged for resolution. 10. Resolve Audit Findings: The HR Administrator investigates each discrepancy, gathers correct information, and updates the staff profile accordingly. Corrected records are re-audited if necessary.
