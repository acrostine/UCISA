## Purpose This process manages the end-to-end delivery of medical and wellness services to the campus community. It ensures that patients receive appropriate, timely care while adhering to clinical standards and regulatory requirements, ultimately supporting student and staff wellbeing. ## Roles - Patient (Student/Staff) - Triage Nurse - Clinician (Doctor/Nurse Practitioner) - Health Records Administrator - System (EHR System) ## Upstream Inputs - Health Service Request - Patient Health History - Regulatory Guidelines - Clinical Resource Availability ## Downstream Outputs - Treatment Plan - Care Delivery Record - Follow-up Schedule - Health Outcome Report ## Key Decisions - Urgency level determination during triage - Diagnosis and treatment plan selection - Approval of treatment plan - Need for follow-up care ## Exception Paths - If triage identifies a life-threatening emergency, the patient is immediately referred to external emergency services. - If diagnosis is inconclusive, additional tests are ordered, looping back to clinical assessment. - If treatment plan is rejected, the clinician revises the plan and resubmits for approval. - If patient does not attend follow-up, a reminder is sent; after two missed appointments, case is escalated to patient outreach. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Receive Health Request**: Patient submits a health service request via the patient portal or walk-in; request is logged in the EHR system. 2. **Triage Patient**: Triage Nurse assesses urgency based on symptoms and history; determines if immediate care or scheduled appointment is needed. 3. **Assess Clinically**: Clinician reviews patient history, performs examination, and orders any necessary tests; produces a diagnosis. 4. **Develop Treatment Plan**: Clinician creates a treatment plan including medications, procedures, and follow-up schedule. 5. **Approve Treatment Plan**: Clinician reviews and approves the plan; if criteria not met, plan is sent back for revision. 6. **Deliver Care**: Care is administered per the approved plan; all actions are documented in the EHR. 7. **Schedule Follow-up**: Follow-up appointment is scheduled; patient receives automated reminders. 8. **Document Outcome**: Clinician records final outcome and patient feedback; report is generated for health records. Steps: 1. Receive Health Request: The patient initiates the process by submitting a health service request through the patient portal or by walking into the health center. The request includes basic symptoms and contact information. The EHR system logs the request and assigns a unique case ID. 2. Triage Patient: The Triage Nurse reviews the logged request, checks patient history, and evaluates symptom severity. Based on triage criteria, the nurse determines whether the patient needs immediate care, a scheduled appointment, or referral to external emergency services. 3. Assess Clinically: The clinician reviews the triage outcome, conducts a physical examination, and orders diagnostic tests if needed. The assessment results in a preliminary diagnosis or identifies the need for further investigation. 4. Develop Treatment Plan: Using the diagnosis, the clinician develops a comprehensive treatment plan that may include medications, procedures, lifestyle recommendations, and a follow-up schedule. The plan is documented in the EHR. 5. Approve Treatment Plan: The clinician performs a final review of the treatment plan against clinical guidelines and patient safety criteria. If the plan meets all standards, it is approved. If issues are found, the plan is returned for revision. 6. Deliver Care: The clinician administers the prescribed care, such as medication, minor procedures, or therapy. All actions are recorded in the EHR in real time, including dosage, duration, and patient response. 7. Schedule Follow-up: The system automatically schedules a follow-up appointment based on the treatment plan. The patient receives an automated reminder via email or SMS. If the patient misses the appointment, a second reminder is sent. 8. Document Outcome: The clinician records the final health outcome, any complications, and patient satisfaction feedback. The Health Records Administrator reviews the documentation for completeness and generates a health outcome report for reporting purposes.
