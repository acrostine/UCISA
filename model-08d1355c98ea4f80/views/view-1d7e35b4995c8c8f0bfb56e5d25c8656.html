## Purpose Financial Reporting ensures that all financial transactions and balances are accurately compiled into statutory and management reports. This process supports accountability, regulatory compliance, and informed governance by producing reliable financial statements, audit reports, and compliance disclosures. ## Roles - Finance Analyst: Extracts and validates financial data, prepares draft reports - Finance Manager: Reviews reports for accuracy and completeness - Chief Financial Officer (CFO): Approves final reports for distribution - System: Automates data extraction and report generation ## Upstream Inputs - General Ledger Balances - Transactional Data from sub-ledgers - Accounting Records and Adjustments - Previous Period Reports ## Downstream Outputs - Approved Financial Statements - Management Reports - Audit Reports - Regulatory Compliance Disclosures ## Key Decisions - Whether data variances exceed materiality thresholds requiring investigation - Whether report adjustments are necessary before finalisation - Whether the final report meets all regulatory and internal standards for approval ## Exception Paths - Data discrepancies found during validation trigger a rework loop back to data extraction - Material errors identified during review require report rework and re-submission - CFO rejection of final report sends it back for revision and re-approval ## Step Walkthrough 1. Distribute Reports: System distributes approved reports to stakeholders and regulatory bodies. Steps: 1. Extract Financial Data: The Finance Analyst runs predefined queries in the ERP system to extract general ledger balances, sub-ledger transactions, and period-end adjustments. All extracted data is saved to a staging area for validation. This step ensures the raw data foundation for all subsequent reporting. 2. Validate Data Completeness: The Finance Analyst runs validation checks including trial balance equality, inter-entity reconciliation, and period-end cutoff verification. Any discrepancies are flagged for investigation. This step ensures the data is reliable before report preparation begins. 3. Prepare Draft Reports: The Finance Analyst uses the reporting module to generate draft income statements, balance sheets, cash flow statements, and management dashboards. Standard templates and accounting policies are applied. The draft reports are saved for review. 4. Review Draft Reports: The Finance Manager examines the draft reports for material misstatements, compliance with IFRS/GAAP, and consistency with prior periods. Variance analysis is performed. The review outcome determines whether the reports proceed to approval or require revision. 5. Approve Final Reports: The CFO reviews the finalised reports and supporting documentation. Approval confirms the reports are accurate, complete, and compliant. Rejection triggers a return to the review step with specific revision instructions. 6. Distribute Reports: The System automatically distributes approved financial statements and management reports to predefined recipients including board members, regulators, and internal management. Distribution is logged for audit trail purposes. 7. Resolve Data Discrepancies: The Finance Analyst investigates flagged discrepancies by reviewing source documents, adjusting entries, and consulting with business units. Corrections are applied and the data is re-validated before proceeding. 8. Revise Draft Reports: The Finance Analyst incorporates corrections and adjustments identified during the review or approval stage. Revised reports are re-submitted for review.
