## Purpose This process ensures that only eligible students receive official graduation records and certificates, maintaining the integrity of the institution's academic credentials. It covers the end-to-end workflow from eligibility verification through certificate generation, quality assurance, and distribution. ## Roles - Records Officer: Reviews and validates eligibility data, initiates certificate generation. - Quality Assurance Officer: Conducts final checks on generated certificates and credentials. - System: Automates certificate generation, digital credential creation, and record updates. - Student: Receives the final graduation documents and digital credentials. ## Upstream Inputs - Confirmed Eligibility Data: List of students cleared for graduation. - Academic Transcripts: Final academic records for each eligible student. ## Downstream Outputs - Official Graduation Certificates: Physical or digital documents certifying graduation. - Digital Credentials: Verifiable electronic credentials for employers and professional bodies. - Distribution Log: Record of certificate distribution and digital credential issuance. ## Key Decisions - Eligibility Validation: Determine if student records match confirmed eligibility data. - Certificate Accuracy: Decide if generated certificates meet quality standards. - Distribution Method: Choose between physical mail, digital download, or secure portal. ## Exception Paths - Data Discrepancy Found: If eligibility data does not match transcripts, the record is flagged for correction and sent back for rework. - Quality Check Failure: If certificates contain errors, they are rejected and regenerated. - Distribution Failure: If digital credential issuance fails, manual intervention is triggered. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Eligibility Data: The Records Officer retrieves the confirmed eligibility list from the graduation system along with final academic transcripts for all students marked as eligible. This data serves as the foundation for all subsequent steps. 2. Validate Student Records: The Records Officer manually reviews each student's academic transcript against the confirmed eligibility data. Any discrepancies in course completion, grades, or credit requirements are identified and flagged for correction. 3. Correct Record Discrepancies: If discrepancies are found, the Records Officer works with academic departments to correct the records. This may involve updating transcripts, adjusting eligibility status, or obtaining missing data. Once corrected, the records are re-submitted for validation. 4. Generate Graduation Certificates: The Graduation Management System automatically generates official graduation certificates in PDF format for all students whose records have been validated. Each certificate includes the student's name, degree, graduation date, and institutional seals. 5. Create Digital Credentials: The System creates digital credentials using blockchain or similar technology, ensuring they are tamper-proof and verifiable by employers and professional bodies. Each credential is linked to the student's unique identifier. 6. Review Certificate Quality: The Quality Assurance Officer reviews a random sample of generated certificates and digital credentials to ensure accuracy, formatting, and compliance with institutional standards. Any errors found are flagged for regeneration. 7. Regenerate Faulty Documents: If quality issues are found, the System regenerates the faulty certificates or digital credentials based on corrected data. The regenerated documents are then re-submitted for quality review. 8. Distribute Certificates: The System sends physical certificates via mail or provides digital download links through the student portal, based on student preference. A distribution log is updated with delivery status. 9. Issue Digital Credentials: The Digital Credentialing Platform issues the verified digital credentials to each student's secure digital wallet or email. The issuance is recorded in the distribution log for audit purposes.
