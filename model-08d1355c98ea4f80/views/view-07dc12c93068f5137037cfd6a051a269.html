Identify Potential Partners: The Commercial Engagement Lead reviews the latest Market Insights Report and the Institutional Capabilities Register to identify external organisations that could offer strategic value. Candidates are recorded in a tracking system with initial notes on alignment. 2. Screen Partner Candidates: The System runs each candidate through the Compliance Frameworks and Strategic Goals Document. Candidates that pass all automated checks are shortlisted; those that fail are flagged and logged for reference. 3. Evaluate Partnership Opportunity: The Commercial Engagement Lead performs a comprehensive evaluation including financial health checks, reputation analysis, and strategic alignment assessment. A due diligence report is produced for each candidate. 4. Review Due Diligence Findings: The Head of Commercial Strategy reviews the Due Diligence Report. If the opportunity meets strategic and risk criteria, it is approved for negotiation. If not, the opportunity is rejected and the candidate is notified. 5. Negotiate Terms: The Commercial Engagement Lead and Legal & Compliance Officer engage with the partner to agree on scope, revenue share, IP rights, and other key terms. Negotiation outcomes are documented in a term sheet. 6. Draft Partnership Agreement: The Legal & Compliance Officer drafts the partnership agreement incorporating all agreed terms, compliance requirements, and risk mitigations. The draft is prepared for executive approval. 7. Approve Partnership Agreement: The Head of Commercial Strategy reviews the draft agreement. If acceptable, it is signed and becomes the final agreement. If issues are found, it is returned with remediation instructions. 8. Develop Engagement Plan: The Commercial Engagement Lead designs an engagement plan detailing activities, milestones, governance structure, and performance metrics. The plan is aligned with the signed agreement. 9. Finalise and Archive: The System stores the signed partnership agreement and engagement plan in the institutional repository. A completion notification is sent to all stakeholders.
