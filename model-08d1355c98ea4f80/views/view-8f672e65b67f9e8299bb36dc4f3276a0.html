## Purpose Personal Learning Management enables students to take ownership of their academic journey by setting individual learning goals that are informed by their performance metrics and feedback. The process ensures goals are realistic, aligned with career aspirations, and supported by a tailored learning pathway. Regular monitoring and review cycles keep students engaged and allow for timely adjustments to their development plans. ## Roles - Student: Initiates goal setting, provides feedback, and monitors progress - Academic Advisor: Reviews and approves goals, provides guidance - System: Generates learning pathways and tracks performance data ## Upstream Inputs - Student Feedback - Performance Metrics - Goal Request ## Downstream Outputs - Personalised Learning Pathway - Development Plan - Progress Report ## Key Decisions - Whether the proposed learning goal is realistic and aligned with performance data - Whether the student's progress warrants a plan adjustment or remains on track ## Exception Paths - If a goal is rejected, the student must revise and resubmit the request - If progress is insufficient, the plan is adjusted and a new review cycle begins ## Step Walkthrough 1. Submit Goal Request: Student submits a learning goal request with initial aspirations and rationale. 2. Review Performance Data: Academic Advisor reviews the student's performance metrics and feedback to assess goal feasibility. 3. Assess Goal Alignment: Academic Advisor evaluates whether the goal aligns with academic standards and career aspirations. 4. Approve or Reject Goal: Academic Advisor makes a decision — if approved, the process continues; if rejected, the student revises the request. 5. Generate Learning Pathway: System creates a personalised learning pathway based on the approved goal and student data. 6. Monitor Progress: Student and Academic Advisor track progress against the pathway using periodic check-ins. 7. Review and Adjust Plan: Academic Advisor reviews progress and decides whether to adjust the development plan or confirm completion. Steps: 1. Submit Goal Request: The student accesses the learning management system and fills out a goal request form, specifying their academic and career aspirations. This step initiates the personal learning management process and captures the student's initial intent. 2. Review Performance Data: The Academic Advisor accesses the student's performance dashboard, reviewing recent grades, assessment results, and feedback from instructors. This data provides context for evaluating the feasibility of the proposed goal. 3. Assess Goal Alignment: The Academic Advisor compares the proposed goal against academic program requirements and the student's career aspirations. They consider whether the goal is realistic, challenging, and supportive of the student's long-term development. 4. Approve or Reject Goal: Based on the alignment assessment, the Academic Advisor either approves the goal, allowing it to proceed, or rejects it with feedback. If rejected, the student must revise and resubmit the goal request. 5. Revise Goal Request: The student reviews the rejection feedback and adjusts the goal request accordingly. This step ensures the goal is refined to meet alignment criteria before resubmission. 6. Generate Learning Pathway: The system automatically generates a structured learning pathway that includes recommended courses, milestones, resources, and timelines tailored to the student's goal and performance data. 7. Monitor Progress: The student follows the learning pathway while the system tracks completion of milestones and performance indicators. The Academic Advisor periodically checks progress reports to identify any issues. 8. Review and Adjust Plan: The Academic Advisor examines the progress report and determines whether the student is on track. If progress is insufficient, the plan is adjusted with new milestones or resources; otherwise, the current plan is confirmed as complete.
