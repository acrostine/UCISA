Merchandising ()
## Purpose This process manages the end-to-end lifecycle of branded merchandise, from initial design concept through procurement and distribution, to final sales reporting. It ensures that all merchandise aligns with brand guidelines, supports promotional events, and stays within budget allocations, ultimately enhancing brand visibility and stakeholder loyalty. ## Roles - Brand Manager - Procurement Officer - Warehouse Coordinator - System ## Upstream Inputs - Brand Guidelines - Event Schedules - Budget Allocations ## Downstream Outputs - Merchandise Inventories - Distribution Plans - Sales Reports ## Key Decisions - Whether the merchandise design meets brand guidelines - Whether supplier quotes fall within budget - Whether received goods pass quality inspection - Whether distribution quantities match event requirements ## Exception Paths - Design rejected: returned to design step for rework - Quote over budget: procurement officer negotiates or selects alternative supplier - Quality check failed: goods returned to supplier or rework initiated - Distribution shortfall: inventory reallocated or emergency procurement triggered ## Step Walkthrough 1. Define Merchandise Requirements: Review brand guidelines and event schedules to specify merchandise types, quantities, and design themes. 2. Design Merchandise: Create design concepts that align with brand guidelines and campaign objectives. 3. Review Design: Evaluate design against brand standards and approve or reject. 4. Source Suppliers: Identify and request quotes from approved suppliers for the required merchandise. 5. Evaluate Quotes: Compare supplier quotes against budget allocations and select the best option. 6. Procure Merchandise: Place purchase orders and manage delivery logistics. 7. Inspect Received Goods: Verify received merchandise for quality, quantity, and brand compliance. 8. Allocate Inventory: Assign merchandise to specific events or distribution channels based on demand. 9. Distribute Merchandise: Ship or deliver merchandise to event locations or retail points. 10. Generate Sales Report: Compile sales data and inventory adjustments into a final performance report. Steps: 1. Define Merchandise Requirements: The Brand Manager reviews brand guidelines and upcoming event schedules to determine what merchandise is needed. This includes defining product categories, quantities, target audiences, and any thematic requirements. The output is a requirements document that guides the design and procurement phases. 2. Design Merchandise: Using the requirements document, the Brand Manager or a designated designer creates initial design concepts. These concepts incorporate brand colors, logos, and messaging appropriate for the target audience and event. Multiple options may be developed for review. 3. Review Design: The Brand Manager reviews the submitted design concepts for compliance with brand guidelines, aesthetic quality, and suitability for the intended audience. A decision is made to approve the design for procurement or to reject it with feedback for revision. 4. Source Suppliers: The Procurement Officer contacts approved suppliers, providing them with the approved design and specifications. Requests for quotes are sent, including quantities, delivery timelines, and quality standards. Supplier responses are collected for evaluation. 5. Evaluate Quotes: The Procurement Officer reviews each supplier quote, comparing pricing, lead times, and quality guarantees against the budget and requirements. A decision is made to select a supplier or to reject all quotes if they exceed budget or fail to meet specifications. 6. Procure Merchandise: The Procurement Officer issues a purchase order to the selected supplier, confirming quantities, pricing, delivery dates, and terms. Logistics are coordinated to ensure timely delivery to the warehouse or distribution center. Order status is tracked until shipment. 7. Inspect Received Goods: Upon delivery, the Warehouse Coordinator inspects the merchandise against the purchase order and approved design. Checks include product quality, quantity accuracy, and adherence to brand specifications. A decision is made to accept the goods or to flag issues for resolution. 8. Allocate Inventory: The Warehouse Coordinator reviews event schedules and distribution plans to allocate inventory appropriately. Quantities are assigned to each event or channel, ensuring that high-priority events receive sufficient stock. Allocation is recorded in the inventory system. 9. Distribute Merchandise: The Warehouse Coordinator arranges for the physical distribution of allocated merchandise. This includes packing, labeling, and coordinating with logistics providers for delivery. Distribution is tracked to confirm receipt at destination. 10. Generate Sales Report: The System aggregates sales data from distribution channels and inventory adjustments from the warehouse. A sales report is generated showing quantities sold, revenue, remaining inventory, and any discrepancies. This report is used for performance analysis and future planning.
