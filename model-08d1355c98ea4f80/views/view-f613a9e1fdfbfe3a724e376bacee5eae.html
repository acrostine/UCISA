Revise Research Protocol: Based on feedback from the Ethics Committee, the Principal Investigator and team update the research protocol, consent forms, or data handling procedures. This includes setting up experiments, conducting surveys, or performing fieldwork. All activities are logged for traceability. 5. Collect and Record Data: Data from experiments, surveys, or observations is collected and recorded in the research database. Automated sensors and manual entry are used. Data is timestamped and attributed to the responsible team member. 6. Validate Data Integrity: Quality Assurance reviews the collected data against predefined quality criteria. Checks include outlier detection, missing value analysis, and consistency with methodology. A pass/fail decision is made. 7. Repeat Data Collection: If data integrity issues are found, the Research Team identifies root causes, corrects procedures, and repeats the data collection process. The new data is submitted for re-validation. 8. Analyse Research Data: The Research Team applies appropriate analytical methods to the validated data. Statistical tests, thematic analysis, or modelling techniques are used. Preliminary findings are documented. 9. Review Findings Quality: The Principal Investigator reviews the preliminary findings against the research objectives and quality criteria. This includes checking statistical significance, reproducibility, and alignment with the hypothesis. A pass/fail decision is made. 10. Rework Data Analysis: If findings do not meet quality thresholds, the Research Team re-examines the analysis methodology, corrects errors, or applies alternative techniques. The revised analysis is resubmitted for review. 11. Certify Ethics Compliance: After research activities are complete, the Ethics Committee reviews the conduct of the research to ensure ongoing compliance. A final certificate is issued confirming ethical standards were maintained throughout. 12. Finalise Validated Findings: The Principal Investigator compiles the validated findings, supporting data, and ethics certificate into a final research output package. This is approved and prepared for dissemination, publication, or patent filing.
