## Purpose This process ensures that all research activities within the institution comply with legal, ethical, and funding agency requirements. It systematically reviews projects, identifies compliance gaps, and produces documented evidence of adherence, thereby protecting the institution from legal and financial penalties. ## Roles - Compliance Officer: Reviews project details against regulations, conducts risk assessments, and approves or rejects compliance status. - Research Administrator: Submits project details and receives compliance reports. - System: Automates regulatory checks and stores compliance records. ## Upstream Inputs - Regulatory Guidelines: Updated legal and ethical standards from external bodies. - Ethical Standards: Institutional and field-specific ethical codes. - Funding Agency Requirements: Conditions attached to research grants. - Project Details: Scope, methodology, and personnel information for each research project. ## Downstream Outputs - Compliance Reports: Documented findings of compliance review for each project. - Audit Records: Log of all compliance checks, decisions, and remediation actions. - Risk Assessments: Evaluations of compliance risk levels for projects. ## Key Decisions - Does the project meet all regulatory and ethical requirements? - What level of risk does the project pose to the institution? - Is remediation required before compliance can be granted? ## Exception Paths - If a project fails compliance review, a remediation step is triggered to address specific gaps. - If remediation is unsuccessful or the project poses unacceptable risk, the project is rejected and escalated to senior management. ## Step Walkthrough 1.
