Student Results Mgt ()
## Purpose This process ensures that all student assessment outcomes are accurately recorded, verified against institutional academic regulations, and published as official results transcripts and progress reports. It maintains data integrity, confidentiality, and compliance with regulatory requirements, providing a reliable basis for academic progression and external reporting. ## Roles - **Results Administrator**: Records and manages assessment data in the system. - **Academic Registrar**: Verifies results against regulations and approves publication. - **Quality Assurance Officer**: Audits results for accuracy and compliance. - **System**: Automated validation and notification services. ## Upstream Inputs - Marked Assessments - Moderation Inputs - Academic Regulations - Student Enrolment Data ## Downstream Outputs - Official Results Transcripts - Progress Reports - Academic Progression Notifications - Audit Trail Logs ## Key Decisions - Are all results recorded correctly and completely? - Do results comply with academic regulations and progression rules? - Are discrepancies identified and resolved before publication? - Is the publication approved by the Academic Registrar? ## Exception Paths - **Data Entry Error**: If a recording error is found, the step loops back to 'Record Results' for correction. - **Regulation Non-Compliance**: If results fail compliance checks, they are sent to 'Resolve Compliance Issues' before re-verification. - **Audit Finding**: If the audit identifies a discrepancy, the process loops to 'Resolve Audit Findings' and then re-enters verification. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Receive Assessment Data**: The Results Administrator collects marked assessments and moderation inputs from upstream sources. 2. **Record Results**: The Results Administrator enters all assessment outcomes into the institutional records system. 3. **Verify Results Accuracy**: The system automatically checks recorded data for completeness and consistency against source documents. 4. **Resolve Data Errors**: If an error is detected, the Results Administrator corrects the data and re-enters it, looping back to step 2. 5. **Check Regulatory Compliance**: The Academic Registrar reviews results against academic regulations and progression rules. 6. **Resolve Compliance Issues**: If non-compliance is found, the Academic Registrar adjusts records or escalates, then re-enters compliance check. 7. **Audit Results**: The Quality Assurance Officer audits a sample of results for accuracy and confidentiality. 8. **Resolve Audit Findings**: If issues are found, the Quality Assurance Officer works with the Results Administrator to correct them, then re-enters audit. 9. **Approve Publication**: The Academic Registrar formally approves the results for release. 10. **Publish Results**: The system generates and distributes official transcripts and progress reports to students and stakeholders. Steps: 1. Receive Assessment Data: The Results Administrator gathers all marked assessments, moderation reports, and any supplementary data from faculty and exam boards. This step ensures all required inputs are available before recording begins. 2. Record Results: The Results Administrator inputs each student's marks, grades, and any special conditions into the student records database. Data entry follows predefined templates to ensure consistency. 3. Verify Results Accuracy: The system runs validation rules to compare recorded results against source documents, checking for missing entries, duplicate records, or out-of-range values. Any discrepancies are flagged for review. 4. Resolve Data Errors: The Results Administrator reviews the error report, corrects the affected records, and re-submits the data for verification. This step ensures data integrity before proceeding. 5. Check Regulatory Compliance: The Academic Registrar examines the verified results to ensure they comply with institutional policies, including grade boundaries, credit requirements, and progression criteria. Non-compliant results are flagged. 6. Resolve Compliance Issues: The Academic Registrar works with faculty to adjust records, apply exceptions, or escalate to the exam board. Once resolved, results are re-submitted for compliance checking. 7. Audit Results: The Quality Assurance Officer selects a representative sample of results and verifies them against source documents, ensuring no unauthorized changes and that confidentiality protocols were followed. 8. Resolve Audit Findings: The Quality Assurance Officer and Results Administrator collaborate to fix any discrepancies found, updating records as needed. Corrected results are re-audited. 9. Approve Publication: The Academic Registrar reviews the final audit report and gives formal approval to publish. This step authorizes the system to generate official documents. 10. Publish Results: The system automatically generates official results transcripts and progress reports, then distributes them to students via the student portal and to authorized stakeholders. An audit trail is logged.
