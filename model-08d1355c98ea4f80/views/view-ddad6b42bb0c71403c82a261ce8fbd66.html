Non-Academic Achievement Mgt ()
## Purpose This process manages the lifecycle of non-academic achievement records, from initial submission through validation and final recording. It ensures that extracurricular involvement, leadership, and community service are formally recognized and added to student profiles, supporting a comprehensive view of student development. ## Roles - Student: Submits achievement claims and provides supporting evidence - Staff Member: Submits achievement claims on behalf of students or verifies submissions - Achievement Coordinator: Reviews and validates submissions against eligibility criteria - System: Automates logging, notifications, and record updates ## Upstream Inputs - Achievement Claim Submission (from student or staff) - Supporting Evidence (documents, certificates, letters) - Eligibility Criteria Reference Data ## Downstream Outputs - Verified Achievement Record (added to student profile) - Rejection Notification (sent to submitter) - Remediation Request (sent to submitter for incomplete claims) ## Key Decisions - Is the submission complete with all required evidence? - Does the achievement meet the defined eligibility criteria? - Is the achievement authentic and verifiable? ## Exception Paths - Incomplete submission: routed back to submitter for remediation - Ineligible achievement: rejected with explanation - Unverifiable evidence: rejected or escalated for manual review ## Step Walkthrough 1. Submit Achievement Claim: Student or staff member submits a claim with supporting evidence through the portal. 2. Log Submission: System automatically logs the claim and assigns a tracking ID. 3. Review Submission Completeness: Achievement Coordinator checks if all required fields and evidence are provided. 4. Assess Achievement Eligibility: Coordinator evaluates the achievement against defined criteria for recognition. 5. Validate Achievement Authenticity: Coordinator verifies the evidence and confirms the achievement is genuine. 6. Record Verified Achievement: System updates the student's profile with the verified achievement record. 7. Notify Submitter of Outcome: System sends a notification to the submitter confirming the record or providing rejection/remediation details. Steps: 1. Submit Achievement Claim: The submitter accesses the achievement submission portal, selects the achievement type (extracurricular, leadership, community service), enters details, and uploads supporting evidence such as certificates or letters. The submission is saved as a draft before final submission. 2. Log Submission: Upon submission, the system automatically captures the claim data, assigns a tracking ID, timestamps the entry, and sets the status to 'Pending Review'. A confirmation is sent to the submitter. 3. Review Submission Completeness: The coordinator reviews the logged claim against a completeness checklist, verifying that all mandatory fields are filled and supporting evidence is attached. If incomplete, the claim is flagged for remediation. 4. Assess Achievement Eligibility: The coordinator checks the achievement type, date, duration, and relevance against the institution's eligibility criteria for non-academic recognition. Eligible claims proceed; ineligible claims are rejected with a reason. 5. Validate Achievement Authenticity: The coordinator contacts the issuing organization or references provided contacts to confirm the achievement's authenticity. If evidence is insufficient or suspicious, the claim is rejected or escalated. 6. Record Verified Achievement: Upon successful validation, the system adds the achievement to the student's non-academic record, updates the status to 'Verified', and makes the record visible on the student's profile and transcript. 7. Notify Submitter of Outcome: The system generates and sends an email or in-app notification to the submitter, confirming the achievement record or providing details of rejection or remediation steps required. 8. Remediate Submission: The submitter receives a remediation request, accesses the claim draft, adds missing evidence or corrects fields, and resubmits the claim for re-review.
