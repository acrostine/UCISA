Research Infrastructure Mgt ()
## Purpose Research Infrastructure Management ensures that physical and digital resources—such as laboratory equipment, high-performance computing clusters, data storage, and specialised software—are provisioned, maintained, and optimised to support research activities. The process balances demand against capacity, schedules preventive maintenance, and provides visibility into infrastructure performance and availability. ## Roles - Research Administrator: Reviews resource requests and makes allocation decisions - Infrastructure Manager: Oversees provisioning and maintenance scheduling - System: Automates performance monitoring and availability reporting ## Upstream Inputs - Resource Requests: Submitted by research teams detailing required infrastructure - Usage Data: Historical and real-time utilisation metrics for all assets - Maintenance Schedules: Planned downtime and service windows from vendors ## Downstream Outputs - Availability Reports: Published schedules of resource availability - Resource Allocation Plans: Approved assignments of infrastructure to projects - Infrastructure Performance Metrics: Dashboards showing utilisation, uptime, and capacity ## Key Decisions - Whether to approve or reject a resource request based on capacity and priority - When to schedule preventive maintenance to minimise research disruption - Whether performance metrics indicate a need for capacity expansion ## Exception Paths - Rejected resource requests are returned to the requester with a justification and an invitation to resubmit with adjusted specifications - If maintenance conflicts with a critical research project, the maintenance is rescheduled in coordination with the project team - Performance degradation triggers an alert and an unscheduled maintenance intervention ## Step Walkthrough 1. Allocate Resources: For approved requests, specific infrastructure assets are assigned and provisioning is initiated. 5. Provision Infrastructure: The Infrastructure Manager coordinates the setup and configuration of the allocated resources. 6. Monitor Infrastructure Performance: The System continuously tracks utilisation, uptime, and performance metrics. 7. Schedule Preventive Maintenance: Based on maintenance schedules and performance data, routine maintenance activities are planned and communicated. Steps: 1. Receive Resource Request: The Research Administrator logs the incoming request, recording the type and quantity of resources needed, the requested timeframe, and the research project details. The request is validated for completeness and entered into the tracking system. 2. Assess Request Against Capacity: The Research Administrator cross-references the request with real-time usage data and upcoming maintenance windows. They determine whether sufficient capacity exists to fulfil the request without disrupting existing commitments. 3. Approve or Reject Request: If capacity is available and the request aligns with research priorities, the request is approved. If not, it is rejected with a clear justification. This step is a decision gate that splits the process into two paths. 4. Allocate Resources: The Research Administrator selects the exact equipment, computing nodes, or storage volumes to be allocated. The allocation is recorded in the resource management system, reserving the assets for the project's duration. 5. Provision Infrastructure: The Infrastructure Manager coordinates with technical teams to provision the resources—installing software, configuring network access, and ensuring the environment is ready. The research team is notified upon completion. 6. Monitor Infrastructure Performance: The System collects real-time data from all infrastructure assets, updating dashboards with utilisation rates, error logs, and capacity thresholds. Alerts are generated if performance degrades below acceptable levels. 7. Schedule Preventive Maintenance: The Infrastructure Manager reviews maintenance schedules and performance data to identify upcoming service windows. Maintenance tasks are scheduled, communicated to affected research teams, and logged in the maintenance calendar. 8. Return Request with Feedback: The Research Administrator sends the rejected request back to the research team with a detailed explanation and suggestions for alternative resources or adjusted specifications. The team may resubmit an updated request.
