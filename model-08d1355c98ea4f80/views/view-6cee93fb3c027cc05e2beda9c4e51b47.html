## Purpose Student Induction Management ensures that every new student receives a comprehensive and tailored introduction to the institution, covering policies, facilities, and support services. This structured process promotes early engagement, reduces dropout risk, and supports student wellbeing and academic success. ## Roles - Induction Coordinator: Plans and oversees induction activities - Academic Advisor: Provides programme-specific guidance - Student Services Officer: Delivers support service information - IT Support Officer: Manages system access and digital resources - System: Automates communications and records attendance ## Upstream Inputs - Enrolment Data: Confirmed student list with programme and contact details - Academic Calendar: Key dates for scheduling induction events - Institutional Policy Documents: Codes of conduct, academic regulations - Campus Facilities Map: Building and service locations ## Downstream Outputs - Induction Attendance Records: Verified attendance data for each student - Induction Completion Report: Summary of completed inductions and outstanding actions - Tailored Induction Pack: Digital and printed materials provided to students - Feedback Summary: Student satisfaction and improvement insights ## Key Decisions - Determine induction format (online, in-person, or blended) based on student cohort - Approve induction schedule and resource allocation - Decide on remediation for students who miss mandatory sessions - Validate completion of all induction requirements before final sign-off ## Exception Paths - Student fails to attend mandatory induction session: triggers follow-up and catch-up session - Induction materials contain errors: requires rework and re-issue - Technical issues during online induction: IT support escalates and reschedules - Student requests special accommodations: handled via individual adjustment plan ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Enrolment Data: The Induction Coordinator accesses the enrolment system to download the confirmed list of newly enrolled students. The data is checked for completeness, including student names, programmes, contact details, and any special requirements. Any discrepancies are flagged and resolved with the enrolment team before proceeding. 2. Review Cohort Profile: The Induction Coordinator reviews the verified enrolment list to understand the cohort composition, including programme types, study modes, international status, and any flagged support needs. This analysis informs the design of targeted induction sessions and materials. 3. Develop Induction Plan: Based on the cohort profile, the Induction Coordinator designs a comprehensive induction plan. This includes session topics, delivery methods (online, in-person, blended), timings, venue bookings, and assigned speakers. The plan also incorporates mandatory policy briefings and optional social events. 4. Approve Induction Plan: The Academic Advisor reviews the draft induction plan for alignment with academic policies, resource availability, and cohort needs. The plan is either approved for execution or returned with comments for revision. Approval triggers the preparation of materials and scheduling. 5. Prepare Induction Materials: The Student Services Officer and IT Support Officer collaborate to create induction materials. This includes digital presentations, printed handbooks, campus maps, policy summaries, and online resource links. Materials are reviewed for accuracy and branded consistently. 6. Deliver Induction Sessions: The Induction Coordinator and Student Services Officer facilitate induction sessions according to the approved plan. Sessions cover institutional policies, campus facilities, academic support services, and student wellbeing resources. Interactive elements encourage student engagement and questions. 7. Collect Attendance and Feedback: The System automatically records attendance via check-in or online login and distributes feedback surveys to all participants. Responses are aggregated to measure satisfaction and identify areas for improvement. Attendance data is linked to each student's record. 8. Review Attendance Gaps: The Induction Coordinator reviews attendance records against the mandatory session list. Students with unauthorised absences are flagged for follow-up. The coordinator determines whether a catch-up session or alternative arrangement is needed. 9. Resolve Attendance Issues: The Induction Coordinator contacts each flagged student via email or phone to explain the missed requirement and schedule a catch-up session. The coordinator updates the attendance record once the student completes the session. Persistent non-attendance is escalated to the Academic Advisor. 10. Finalise Induction Records: The Induction Coordinator performs a final check to ensure all students have completed mandatory induction requirements and that feedback data is captured. The induction status is updated to 'Complete' in the student system, and a summary report is generated for academic departments.
