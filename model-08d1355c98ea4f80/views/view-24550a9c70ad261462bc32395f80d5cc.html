Gather Financial Inputs: The Budget Analyst retrieves historical financial data from the financial system, obtains the latest strategic plans from the planning office, and collects budget requests from academic and administrative units. Consolidate Budget Data: The system processes the collected financial inputs, normalises data formats, and consolidates them into a single draft budget dataset. This dataset includes historical trends, strategic targets, and unit-level requests ready for allocation. 3. Draft Budget Allocations: The Budget Analyst uses the consolidated dataset to draft preliminary budget allocations. Allocations are distributed across academic and administrative units, ensuring alignment with strategic priorities and historical spending patterns. 4. Review Budget Draft: The Budget Manager examines the draft budget allocations, checking for alignment with institutional priorities, compliance with financial policies, and reasonableness of unit-level distributions. The review identifies any issues requiring adjustment. 5. Adjust Allocations: If the Budget Manager identifies issues, the Budget Analyst revises the draft allocations accordingly. Adjustments may involve reallocating funds between units, reducing overspend, or aligning with revised priorities. 6. Approve Budget Framework: The CFO examines the final budget allocations, considering strategic alignment, financial sustainability, and unit feedback. The decision results in either approval to publish or rejection requiring further rework. 7. Publish Approved Budget: The system distributes the approved budget framework to all academic and administrative units, making it available for operational planning and expenditure tracking. 8. Generate Variance Reports: The system produces variance reports that compare actual financial performance against the approved budget allocations. These reports highlight significant variances for management attention and corrective action.
