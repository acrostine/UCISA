Student Exceptional Factors Misconduct / Appeal Mgt ()
## Purpose This process manages the lifecycle of student exceptional factors, misconduct, and appeal cases from initial report through to final resolution. It ensures that each case is evaluated fairly against institutional policies, with clear documentation of evidence, decisions, and remediation actions. The process supports academic integrity by providing structured review pathways for different case types while protecting student rights through transparent procedures and formal communication of outcomes. ## Roles - Student: Submits the case or is the subject of the report - Case Administrator: Logs cases, assigns reviewers, and manages documentation - Case Reviewer: Evaluates evidence and policy, determines outcome - Academic Registrar: Reviews escalated or complex cases - System: Case Management System (CMS) ## Upstream Inputs - Incident Report or Appeal Submission - Supporting Evidence (documents, statements, records) - Relevant Policy Documentation - Student Academic Record ## Downstream Outputs - Case Record with Decision Outcome - Formal Outcome Notification to Student - Remediation Action Plan (if applicable) - Escalation Record (if escalated) ## Key Decisions - Whether the case qualifies as an exceptional factor, misconduct, or appeal - Whether sufficient evidence exists to proceed with review - Whether the case requires escalation to the Academic Registrar - Whether the outcome is approved, rejected, or requires remediation ## Exception Paths - Insufficient evidence: case returned to student for additional information - Complex or high-severity case: escalated to Academic Registrar for review - Student disputes outcome: triggers a separate appeal process - Non-compliance with remediation: escalated to disciplinary committee ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Case Report: The Case Administrator receives the incoming case report, which may be an incident report, an exceptional factors application, or a formal appeal submission. The submission is acknowledged and logged into the Case Management System with a preliminary status of 'Received'. Basic details such as student ID, case type, and date are recorded. 2. Log and Categorise Case: The Case Administrator categorises the case as one of three types: exceptional factor, misconduct, or appeal. A unique case identifier is generated, and the case record is updated with the category, priority level, and any initial notes. The case status changes to 'Logged'. 3. Assign Case to Reviewer: The Case Administrator selects a Case Reviewer based on case type, workload balance, and expertise. The assignment is recorded in the system, and the reviewer receives a notification with a link to the case record. The case status changes to 'Assigned'. 4. Review Evidence and Policy: The Case Reviewer accesses the case record and reviews all submitted evidence, including documents, statements, and records. Relevant policy documentation is consulted to understand the applicable rules and procedures. Notes are added to the case record summarising key findings. 5. Assess Case Validity: The Case Reviewer evaluates whether the case contains sufficient evidence to proceed and whether it meets the policy criteria for the assigned case type. If the evidence is insufficient or the case does not meet criteria, the case is returned to the student for additional information. If valid, the case proceeds to outcome determination. 6. Determine Outcome: The Case Reviewer makes a final determination on the case outcome. Options include approval of the exceptional factor or appeal, rejection with rationale, or a remediation action plan for misconduct cases. The decision is recorded in the case record with supporting justification. 7. Escalate if Required: If the case is deemed complex, high-severity, or outside the Case Reviewer's authority, it is escalated to the Academic Registrar for a final decision. The escalation is recorded with a summary of findings and the reason for escalation. The Academic Registrar reviews and issues a binding decision. 8. Communicate Decision: The Case Administrator prepares and sends a formal outcome notification to the student. The notification includes the decision, rationale, and any next steps such as remediation requirements or appeal rights. The case record is updated with the communication date and status changed to 'Decision Communicated'. 9. Implement Remediation: If the outcome includes remediation actions, the Case Administrator creates a remediation action plan with specific tasks, deadlines, and responsible parties. Progress is tracked in the system until all actions are completed and the case is closed. The case status changes to 'Closed' upon completion.
