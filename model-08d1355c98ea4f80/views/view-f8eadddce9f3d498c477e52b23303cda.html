Receive Expenditure Request: The process begins when a purchase order, invoice, or payment request is submitted by an internal department or vendor. The request is logged into the financial system with relevant details such as amount, vendor, and reference number. 2. Validate Budget Availability: The system automatically compares the request amount against the remaining budget for the relevant cost center. If funds are sufficient, the request proceeds; otherwise, a budget alert is triggered. 3. Review Request Details: The Finance Officer examines the request for correct coding, proper authorization, and alignment with procurement policies. Any discrepancies or missing information are noted for correction. 4. Approve or Reject Expenditure: Based on the review, the Finance Officer either approves the request for payment or rejects it due to policy violations, inaccuracies, or budget issues. Rejected requests are sent back for remediation. 5. Resolve Rejected Request: The original requester receives notification of rejection with reasons. They correct the issues (e.g., update coding, attach missing approvals) and resubmit the request for re-review. 6. Process Payment: The Accounts Payable Clerk processes the approved expenditure by scheduling payment via bank transfer or check. Payment confirmation is recorded in the system. 7. Generate Expenditure Report: The system aggregates all processed expenditures for the period, including budget variances and payment statuses, and generates a report for management review. 8. Archive Expenditure Record: The final expenditure record, including all approvals and payment details, is archived in the financial system for compliance and future reference.
