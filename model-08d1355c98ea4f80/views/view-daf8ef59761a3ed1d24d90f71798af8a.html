## Purpose Personal Tutor Provision ensures every student has a dedicated academic mentor who provides ongoing pastoral support and guidance. By leveraging student academic records and wellbeing indicators, the process enables early identification of at-risk students and delivers tailored interventions that promote engagement and resilience. ## Roles - Programme Coordinator: Oversees tutor allocation and approves assignments. - Personal Tutor: Provides mentorship, conducts meetings, and develops support plans. - Student: Engages with the tutor and participates in the support process. - System: Automates data analysis, matching, and record-keeping. ## Upstream Inputs - Student Academic Records - Wellbeing Indicators - Tutor Availability Data ## Downstream Outputs - Personalised Guidance Plan - Early Intervention Strategy - Tutor Assignment Record ## Key Decisions - Does the student require a standard or specialist tutor based on wellbeing indicators? - Is the initial support plan effective based on progress monitoring? - Should the case be escalated to student support services? ## Exception Paths - If no suitable tutor is available, the coordinator must manually assign or request a temporary allocation. - If the student does not attend the initial meeting, the tutor follows up twice before escalating. - If the support plan is ineffective after two reviews, the case is escalated to student support services. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Identify Student Need: The process is triggered when a new student enrols or when a wellbeing indicator crosses a threshold. The system automatically creates a tutor assignment request, capturing the student's ID and the trigger reason. 2. Analyse Student Profile: The system analyses the student's academic performance, attendance, and wellbeing flags. It categorises the student as standard, at-risk, or requiring specialist support, which influences tutor matching criteria. 3. Recommend Tutor Match: Using the student's support profile and tutor availability data, the system ranks potential tutors by expertise match and current caseload. The top three recommendations are presented to the coordinator. 4. Allocate Tutor: The coordinator examines the recommended tutors, considers any additional context, and selects the most appropriate tutor. The assignment is recorded in the system, and notifications are sent to the tutor and student. 5. Review Student Context: The tutor logs into the system, reviews the student's academic history, wellbeing notes, and the reason for assignment. This preparation ensures the initial meeting is focused and productive. 6. Conduct Initial Meeting: The tutor holds a one-to-one meeting (in person or virtual) to build rapport, understand the student's challenges, and set expectations for the tutoring relationship. Meeting notes are recorded in the system. 7. Develop Support Plan: Based on the meeting, the tutor drafts a support plan outlining goals, actions, resources, and review dates. The plan is shared with the student for agreement and stored in the system. 8. Monitor Progress: The tutor periodically checks the student's attendance, assignment submissions, and any new wellbeing flags. Progress is logged against the support plan goals. 9. Review Plan Effectiveness: The tutor evaluates progress data against the plan's goals. If objectives are being met, the plan continues. If not, the tutor decides whether to adjust the plan or escalate to student support services.
