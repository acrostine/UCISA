It supports a culture of accountability and continuous improvement by turning individual experiences into actionable service enhancements. ## Roles - Feedback Officer: Logs and triages incoming feedback - Complaint Handler: Investigates and resolves complaints - Service Manager: Reviews trends and approves service improvements - System: Automated notifications and record updates ## Upstream Inputs - Feedback Reports - Feedback Forms - Communication Channel Records ## Downstream Outputs - Resolved Complaint Record - Acknowledged Compliment Record - Service Improvement Recommendation ## Key Decisions - Is the feedback a complaint or a compliment? - Does the complaint require escalation? - Are systemic issues identified? ## Exception Paths - If a complaint is assessed as high severity, it is escalated to senior management - If investigation reveals a recurring issue, a service improvement recommendation is raised - If feedback is incomplete, the Feedback Officer requests clarification ## Step Walkthrough 1. Receive Feedback: The Feedback Officer monitors reports, feedback forms, and communication channels for new submissions. Each item is logged into the feedback management system with a unique identifier, timestamp, and source. Incomplete submissions are flagged for clarification. 2. Assess Feedback Type: The Feedback Officer reviews the content and context of the logged feedback. Based on predefined criteria, the item is categorised as either a complaint (requiring investigation) or a compliment (requiring acknowledgment). Misclassified items are re-routed. 3. Acknowledge Compliment: For feedback categorised as a compliment, the system triggers an automated acknowledgment email to the submitter. The compliment is recorded in the system for reporting and recognition purposes. No further action is required. 4. Investigate Complaint: The Complaint Handler reviews the complaint record, interviews relevant parties, and collects supporting evidence. The investigation aims to identify the root cause and contributing factors. A preliminary findings report is prepared. 5. Resolve Complaint: Based on the investigation findings, the Complaint Handler determines and implements an appropriate resolution. This may include corrective actions, apologies, or process adjustments. The complaint record is updated with the resolution details and closed. 6. Review for Trends: The Service Manager reviews a batch of resolved complaints and acknowledged compliments over a defined period. The analysis looks for recurring themes, frequent issues, or areas of excellence. A trend report is generated. 7. Initiate Improvement: If the trend analysis identifies a systemic issue or opportunity for improvement, the Service Manager creates a formal service improvement recommendation. This includes proposed changes, expected benefits, and an owner for implementation. The recommendation is logged for tracking.
