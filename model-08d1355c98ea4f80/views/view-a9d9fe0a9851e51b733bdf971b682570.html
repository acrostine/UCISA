Other Tertiary Institution Relationship Mgt ()
## Purpose This process governs how the institution identifies, evaluates, formalises, and manages strategic relationships with other higher education institutions. It ensures that partnerships align with institutional goals, are properly documented via MoUs, and deliver tangible outcomes such as joint research, student exchanges, and benchmarking insights. ## Roles - **Partnership Manager**: Initiates and stewards institutional relationships - **Academic Lead**: Provides academic input for joint research and exchange programs - **Legal Counsel**: Reviews and approves MoU terms - **Executive Sponsor**: Provides final approval for strategic partnerships - **System**: CRM and document management system ## Upstream Inputs - Collaboration Proposals - Academic Data (research strengths, program offerings) - Institutional Strategic Plan - Partner Due Diligence Reports ## Downstream Outputs - Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) - Collaborative Program Agreements - Partnership Performance Reports - Academic Network Expansion Records ## Key Decisions - Whether a proposed partnership aligns with institutional strategy - Whether due diligence findings are acceptable - Whether MoU terms are legally sound - Whether to renew, modify, or terminate a partnership ## Exception Paths - Due diligence reveals reputational or financial risks → partnership declined - MoU negotiation fails to reach agreement → partnership abandoned - Collaborative program underperforms → remediation or termination ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Identify Collaboration Opportunities**: Partnership Manager scans academic networks and reviews incoming proposals to identify potential partner institutions. 2. **Evaluate Partnership Proposal**: Academic Lead and Partnership Manager assess the proposal against strategic fit, academic value, and resource requirements. 3. **Conduct Due Diligence**: Partnership Manager investigates the prospective partner's reputation, financial stability, and accreditation status. 4. **Assess Due Diligence Findings**: Partnership Manager reviews the due diligence report and determines whether to proceed or decline. 5. **Negotiate MoU Terms**: Partnership Manager and Legal Counsel negotiate the scope, obligations, and legal terms of the MoU with the partner institution. 6. **Review MoU Legally**: Legal Counsel reviews the final draft MoU for compliance and risk. 7. **Approve Partnership**: Executive Sponsor provides final sign-off on the MoU and partnership. 8. **Execute MoU**: Partnership Manager coordinates signing and stores the executed MoU in the document management system. 9. **Establish Collaborative Programs**: Academic Lead and Partnership Manager design and launch joint research projects, student exchange frameworks, or benchmarking activities. 10. **Monitor Partnership Performance**: Partnership Manager tracks program outcomes, gathers feedback, and produces a performance report. 11. **Review Partnership Outcomes**: Partnership Manager and Academic Lead assess performance against objectives and decide on renewal, modification, or termination. Steps: 1. Identify Collaboration Opportunities: The Partnership Manager actively monitors academic conferences, research networks, and unsolicited proposals from other institutions. They compile a shortlist of potential partners based on strategic alignment and academic reputation. 2. Evaluate Partnership Proposal: The Academic Lead and Partnership Manager jointly review the shortlisted partner's proposal. They evaluate alignment with institutional research strengths, program gaps, and potential mutual benefits. 3. Conduct Due Diligence: The Partnership Manager gathers information on the partner's accreditation, financial health, legal history, and reputation. This may involve reviewing public records, contacting references, and consulting internal stakeholders. 4. Assess Due Diligence Findings: The Partnership Manager analyses the due diligence report for red flags. If findings are acceptable, the process moves to negotiation; if not, the partnership is declined. 5. Negotiate MoU Terms: The Partnership Manager and Legal Counsel engage with the partner institution to negotiate the MoU. Key areas include research areas, exchange quotas, intellectual property, duration, and termination clauses. 6. Review MoU Legally: Legal Counsel examines the draft MoU to ensure it meets institutional policies, regulatory requirements, and adequately protects the institution's interests. They may request amendments. 7. Approve Partnership: The Executive Sponsor reviews the final MoU and the overall business case for the partnership. Approval authorises execution of the MoU and allocation of resources for collaborative programs. 8. Execute MoU: The Partnership Manager coordinates the signing ceremony or electronic signature process. The executed MoU is stored in the document management system and communicated to relevant stakeholders. 9. Establish Collaborative Programs: The Academic Lead and Partnership Manager work with the partner to define specific programs. This includes setting objectives, timelines, resource commitments, and success metrics for each initiative. 10. Monitor Partnership Performance: The Partnership Manager collects data on program outputs (e.g., publications, exchange numbers, benchmarking reports) and solicits feedback from participating faculty and students. A performance report is compiled. 11. Review Partnership Outcomes: The Partnership Manager and Academic Lead review the performance report against the original objectives. They recommend renewal, modification, or termination of the partnership.
