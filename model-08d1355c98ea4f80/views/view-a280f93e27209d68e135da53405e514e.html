Commercial Market Analysis ()
## Purpose This process ensures the institution systematically gathers and evaluates external market data — including competitor activities, sector trends, and market research — to produce reliable market intelligence. The output directly supports strategic commercial decisions, opportunity targeting, and alignment of offerings with market demand. ## Roles - **Commercial Analyst**: Responsible for data collection, validation, and initial analysis. - **Senior Commercial Manager**: Reviews findings, approves the intelligence report, and authorizes distribution. - **System**: Automated data ingestion and report generation tasks. ## Upstream Inputs - Market Research Reports - Competitor Insights - Sector Trend Data - Commercial Strategy Objectives ## Downstream Outputs - Market Intelligence Report - Opportunity Targeting Recommendations - Competitive Positioning Analysis ## Key Decisions - Whether collected data meets quality thresholds for analysis - Whether analysis findings are sufficiently robust for strategic use - Whether the final intelligence report is approved for distribution ## Exception Paths - If data quality is insufficient, the analyst must source additional or replacement data before proceeding. - If analysis findings are rejected by the Senior Commercial Manager, the analyst must rework the analysis and resubmit. ## Step Walkthrough 1. **Define Analysis Scope**: The Commercial Analyst defines the market segments, geographies, and time horizon for the analysis based on strategic objectives. 2. **Collect External Data**: The Commercial Analyst gathers market research reports, competitor insights, and sector trends from subscribed databases and public sources. 3. **Validate Data Quality**: The Commercial Analyst checks collected data for completeness, accuracy, and relevance; data that fails validation is flagged for re-collection. 4. **Analyze Market Data**: The Commercial Analyst performs quantitative and qualitative analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and competitive threats. 5. **Review Analysis Findings**: The Senior Commercial Manager reviews the analysis output for strategic alignment and robustness; if rejected, the analyst reworks the analysis. 6. **Approve Intelligence Report**: The Senior Commercial Manager approves the final market intelligence report for distribution. 7. **Generate and Distribute Report**: The system compiles the approved findings into a formatted Market Intelligence Report and distributes it to stakeholders. Steps: 1. Define Analysis Scope: The Commercial Analyst meets with stakeholders to determine the specific market segments, geographic regions, competitor set, and time horizon for the analysis. This scope is aligned with current commercial strategy objectives and documented for reference throughout the process. 2. Collect External Data: Using the defined scope, the Commercial Analyst collects relevant data from subscribed market research databases, competitor monitoring tools, industry publications, and public sector reports. All collected data is catalogued with source metadata for traceability. 3. Validate Data Quality: The Commercial Analyst reviews the collected data against quality criteria including completeness, recency, source credibility, and relevance to the defined scope. Data items that fail validation are flagged for re-collection or replacement. 4. Analyze Market Data: The Commercial Analyst applies quantitative methods (market sizing, growth rate calculation) and qualitative techniques (SWOT analysis, competitor profiling) to the validated data. Key outputs include trend identification, opportunity assessment, and competitive positioning insights. 5. Review Analysis Findings: The Senior Commercial Manager evaluates the draft analysis findings for strategic relevance, analytical rigor, and actionable insights. If the findings are insufficient or misaligned, the manager provides feedback and returns the analysis for rework. 6. Approve Intelligence Report: After satisfactory review, the Senior Commercial Manager formally approves the analysis findings as the basis for the final market intelligence report. This approval authorizes the report to be compiled and distributed. 7. Generate and Distribute Report: The system automatically formats the approved intelligence content into a standardized Market Intelligence Report template, including executive summary, key findings, opportunity recommendations, and competitive analysis. The report is distributed via email to the predefined stakeholder list and archived in the knowledge repository. 8. Source Replacement Data: When data quality validation fails, the Commercial Analyst identifies alternative sources for the flagged data items, collects the replacement data, and re-submits it for validation. This step ensures only high-quality data enters the analysis phase. 9. Rework Analysis: The Commercial Analyst addresses the feedback provided by the Senior Commercial Manager, adjusting the analysis methodology, correcting errors, or adding additional insights as required. The revised analysis is then resubmitted for review.
