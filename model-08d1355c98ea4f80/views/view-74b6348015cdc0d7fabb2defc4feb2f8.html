## Purpose The Campus Parking process ensures that parking facilities across the university estate are allocated fairly, accessed securely, and managed efficiently. It balances demand from staff, students, and visitors with available capacity, enforces compliance with parking regulations, and provides data-driven insights for continuous improvement. ## Roles - Parking Applicant (Staff, Student, or Visitor) - Parking Administrator - Enforcement Officer - System (Automated validation and reporting) ## Upstream Inputs - Parking Demand Data - Regulatory Guidelines - User Eligibility Information ## Downstream Outputs - Parking Permits - Usage Reports - Enforcement Actions ## Key Decisions - Whether the applicant is eligible for a parking permit - Whether parking capacity is available for the requested period - Whether a parking violation has occurred and what enforcement action to take ## Exception Paths - If eligibility check fails, the application is rejected with a notification to the applicant - If capacity is full, the applicant is placed on a waitlist - If a violation is disputed, the case is escalated to a review process ## Step Walkthrough 1. Submit Parking Request: The applicant, who may be a staff member, student, or visitor, completes a parking request form via the university portal. The form captures personal identification, vehicle registration, requested parking zone, and duration. The request is submitted to the system for processing. 2. Validate Eligibility: The system automatically validates the applicant's eligibility by cross-referencing their details with university policy, regulatory guidelines, and any existing permits. This step ensures that only authorised individuals receive parking access. 3. Check Capacity Availability: The system checks real-time parking capacity data for the requested zone and time period. If capacity is available, the request proceeds; otherwise, the applicant is notified and placed on a waitlist. 4. Allocate Permit: Upon successful eligibility and capacity checks, the Parking Administrator reviews the request and assigns a specific parking permit, including zone, duration, and any special conditions. The permit is then prepared for issuance. 5. Issue Permit: The system automatically generates the parking permit in digital format and sends it to the applicant via email or the university portal. The permit includes a unique identifier, validity period, and parking zone details. 6. Monitor Parking Usage: The Enforcement Officer conducts regular patrols of parking facilities, checking for violations such as expired permits, unauthorised parking, or overstaying. Observed violations are recorded in the system for enforcement action. 7. Generate Usage Report: The system aggregates data from permit allocations, capacity checks, and violation records to produce a comprehensive usage report. This report is used by administrators to optimise parking resource allocation and inform policy decisions.
