## Purpose Quality Management establishes and maintains standards, policies, and procedures to ensure excellence in institutional processes and outcomes. It leverages audit results, feedback, and performance metrics to drive continuous improvement. The process produces quality assurance reports and improvement plans that uphold academic and operational standards across the university. ## Roles - Quality Manager: Oversees the quality management process, reviews inputs, and approves improvement plans. - Quality Analyst: Collects and analyses audit results, feedback, and performance metrics. - Process Owner: Implements improvement actions within their area of responsibility. - System: Automates data collection, analysis, and report generation. ## Upstream Inputs - Audit Results - Stakeholder Feedback - Performance Metrics - Existing Quality Standards and Policies ## Downstream Outputs - Quality Assurance Report - Improvement Plan - Updated Standards and Policies ## Key Decisions - Whether performance gaps require formal improvement planning - Approval of proposed improvement actions and resource allocation - Determination of improvement plan effectiveness after implementation ## Exception Paths - If audit results indicate critical non-compliance, immediate escalation to senior management occurs. - If an improvement plan is rejected, it is returned for revision with documented reasons. - If implemented improvements fail to close the gap, a root cause analysis is triggered. ## Step Walkthrough 1. Collect Inputs: The Quality Analyst collects all relevant data sources including internal audit reports, stakeholder surveys, student feedback, and operational performance dashboards. Assess Gaps and Opportunities: The Quality Manager reviews the gap analysis results, assesses the severity and root cause of each gap, and prioritises improvement opportunities based on strategic impact, risk, and resource availability. 4. Develop Improvement Plan: The Quality Manager creates a detailed improvement plan that specifies corrective actions, responsible process owners, required resources, and target completion dates. The plan is documented for approval. 5. Approve Improvement Plan: The Quality Manager reviews the draft improvement plan for feasibility, alignment with standards, and resource adequacy. The plan is either approved for implementation or returned with revision requests. 6. Implement Improvements: The Process Owner carries out the improvement actions as defined in the approved plan. This may involve updating procedures, training staff, or modifying systems. Progress is tracked against the plan timeline. 7. Monitor and Report Outcomes: The Quality Analyst monitors the effectiveness of implemented improvements, collects outcome data, and compiles a comprehensive quality assurance report. The report is distributed to stakeholders and archived for future audits.
