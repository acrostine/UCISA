Programme & Project Mgt ()
Programme and Project Management oversees the planning, execution, and monitoring of initiatives that deliver strategic outcomes. It consumes project charters, resource plans, and risk assessments to ensure timely and within-budget delivery. This process produces status reports and deliverable assessments that enhance transparency and stakeholder confidence in institutional projects.
