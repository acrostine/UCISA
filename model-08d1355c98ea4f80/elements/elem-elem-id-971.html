Information & Data Mgt ()
This process ensures the integrity, quality, and availability of institutional data assets by managing their collection, storage, and maintenance. It consumes raw data from academic and administrative sources and produces validated, consistent datasets. Reliable data underpins effective operations, reporting, and research activities within the institution.
