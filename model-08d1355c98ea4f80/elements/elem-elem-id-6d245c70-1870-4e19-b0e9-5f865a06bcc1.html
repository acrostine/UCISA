Room Inventory and Condition Updates ()

Accommodation Facilities Mgt
Accommodation Allocation

This flow provides Accommodation Allocation with current room inventory status, including rooms taken offline for maintenance, newly refurbished spaces, and updated condition ratings. Accommodation Allocation relies on this to ensure only available, habitable rooms are offered; without it, allocations could assign rooms under repair, causing health and safety risks.
