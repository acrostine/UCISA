Student Completion & Graduation ()
The Student Completion & Graduation capability ensures the effective management and recognition of student achievements at the conclusion of their academic programs. It encompasses the assessment of graduation eligibility, coordination of graduation ceremonies, issuance of completion records and certificates, and the acknowledgement of non-academic accomplishments. Inputs include academic records, program requirements, and extracurricular achievement data, while outputs consist of verified graduation statuses, official certificates, and documented recognitions. This capability enhances institutional reputation, supports student satisfaction, and upholds compliance with academic standards.
