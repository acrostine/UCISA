Commercial Tenancy ()
Commercial Tenancy manages leasing arrangements for university-owned commercial properties, including contract negotiation and tenant relations. It consumes market analysis, property availability data, and legal frameworks to generate tenancy agreements and rental income. This process supports income diversification and strategic use of campus property to benefit institutional financial sustainability.
