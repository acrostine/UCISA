Venue Mgt ()
Venue Management coordinates the scheduling, maintenance, and utilisation of campus spaces for academic, social, and external events. It consumes booking requests, facility maintenance data, and event requirements to optimise space availability and functionality. This process maximises resource use and supports a dynamic environment for learning, collaboration, and community engagement.
