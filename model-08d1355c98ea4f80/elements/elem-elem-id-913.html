Commercial Partner Engagement ()
Commercial Partner Engagement initiates and manages relationships with external organisations to develop collaborative opportunities that align with institutional strategic goals. It consumes market insights, institutional capabilities, and compliance frameworks to identify and evaluate potential partners. This process produces formal partnership agreements and engagement plans that support revenue generation, knowledge exchange, and enhanced institutional reputation. The organisational value lies in expanding the institution’s commercial footprint while mitigating risks associated with external collaborations.
