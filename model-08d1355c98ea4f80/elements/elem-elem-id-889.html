Research Prioritisation ()
Research Prioritisation ranks and selects research opportunities based on strategic alignment, resource availability, and potential impact. It consumes analysis reports, stakeholder inputs, and resource forecasts, producing a prioritized portfolio of research projects. This process ensures optimal allocation of institutional resources towards research activities that maximize academic excellence, societal benefit, and funding success.
