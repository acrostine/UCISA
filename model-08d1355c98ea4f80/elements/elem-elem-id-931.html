Marketing Mgt ()
Marketing Mgt oversees the overall planning, resource allocation, and performance measurement of promotional activities across the institution. It consumes institutional goals, market research insights, and campaign results to align marketing efforts with strategic priorities. The process produces marketing plans, budgets, and performance dashboards that optimise outreach effectiveness and resource use.
