Research Delivery ()
The Research Delivery capability enables the effective execution and management of research activities within a Higher Education institution. It encompasses the processes of Research Dataset Management, Research Output Management, and Research Production, ensuring data integrity, compliance, and the generation of high-quality research outputs. Inputs include research proposals, data sets, and institutional resources, while outputs consist of validated datasets, published research findings, and documented research artifacts. This capability supports the institution's strategic goals by facilitating impactful research, enhancing academic reputation, and securing funding through demonstrable research excellence.
