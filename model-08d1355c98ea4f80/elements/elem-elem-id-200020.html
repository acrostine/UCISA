Research Administration ()
Research Administration manages the operational and regulatory aspects of research activities within the institution. It ensures compliance with ethical, legal, and funding requirements through Research Compliance Management, safeguarding integrity and accountability. Additionally, it oversees the provision and maintenance of research infrastructure, including laboratories, equipment, and digital resources, enabling effective research execution. This capability supports researchers by streamlining administrative processes and facilitating access to essential resources, thereby enhancing the institution's research excellence and reputation.
