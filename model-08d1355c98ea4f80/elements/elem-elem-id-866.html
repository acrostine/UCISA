Student Exceptional Factors Misconduct / Appeal Mgt ()
Student Exceptional Factors, Misconduct, and Appeal Management addresses cases involving academic exceptions, disciplinary issues, and formal appeals submitted by students. It consumes reports, evidence, and relevant policy documentation to evaluate each case and determine appropriate outcomes. This process upholds academic integrity and fairness, providing transparent resolution mechanisms that protect student rights and institutional standards.
