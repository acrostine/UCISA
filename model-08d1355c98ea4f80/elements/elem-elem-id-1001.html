Accommodation Allocation ()
Accommodation Allocation assigns available residential spaces to students and staff based on eligibility, preferences, and institutional policies. It consumes application data, room inventory, and allocation criteria to produce confirmed housing assignments and occupancy records. This process ensures optimal utilisation of accommodation resources while supporting student experience and institutional compliance.
