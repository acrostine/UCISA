Academic Policy & Regulation Mgt ()
This process governs the development, review, and maintenance of academic policies and regulations that ensure compliance with external accreditation standards and internal governance. It consumes inputs from regulatory frameworks, academic committees, and stakeholder consultations to produce formalized policy documents and regulatory guidelines. The process provides a structured framework that supports consistent academic quality and integrity across the institution, enabling transparent decision-making and risk mitigation.
