Sport & Recreation Mgt ()
Sport & Recreation Management organises and maintains athletic facilities, programs, and events to promote physical health and social engagement. It consumes participant data, facility resources, and funding inputs to deliver diverse recreational opportunities. This process enhances community spirit, wellness, and student experience through active campus life.
