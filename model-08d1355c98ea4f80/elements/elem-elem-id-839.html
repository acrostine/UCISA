Student Induction Mgt ()
Student Induction Management coordinates the introduction of new students to institutional policies, facilities, and support services. It consumes enrolment data and delivers tailored induction programmes and communication materials. By fostering early engagement and awareness, this process supports student retention, wellbeing, and successful transition into academic life.
