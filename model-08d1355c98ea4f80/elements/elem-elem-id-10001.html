Commercial Activity ()
The Commercial Activity domain encompasses capabilities that enable a Higher Education institution to effectively manage its external commercial relationships and revenue-generating operations. It includes Commercial Engagement, which focuses on establishing and maintaining partnerships with industry, government, and community organisations to create commercial opportunities. Commercial Monitoring ensures oversight of commercial performance, compliance, and risk management to safeguard institutional interests. Commercial Sourcing manages procurement and contracting activities related to commercial ventures, while Commercial Delivery oversees the execution and fulfilment of commercial services and products. Together, these capabilities support sustainable income diversification and enhance the institution’s strategic impact beyond traditional academic functions.
