Alumni Engagement ()
Alumni Engagement enables sustained interaction and collaboration between the institution and its graduates, enhancing lifelong relationships that support the university's mission. It encompasses activities focused on organising events, managing targeted campaigns, and fostering meaningful connections to promote alumni involvement and benefaction. Key processes include Alumni Event & Campaign Management, which coordinates networking and fundraising events; Alumni Relationship Management, which maintains ongoing communication and engagement; and Benefactor Management, which oversees donor relations and philanthropy. This capability supports reputation building, resource development, and opportunities for current students through mentorship and networking.
