Insurance Mgt ()
Insurance Management administers institutional risk coverage through policy procurement, claims processing, and compliance monitoring. It consumes risk assessments and asset registers to produce insurance schedules and claims reports. This process mitigates financial exposure and safeguards institutional assets and operations against unforeseen events.
