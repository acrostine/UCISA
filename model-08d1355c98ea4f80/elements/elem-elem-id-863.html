Student Record & Details Mgt ()
Student Record and Details Management maintains comprehensive and secure records of student personal information, academic history, and administrative data. It consumes enrolment data, assessment results, and changes in student status to ensure integrity and accessibility of information. This process supports informed decision-making, compliance with data protection regulations, and efficient service delivery across the institution.
