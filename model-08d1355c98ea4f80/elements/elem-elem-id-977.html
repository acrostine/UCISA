Legal & Legislative Compliance ()
Legal & Legislative Compliance monitors and interprets applicable laws, regulations, and institutional policies to guide organisational adherence. It consumes legislative updates, regulatory guidance, and internal procedures. This process safeguards the institution against legal risks, ensures accountability, and promotes a culture of lawful operation in a complex regulatory environment.
