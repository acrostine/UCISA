Offer, Acceptance & Quota Mgt ()
This process manages the allocation and monitoring of course offers, acceptance decisions, and quota limits to ensure balanced enrolment. It consumes applicant data and institutional capacity information, producing confirmed offer lists and updated quota utilization reports. The process supports optimal resource planning and maintains regulatory compliance by aligning student intake with strategic enrolment targets.
