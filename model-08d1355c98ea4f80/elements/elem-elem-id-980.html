Estate Development & Mgt ()
Estate Development & Management plans, coordinates, and oversees construction and refurbishment projects across the university estate. It consumes strategic plans, funding allocations, and compliance standards, delivering completed infrastructure aligned with institutional goals. This process ensures that physical assets meet current and future academic, research, and community needs.
