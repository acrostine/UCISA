Commercial Delivery ()
The Commercial Delivery capability manages the execution and fulfilment of commercial activities within the Higher Education context, ensuring that services and products offered to external clients meet agreed standards and contractual obligations. It encompasses processes such as Commercial Activity Exit Management, which oversees the orderly conclusion or transfer of commercial engagements; Commercial Activity Issue Management, which addresses and resolves operational challenges during service delivery; and Commercial Activity Output Management, which monitors and controls the quality and timeliness of deliverables. This capability ensures effective performance, mitigates risks, and safeguards the institution's reputation in competitive markets by maintaining high standards of service delivery and client satisfaction.
