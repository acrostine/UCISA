Research Output Reporting ()
Research Output Reporting compiles data on scholarly publications and research outputs generated by academic staff. It consumes publication records, citation metrics, and funding data to produce comprehensive reports for internal assessment, funding bodies, and external rankings. This process supports transparency, compliance with funding requirements, and enhances institutional reputation by demonstrating research impact.
