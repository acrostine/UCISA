Treasury Mgt ()
Treasury Management oversees cash flow, investments, and debt financing to optimise institutional liquidity and financial stability. It consumes cash forecasts, investment opportunities, and borrowing needs to produce cash management reports and funding strategies. This process secures necessary funding, maximises returns, and manages financial risks effectively.
