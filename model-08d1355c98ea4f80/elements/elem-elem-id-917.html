Commercial Activity Exit Mgt ()
Commercial Activity Exit Management oversees the orderly conclusion of commercial contracts and partnerships. It consumes exit criteria, contractual obligations, and stakeholder inputs to ensure compliance and mitigate risks. The process produces formal closure documentation, asset reallocation plans, and final performance reports. This management safeguards institutional reputation and financial interests by ensuring smooth transitions and minimizing disruption to ongoing activities.
