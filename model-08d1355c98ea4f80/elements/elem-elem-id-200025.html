Research Opportunity & Planning ()
The Research Opportunity & Planning capability identifies and evaluates potential research initiatives aligned with institutional goals and funding landscapes. It integrates Collaborative Opportunity Management to engage with partners and stakeholders, Research Opportunity Analysis to assess feasibility and impact, and Research Prioritisation to allocate resources effectively. This capability also encompasses Research Programme Development, structuring viable research projects for successful execution. By systematically managing research opportunities, it enhances strategic decision-making and maximises research impact within the Higher Education context.
