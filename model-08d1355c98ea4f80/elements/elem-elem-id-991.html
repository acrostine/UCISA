Budget Mgt ()
Budget Management consolidates financial planning inputs to produce detailed budget allocations aligned with institutional priorities. It consumes historical financial data and strategic plans, producing approved budget frameworks and variance reports. The process enables effective resource allocation and financial control across academic and administrative units.
