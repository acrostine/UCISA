Student Enrolment ()
The Student Enrolment capability manages the full lifecycle of student registration within the institution, ensuring accurate and timely enrolment in programmes and modules. It encompasses processes such as Programme Enrolment, where students formally register for their chosen course of study; Module Selection Management, which facilitates student choice and allocation of curriculum components; and Student Induction Management, supporting the transition into academic life through orientation activities. This capability ensures compliance with academic regulations and institutional policies while providing a seamless experience for students. It delivers organisational value by enabling efficient resource planning, maintaining enrolment data integrity, and supporting student progression and retention.
