Research Dataset Mgt ()
Research Dataset Management coordinates the collection, curation, storage, and preservation of datasets generated during research projects. It consumes raw experimental or observational data and metadata, ensuring compliance with data governance and ethical standards. This process produces well-documented, accessible datasets that support transparency, reproducibility, and future reuse, enhancing the institution's research credibility and compliance with funder mandates.
