Finance Management ()
Finance Management governs the institution's financial resources to ensure sustainability, accountability, and compliance with regulatory requirements. It encompasses budgeting, expenditure control, income generation, asset oversight, and risk mitigation through insurance and taxation management. This capability supports strategic decision-making by providing accurate financial reporting and advisory services. Procurement, supplier relations, and treasury functions enable efficient allocation and safeguarding of funds, underpinning the institution’s operational and academic objectives.
