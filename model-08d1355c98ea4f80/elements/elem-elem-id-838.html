Module Selection Mgt ()
Module Selection Management facilitates students in choosing appropriate modules aligned with their academic goals and programme requirements. It consumes programme curricula, academic regulations, and student preferences to generate confirmed module enrolments. This process ensures compliance with academic policies and optimises course load balancing, enhancing student satisfaction and progression tracking.
