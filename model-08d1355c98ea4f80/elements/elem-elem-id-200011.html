Facilities & Property Management ()
Facilities & Property Management ensures the effective stewardship and optimisation of the physical estate and related assets within the higher education institution. It encompasses processes such as Campus Parking, Campus Security, Commercial Tenancy, Environmental Management, Estate Development & Management, Health & Safety, Mail Management, Property Maintenance, Space Utilisation, and Vehicle Management. This capability supports the institution's operational continuity, regulatory compliance, and strategic growth by maintaining safe, functional, and sustainable environments for staff, students, and visitors. Inputs include estate data, regulatory requirements, and user needs, while outputs deliver secure, well-maintained facilities and optimised space utilisation. Ultimately, it enables a supportive campus experience and facilitates institutional reputation and effectiveness.
