Assessment Administration ()
Assessment Administration organises and manages assessment schedules, submission guidelines, and compliance with academic regulations. It consumes course requirements, institutional policies, and student enrolment data to ensure assessments align with curriculum standards. This process produces assessment timetables, registration records, and administrative documentation. It enables efficient coordination and reduces risks of procedural errors, supporting academic integrity and student experience.
