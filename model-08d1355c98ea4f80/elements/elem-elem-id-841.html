Academic Year Scheduling ()
This process establishes the academic calendar by defining term dates, examination periods, and enrolment deadlines based on institutional priorities and regulatory requirements. It consumes inputs such as faculty availability, resource constraints, and external accreditation timelines to develop a coordinated schedule. The output enables efficient resource planning, supports student progression, and aligns academic activities with strategic institutional goals.
