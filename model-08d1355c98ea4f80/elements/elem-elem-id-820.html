Strategic Vision Development ()
Strategic Vision Development crafts a forward-looking and inspirational vision statement that encapsulates the institution’s aspirations and core values. It consumes stakeholder engagement insights and environmental context to produce a clear and motivating vision narrative. This process provides a shared sense of purpose, guiding strategic priorities and fostering unity across the university community.
