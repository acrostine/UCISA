Commercial Engagement ()
The Commercial Engagement capability drives strategic partnerships and collaborations with external commercial entities to enhance the institution's revenue streams and market presence. It encompasses engaging potential partners, negotiating agreements, and managing ongoing relationships to ensure mutual value creation. Key processes include Commercial Partner Engagement, which focuses on identifying and initiating partnerships, and Commercial Partner Integration & Transition, which manages the onboarding and alignment of commercial partners with institutional objectives. This capability enables the institution to leverage external expertise and resources, fostering innovation and expanding commercial opportunities within the Higher Education sector.
