Research Publication Mgt ()
Research Publication Management oversees the submission, approval, and archiving of academic publications within the institution. It consumes manuscripts, peer reviews, and compliance guidelines to ensure publications meet quality standards and policy requirements. This process adds value by streamlining publication workflows, maintaining accurate records for audits, and facilitating dissemination of research outcomes.
