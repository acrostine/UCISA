Income Mgt ()
Income Management oversees the receipt and recording of all institutional revenues, including tuition fees, grants, and donations. It consumes payment data and contractual agreements to generate income statements and reconcile accounts. This process ensures timely and accurate revenue recognition, supporting cash flow stability and financial planning.
