Expenditure Mgt ()
Expenditure Management monitors and controls institutional spending against approved budgets. It consumes purchase orders, invoices, and payment requests to generate expenditure reports and ensure timely payment processing. This process supports financial discipline, prevents overspending, and provides transparency in financial commitments.
