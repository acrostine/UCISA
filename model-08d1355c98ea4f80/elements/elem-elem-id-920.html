Commercial Contingency Planning ()
Develops risk mitigation strategies and response plans for potential disruptions in commercial activities by analyzing operational risks, market conditions, and contractual obligations. It consumes risk assessments and business continuity frameworks to ensure preparedness for unforeseen events. This planning safeguards institutional revenue and reputation by maintaining operational resilience in commercial engagements.
