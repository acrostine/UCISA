Non-Commercial Research Impact Mgt ()
This process facilitates the dissemination and application of research findings that contribute to public policy, community development, and societal wellbeing without direct commercial gain. It consumes validated research outputs and stakeholder feedback to tailor impact strategies. Key outputs include impact case studies, policy briefs, and community engagement activities. This process enhances the institution’s societal relevance and supports knowledge exchange beyond commercial parameters.
