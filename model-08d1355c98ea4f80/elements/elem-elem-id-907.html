Researcher Performance Mgt ()
Researcher Performance Management evaluates individual researchers’ contributions, outputs, and development needs using publication records, grant success rates, and peer evaluations. It generates performance appraisals, development plans, and recognition reports. This process drives talent development, motivates high performance, and aligns researcher goals with institutional research priorities.
