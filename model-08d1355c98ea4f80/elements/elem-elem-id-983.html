Property Maintenance ()
Property Maintenance schedules and executes routine and reactive upkeep for university facilities and infrastructure. It consumes maintenance requests, inspection reports, and resource availability to deliver safe, functional, and compliant environments. This process preserves asset value, reduces downtime, and supports uninterrupted university activities.
