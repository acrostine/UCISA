Alumni Relationship Mgt ()
Alumni Relationship Mgt maintains and nurtures long-term connections with graduates by managing communication preferences, tracking engagement activities, and responding to alumni inquiries. It uses alumni profiles and interaction history to deliver personalized communications and relationship-building initiatives. This process drives sustained alumni loyalty, encourages advocacy, and facilitates collaboration opportunities that benefit both alumni and the institution.
