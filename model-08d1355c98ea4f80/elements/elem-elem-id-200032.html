Student Attraction & Recruitment ()
The Student Attraction & Recruitment capability focuses on engaging and enrolling prospective students to meet institutional intake targets and diversity goals. It encompasses targeted domestic and international recruitment processes tailored to distinct markets and demographics. This capability manages proactive engagement with prospective students through outreach, events, and digital communications to enhance awareness and interest. It also includes administering scholarships and bursaries to incentivize applications and managing relationships with recruitment agents to extend the institution's reach. Together, these processes ensure a steady pipeline of qualified applicants aligned with the institution’s teaching and learning objectives.
