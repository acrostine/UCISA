Alumni Event & Campaign Mgt ()
Alumni Event & Campaign Mgt coordinates and executes events and communication campaigns targeting alumni to foster ongoing engagement and support. It consumes alumni data, event schedules, and marketing resources to produce targeted invitations, event logistics, and campaign performance reports. This process enhances alumni participation, strengthens community ties, and supports fundraising and institutional reputation objectives.
