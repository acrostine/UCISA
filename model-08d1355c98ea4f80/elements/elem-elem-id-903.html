Commercialise Research Outcomes ()
This process transforms research findings into marketable products, services, or intellectual property by leveraging institutional resources and external partnerships. It consumes validated research outputs and market intelligence to identify commercial potential. The process produces licensing agreements, spin-off enterprises, and revenue streams that enhance institutional reputation and financial sustainability. It drives innovation adoption and societal benefit through effective knowledge transfer.
