Research Output Mgt ()
Research Output Management oversees the organisation, dissemination, and archiving of scholarly products such as publications, presentations, and patents. It consumes research findings and publication materials, managing copyright, open access requirements, and impact metrics. The process delivers verified and accessible research outputs that increase institutional visibility, support academic reputation, and comply with reporting obligations.
