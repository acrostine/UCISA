Private Sector Housing Mgt
Accommodation Allocation

This flow transfers details of available private-sector properties, allocated student placements, and contract terms from Private Sector Housing Management to Accommodation Allocation. Accommodation Allocation uses this to update its overall housing portfolio and refer remaining applicants; without it, the allocation process would be blind to external capacity, leading to underutilized partnerships.
