Academic Administration ()
The Academic Administration capability ensures the effective management of academic operations within the institution's Teaching & Learning domain. It encompasses the development and enforcement of academic policies and regulations, the planning and scheduling of the academic year, and the coordination of timetabling activities. Inputs include regulatory frameworks, enrolment data, and resource availability, while outputs consist of approved academic calendars, timetables, and compliance reports. This capability supports academic staff and students by providing structured, timely, and compliant academic operations, enhancing the overall educational experience and institutional governance.
