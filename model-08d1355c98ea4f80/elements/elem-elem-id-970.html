Knowledge Mgt ()
This process facilitates the creation, sharing, and preservation of institutional knowledge assets, including best practices and expertise. It consumes content from academic staff, researchers, and administrators, producing knowledge repositories and collaboration platforms. Effective knowledge management fosters innovation, continuous improvement, and organisational learning.
