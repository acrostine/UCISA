Research Programme Performance Mgt ()
Research Programme Performance Management monitors and evaluates the progress and outcomes of research initiatives against defined objectives and funding criteria. It consumes performance data, project reports, and stakeholder feedback to generate performance reviews and improvement plans. This process supports strategic decision-making, ensures effective resource allocation, and enhances the impact of research programmes within the institution.
