Non Presence Based Teaching ()
Non Presence Based Teaching delivers educational experiences through online, blended, and distance learning methods, producing accessible and flexible course delivery. It consumes digital learning materials, student data, and virtual communication tools to facilitate engagement. This process enhances inclusivity and supports diverse learner needs by removing geographical and time constraints, thereby increasing overall student participation and satisfaction.
