Human Resource Management ()
Human Resource Management in Higher Education institutions encompasses the strategic and operational activities required to attract, develop, support, and retain academic and professional staff. This capability integrates processes such as Staff Recruitment, Workforce Planning, and Talent Management to ensure the institution meets its staffing needs aligned with its academic and research goals. It also includes Employee Performance Management, Training & Development, and Staff Engagement to enhance staff effectiveness and satisfaction. Administrative and welfare functions like Remuneration & Entitlements Management, Staff Absence Management, Occupational Health, Workforce Relations Management, and Staff Record & Details Management provide essential support and compliance. Comprehensive Workforce Reporting delivers insights to inform decision-making and optimise human capital across the institution.
