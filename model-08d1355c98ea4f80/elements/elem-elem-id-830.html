Enterprise Architecture ()
Enterprise Architecture develops and maintains a comprehensive blueprint of the institution's business capabilities, information systems, and technology infrastructure. It consumes strategy documents, technology standards, and operational data to guide decision-making. This process produces architecture models and roadmaps that ensure alignment between IT and academic objectives, supporting institutional agility and innovation.
