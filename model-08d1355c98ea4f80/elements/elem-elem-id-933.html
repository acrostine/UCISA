Food & Retail Mgt ()
Food & Retail Management oversees the operation and quality of on-campus dining and retail services, ensuring diverse and accessible options. It consumes supplier inputs, health regulations, and customer demand data to manage inventory and service delivery. This process contributes to campus life quality and supports a vibrant, convenient environment for the university community.
