Supporting Services ()
Supporting Services enable the provision of essential non-academic functions that enhance the campus experience and contribute to the well-being of students, staff, and visitors. This capability encompasses managing child care, handling complaints and compliments, overseeing food and retail operations, delivering healthcare services, coordinating internal communications, managing printing services, providing religious support, facilitating sport and recreation activities, and administering venue management. Inputs include service requests, regulatory requirements, and stakeholder feedback, while outputs comprise service delivery reports, satisfaction metrics, and operational improvements. By maintaining these supportive functions, the institution fosters a safe, inclusive, and engaging environment that underpins academic success and community cohesion.
