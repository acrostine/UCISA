Compliance Monitoring & Reporting

This flow carries compliance reports detailing adherence to research ethics, data protection, and funding regulations from Research Compliance Mgt to Compliance Monitoring & Reporting. Compliance Monitoring & Reporting depends on this to compile institutional compliance dashboards and report to regulators; without it, the university would lack oversight of research integrity, risking sanctions or loss of funding.
