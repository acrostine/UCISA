Corporate Performance Mgt ()
Corporate Performance Management monitors and evaluates institutional performance against strategic objectives through data collection from academic, financial, and operational sources. It consumes performance metrics and benchmarks to produce comprehensive performance dashboards and variance analyses. This process supports informed decision-making by providing evidence-based insights, enhancing accountability, and driving continuous improvement across the university.
