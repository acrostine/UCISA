Information & Data Mgt

This flow carries research datasets including raw data, processed files, and associated documentation from Research Dataset Mgt to Information & Data Mgt. Information & Data Mgt depends on this to store, backup, and ensure long-term accessibility of research data; without it, datasets risk loss or corruption, undermining reproducibility and compliance with data policies.
