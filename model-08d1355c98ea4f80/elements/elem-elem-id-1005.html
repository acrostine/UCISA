Private Sector Housing Mgt ()
Private Sector Housing Management coordinates partnerships and agreements with external housing providers to supplement institutional accommodation capacity. It consumes contract data, market information, and demand forecasts to produce leasing arrangements, compliance reports, and occupant allocations. This process expands housing options, supports diverse student needs, and optimises institutional accommodation strategy.
