Strategic Reporting ()
Strategic Reporting compiles and communicates progress, outcomes, and impacts related to strategic objectives to internal and external stakeholders. It consumes performance data, plan updates, and risk assessments to produce formal reports and presentations. This process enhances transparency, supports governance oversight, and fosters stakeholder confidence by demonstrating accountability and strategic progress.
