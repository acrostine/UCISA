Student Assessment ()
The Student Assessment capability enables the design, delivery, and management of student evaluations within the Teaching & Learning domain. It encompasses processes such as Assessment Administration, which coordinates scheduling and compliance; Assessment Delivery, managing the execution of exams and assignments; Assessment Marking & Feedback, providing timely evaluation and constructive responses; and Student Results Management, ensuring accurate recording and dissemination of outcomes. This capability supports academic standards, regulatory compliance, and student progression by maintaining integrity and transparency throughout the assessment lifecycle.
