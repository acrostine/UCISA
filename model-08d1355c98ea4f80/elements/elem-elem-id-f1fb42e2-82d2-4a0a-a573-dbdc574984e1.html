Teaching & Learning Content Mgt

This flow carries data on which modules students have selected, including module codes, credits, and timetabling preferences. Teaching & Learning Content Mgt depends on this to allocate teaching staff, prepare materials, and schedule classes; without it, delivery would be unplanned, leading to resource shortages and student dissatisfaction.
