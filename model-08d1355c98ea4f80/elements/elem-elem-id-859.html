Graduation Record Certificate Mgt ()
Manages the creation, verification, and distribution of graduation records and certificates to eligible students. It utilizes confirmed eligibility data and academic transcripts as inputs and generates official graduation documents and digital credentials. This process ensures the integrity and authenticity of graduate certification, facilitating recognition by employers and professional bodies while safeguarding institutional credibility.
