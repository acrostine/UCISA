Legal Services ()
The Legal Services capability provides expert legal support to the institution, ensuring compliance with relevant laws and regulations while managing legal risks. It encompasses contract management, dispute resolution, legal and legislative compliance, and legal advisory processes. This capability safeguards the institution's interests by facilitating sound decision-making and protecting against legal liabilities. It supports academic and administrative functions by delivering timely legal guidance and managing contractual obligations effectively.
