Commercial Sourcing ()
The Commercial Sourcing capability enables the Higher Education institution to systematically identify, assess, and engage with commercial opportunities to generate external income and enhance institutional sustainability. It encompasses processes such as Commercial Market Analysis, which gathers and interprets market data relevant to academic and non-academic offerings; Commercial Opportunity Assessment, which evaluates potential ventures for alignment with institutional strategy and risk profile; and Commercial Opportunity Identification, which detects new commercial prospects in education, research, and ancillary services. This capability supports informed decision-making and strategic partnerships, driving revenue diversification and strengthening the institution's competitive position in the external market.
