Student Support & Wellbeing Management ()
The Student Support & Wellbeing Management capability ensures comprehensive support services that promote student success and holistic wellbeing throughout their academic journey. It integrates processes such as Academic Advice Management, Academic Skills Development, and Personal Tutor Provision to enhance learning outcomes and personal growth. Disability Support Management, Student Health & Wellbeing, and Housing Advice address diverse student needs, fostering an inclusive and supportive campus environment. Career & Employability Engagement Management and Student Financial Advice provide critical guidance for future planning and financial stability. This capability enhances student engagement, retention, and satisfaction, contributing to the institution's overall academic excellence and reputation.
