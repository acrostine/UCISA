Research Training ()
The Research Training capability encompasses the structured development of postgraduate researchers and academic staff within the research domain. It ensures provision of targeted training activities including PGR Student Skills Development, PGR Supervisor Development, and Researcher Training & Development. This capability supports the enhancement of research competencies, supervisory effectiveness, and career progression for researchers. It inputs institutional policies, funding, and academic resources, producing skilled researchers and supervisors equipped to contribute to high-quality research outputs. By fostering research capacity building, this capability underpins the institution's research excellence and reputation.
