Strategy & Governance ()
The Strategy & Governance domain establishes the framework and oversight necessary to align institutional objectives with operational execution in Higher Education. It encompasses Corporate Governance, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and stakeholder accountability. Business Capability Management drives the development and optimisation of institutional capabilities to support strategic goals. Strategy Management formulates, monitors, and adjusts strategic plans to respond to evolving educational landscapes and funding environments. Together, these capabilities enable the institution to maintain organisational integrity, enhance decision-making, and sustain long-term academic and operational excellence.
