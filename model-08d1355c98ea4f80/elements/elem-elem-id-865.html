Student Mobility ()
Student Mobility manages the administration of student exchanges, study abroad programmes, and other forms of academic mobility. It consumes application details, partner institution agreements, and credit transfer information to coordinate placements and validate academic outcomes. This process enhances the institution’s global engagement and provides students with enriched learning experiences while maintaining accurate academic records.
