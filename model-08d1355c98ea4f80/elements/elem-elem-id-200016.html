Information Management ()
Information Management encompasses the systematic handling of data and information assets across the institution to support decision-making, compliance, and operational efficiency. It integrates processes such as Business & Operational Reporting, Enterprise Content Management, and Information & Data Security Management to ensure accurate, secure, and accessible information. This capability manages the lifecycle of information, including collection, storage, retrieval, analysis, and preservation, while addressing copyright and records management requirements unique to the higher education environment. By enabling effective knowledge management and information discovery, it enhances research, teaching, and administrative functions, thereby underpinning institutional agility and accountability.
