Merchandising ()
Merchandising manages the design, procurement, and distribution of branded merchandise to support promotional efforts and community building. It consumes brand guidelines, event schedules, and budget allocations to supply relevant items. The process produces merchandise inventories, distribution plans, and sales reports, enhancing brand visibility and stakeholder loyalty.
