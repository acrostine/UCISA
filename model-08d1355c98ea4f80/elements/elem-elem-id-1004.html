Accommodation Departure & Turnaround Mgt ()
Accommodation Departure & Turnaround Management coordinates the exit of occupants and prepares rooms for subsequent allocation by overseeing cleaning, maintenance, and inspections. It consumes departure notices, room condition reports, and maintenance schedules to output ready-to-occupy accommodation units. This process maintains high standards of living conditions and minimises vacancy periods to maximise accommodation efficiency.
