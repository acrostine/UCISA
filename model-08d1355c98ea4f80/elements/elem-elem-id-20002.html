Accommodation Management ()
Accommodation Management oversees the provision and administration of student and staff housing within the institution. It ensures effective allocation of accommodation resources, manages departures and turnaround processes to maintain availability, and coordinates facilities maintenance to uphold living standards. The capability also handles incident and emergency responses related to accommodation, safeguarding residents and property. Additionally, it manages relationships with private sector housing providers to supplement institutional offerings. This capability supports student wellbeing and campus community cohesion by delivering safe, reliable, and responsive housing services.
