Research Impact ()
The Research Impact capability enables a Higher Education institution to maximise the societal, economic, and cultural benefits derived from its research activities. It encompasses processes that translate research outcomes into practical applications, foster strategic partnerships, and manage non-commercial impacts such as policy influence and community engagement. Inputs include research findings, stakeholder requirements, and external collaboration opportunities, while outputs deliver measurable benefits to industry, government, and society at large. This capability supports the institution's mission to demonstrate value beyond academia, enhance reputation, and attract funding through impactful research contributions.
