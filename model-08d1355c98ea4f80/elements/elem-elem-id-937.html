Child Care Mgt ()
Child Care Management coordinates the provision of on-campus childcare services, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for students, staff, and faculty with childcare needs. It consumes regulatory standards, staffing resources, and facility inputs to deliver accessible childcare options. This process enhances institutional inclusivity and supports student and employee retention by addressing family care responsibilities.
