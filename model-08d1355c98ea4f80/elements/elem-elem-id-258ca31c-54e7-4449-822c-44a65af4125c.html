This flow carries dataset metadata such as descriptions, collection methods, variables, and usage licences from Research Dataset Mgt to Research Output Reporting. Research Output Reporting depends on this to catalogue and report research outputs accurately in institutional repositories; without it, reporting would lack the descriptive detail needed for discoverability and impact assessment.
