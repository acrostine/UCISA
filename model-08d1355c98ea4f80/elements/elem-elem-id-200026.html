Research Publications ()
The Research Publications capability manages the creation, dissemination, and archiving of scholarly outputs produced by researchers within the institution. It encompasses the processes of Research Output Reporting, which compiles and communicates research achievements, and Research Publication Management, which oversees submission, peer review coordination, and compliance with open access policies. This capability ensures the institution's research contributions are accurately represented, accessible, and aligned with academic standards. It supports institutional reputation, funding opportunities, and knowledge transfer by facilitating transparent and effective publication workflows.
