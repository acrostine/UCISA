Student Admission Management ()
The Student Admission Management capability governs the end-to-end process of enrolling students into academic programs. It encompasses the Offer, Acceptance & Quota Management process, which controls the allocation of available places and manages offer issuance and acceptance tracking. The Student Application Management process handles the receipt, validation, and assessment of student applications against entry criteria. This capability ensures a fair, transparent, and efficient admission cycle, aligning student intake with institutional capacity and strategic objectives, thereby supporting effective Teaching & Learning operations.
