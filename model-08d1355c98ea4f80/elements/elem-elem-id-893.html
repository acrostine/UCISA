Research Funds Mgt ()
Research Funds Management consumes awarded grant agreements and financial regulations to produce accurate budget allocations, expenditure tracking, and compliance reports. It ensures that research projects operate within financial constraints and meet funder requirements. This process supports transparent financial stewardship, mitigates risk of non-compliance, and maximises the impact of research funding on institutional objectives.
