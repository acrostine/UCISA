Quality Mgt ()
Quality Management establishes and maintains standards, policies, and procedures to ensure excellence in institutional processes and outcomes. It leverages audit results, feedback, and performance metrics to drive continuous improvement. The process produces quality assurance reports and improvement plans that uphold academic and operational standards across the university.
