Housing Advice ()
Housing Advice offers guidance on accommodation options, tenancy rights, and related welfare issues. It utilises student housing surveys and local accommodation market information, producing informed housing choices and support referrals. This process reduces housing-related stress and enhances overall student wellbeing.
