Teaching & Learning Delivery ()
The Teaching & Learning Delivery capability focuses on the effective execution of educational activities to facilitate student learning and skill development. It encompasses both presence-based and non-presence-based teaching methods to accommodate diverse learning modalities. This capability manages the organisation and coordination of student placements to provide practical experience aligned with academic programs. Additionally, it oversees the development, management, and distribution of teaching and learning content to ensure accessibility and quality. Together, these processes enable a flexible, comprehensive learning environment that supports academic success and workforce readiness.
