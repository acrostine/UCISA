Library Management ()
The Library Management capability coordinates the acquisition, organization, access, and preservation of physical and digital collections to support academic and research activities. It encompasses processes such as Art/Museum Collection Management, Library Collection Access Management, Library Collection Resource Management, and Library Membership Management. Inputs include collection materials, membership data, and resource metadata, while outputs consist of curated collections, accessible resources, and membership services. This capability ensures efficient resource availability, enhances user experience, and supports the institution’s educational and research missions by maintaining comprehensive and accessible knowledge assets.
