Teaching & Learning ()
The Teaching & Learning domain encompasses the core capabilities that support the delivery of academic programs and the overall student lifecycle within a Higher Education institution. It includes processes from attracting and recruiting prospective students, managing admissions, enrolment, and academic administration, to delivering teaching and assessment activities. The domain ensures effective curriculum management and supports student progression through to completion and graduation. Additionally, it addresses student support and wellbeing, fostering engagement, and maintaining lifelong connections through alumni relations. This domain is critical for maintaining academic quality, enhancing student experience, and supporting institutional reputation and compliance.
