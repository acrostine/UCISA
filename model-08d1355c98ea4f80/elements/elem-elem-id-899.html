PGR Supervisor Development ()
PGR Supervisor Development focuses on equipping research supervisors with up-to-date knowledge, pedagogical skills, and leadership capabilities. It consumes institutional policies, best practice guidelines, and feedback from supervisors and students to shape its training offerings. This process produces competent supervisors who can effectively guide postgraduate researchers through their studies. Organisational value arises from enhanced supervisory quality, leading to improved research outcomes and student satisfaction.
