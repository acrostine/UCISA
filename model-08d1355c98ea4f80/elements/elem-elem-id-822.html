Business Horizon Scanning ()
Business Horizon Scanning systematically gathers and analyses external and internal trends, emerging technologies, regulatory changes, and competitive intelligence relevant to the Higher Education sector. It consumes environmental data and stakeholder insights to identify potential opportunities and threats. This process produces actionable foresight reports that inform strategic decision-making, enabling the institution to anticipate changes and adapt proactively to maintain its competitive edge.
