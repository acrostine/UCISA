Promotions Management ()
Promotions Management orchestrates the strategic planning and execution of promotional activities to enhance the visibility and appeal of the institution's offerings. It integrates key processes such as Brand Management, Campaign Management, Event Management, Market Research, Marketing Management, and Merchandising to deliver cohesive and targeted promotional initiatives. Inputs include market insights, brand guidelines, campaign objectives, and event plans, while outputs encompass promotional materials, campaign performance reports, and enhanced institutional reputation. This capability drives student recruitment, stakeholder engagement, and competitive positioning within the higher education sector.
