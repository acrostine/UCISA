Commercial Activity Output Mgt ()
Commercial Activity Output Management monitors and controls the deliverables generated from commercial projects and contracts. It consumes project plans, performance data, and quality standards to verify outputs against expectations. The process produces validated deliverables, performance metrics, and client feedback reports. This management enhances institutional accountability and client satisfaction by ensuring outputs meet defined quality and contractual requirements.
