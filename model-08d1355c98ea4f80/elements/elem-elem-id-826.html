Benefits Mgt ()
Benefits Management identifies, defines, and tracks the realisation of expected benefits from strategic initiatives and projects. It consumes business cases, project plans, and performance data to ensure alignment with institutional goals. This process produces benefits realisation reports and corrective action plans, enhancing accountability and value delivery across the university.
