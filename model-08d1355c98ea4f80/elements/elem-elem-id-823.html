Strategic Plan Development ()
Strategic Plan Development integrates institutional goals, stakeholder inputs, and environmental analysis to formulate a coherent multi-year strategic plan. It consumes insights from horizon scanning, performance data, and stakeholder consultations to produce a documented strategic framework. This process aligns resources and priorities, guiding the institution’s long-term direction and ensuring responsiveness to sector challenges and opportunities.
