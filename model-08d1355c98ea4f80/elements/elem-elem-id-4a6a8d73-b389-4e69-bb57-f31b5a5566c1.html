Budget Mgt

This flow carries data on allocated research funds, including amounts, sources, and spending restrictions. Budget Mgt depends on this to integrate research funding into institutional financial planning and monitoring; without it, budget allocations would be incomplete, leading to potential overspend or underutilisation of funds.
