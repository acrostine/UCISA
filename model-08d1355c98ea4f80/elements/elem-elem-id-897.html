Researcher Training & Development ()
Researcher Training & Development provides continuous professional development opportunities for academic and research staff, including methodological, ethical, and career progression training. It consumes strategic research priorities, staff development needs assessments, and external funding criteria to design relevant programs. The process produces a highly skilled research workforce capable of driving innovation and securing competitive funding. This capability supports institutional research excellence and sustained academic reputation.
