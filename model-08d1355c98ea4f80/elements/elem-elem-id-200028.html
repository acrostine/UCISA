Strategy Management ()
The Strategy Management capability governs the formulation, execution, and oversight of the institution’s strategic objectives within the Strategy & Governance domain. It enables the institution to anticipate external trends through Business Horizon Scanning and aligns operational activities with long-term goals via Corporate Performance Management. This capability supports the development, management, and continuous refinement of the Strategic Plan, ensuring responsiveness to evolving sector demands. Strategic Reporting provides critical insights for informed decision-making, while Strategic Vision Development establishes a clear and inspiring direction for the institution’s future. Collectively, these processes ensure that the institution maintains a competitive position and fulfils its mission effectively.
