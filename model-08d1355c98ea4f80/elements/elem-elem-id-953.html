Staff Record & Details Mgt ()
This process maintains accurate and up-to-date personnel records, including qualifications, contact details, and employment history, by inputting data from recruitment, training, and HR updates. It consumes various HR transactions and documentation to ensure comprehensive staff profiles. Reliable record management underpins effective decision-making and regulatory compliance in human resource operations.
