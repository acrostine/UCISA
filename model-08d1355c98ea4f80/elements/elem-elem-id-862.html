Enrolment Status Mgt ()
Enrolment Status Management maintains accurate and up-to-date records of student enrolment statuses throughout their academic lifecycle. It consumes applications, registration data, and academic progress information to determine and update enrolment conditions. This process ensures compliance with institutional policies and regulatory requirements while enabling timely access to services based on enrolment status, thereby supporting effective student administration and resource planning.
