Government, Public & Stakeholder Relationships ()
The Government, Public & Stakeholder Relationships capability manages interactions and partnerships with external and internal stakeholders critical to the institution's success. It encompasses processes that maintain and enhance relationships with government bodies, industry partners, media, other tertiary institutions, and internal stakeholders. These activities ensure effective communication, collaboration, and alignment with regulatory requirements, funding opportunities, and public expectations. By nurturing these diverse relationships, the institution strengthens its reputation, influences policy, and secures resources essential for its strategic objectives.
