Professional Learning (Staff) ()
Provides continuous development opportunities for academic staff to update skills and knowledge relevant to curriculum delivery and pedagogy. Consumes staff feedback, educational research, and institutional priorities to design learning activities. This process supports teaching excellence, promotes innovation in curriculum delivery, and contributes to staff retention and career progression.
