Academic Advice Mgt ()
Academic Advice Management facilitates the delivery of tailored guidance to students regarding course selection, progression, and academic requirements. It consumes student academic records and curriculum information, producing personalised academic plans and support interventions. This process enhances student success by enabling informed decision-making and proactive management of academic challenges.
