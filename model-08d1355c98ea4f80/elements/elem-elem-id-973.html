Records Mgt ()
This process administers the classification, retention, and disposal of institutional records in compliance with legal and regulatory frameworks. It consumes both physical and digital records, ensuring they are properly archived or destroyed as required. Proper records management mitigates risk, supports audits, and preserves institutional memory.
