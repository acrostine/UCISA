Contract Mgt ()
Contract Mgt produces legally sound and enforceable agreements by reviewing, drafting, and managing contracts with external and internal parties. It consumes inputs such as proposed terms, institutional policies, and regulatory requirements. This process ensures risk mitigation, clarity of obligations, and compliance, supporting the institution’s strategic partnerships and operational integrity.
