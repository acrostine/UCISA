Enabling Capabilities ()
The Enabling Capabilities domain encompasses essential support functions that underpin the effective operation of a Higher Education institution. This domain integrates a diverse set of capabilities including Human Resource Management, Finance Management, and Information & Communication Technology Management, ensuring that core administrative and operational needs are met efficiently. Facilities & Property Management and Accommodation Management provide the physical infrastructure and living environments necessary for academic and student activities. Legal Services and Government, Public & Stakeholder Relationships manage compliance, governance, and external engagement critical to institutional reputation and sustainability. Promotions Management, Information Management, Supporting Services, and Library Management contribute to the institution’s communication, knowledge resources, and overall service quality, collectively enabling academic excellence and organisational resilience.
