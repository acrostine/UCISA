Student Administration ()
The Student Administration capability manages all administrative aspects of student lifecycle within the Teaching & Learning domain. It oversees enrolment status, programme transfers, and the management of exceptional factors such as misconduct and appeals. This capability handles student financial administration, ensuring accurate processing of fees, payments, and scholarships. It supports student mobility, facilitating exchanges and study abroad arrangements, while maintaining comprehensive and secure student records and details. By integrating these processes, it delivers efficient, accurate, and compliant student administration services that enhance the student experience and institutional effectiveness.
