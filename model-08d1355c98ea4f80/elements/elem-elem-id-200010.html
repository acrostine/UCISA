Curriculum Management ()
The Curriculum Management capability oversees the end-to-end lifecycle of academic programs and modules within the Teaching & Learning domain. It ensures the systematic development, design, approval, and continuous improvement of curricula to meet academic standards and professional accreditation requirements. This capability integrates processes such as Curriculum & Resource Development, Curriculum Change Management, and Curriculum Design to align educational offerings with institutional goals and stakeholder expectations. It manages curriculum performance through regular monitoring and evaluation, supports curriculum retirement when programs become obsolete, and promotes professional learning for staff to maintain teaching excellence. By maintaining rigorous curriculum oversight, this capability enhances educational quality, compliance, and responsiveness to evolving academic and industry demands.
