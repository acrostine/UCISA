Accommodation Incident & Emergency Response

This flow transmits records of structural issues, fire safety system faults, and other infrastructure hazards identified during maintenance to Accommodation Incident & Emergency Response. The incident response process depends on this to update risk assessments and emergency protocols; without it, responders would lack critical safety information, increasing harm during emergencies.
