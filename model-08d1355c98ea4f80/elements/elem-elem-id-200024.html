Research Improvement ()
The Research Improvement capability enhances the effectiveness and impact of research activities within the institution. It encompasses managing research programme performance, ensuring research quality, and monitoring researcher performance to drive continuous advancement. Inputs include research outputs, performance data, and quality assessments, which inform targeted interventions and development initiatives. Outputs consist of improved research outcomes, higher standards of academic rigor, and strengthened researcher capabilities, contributing to the institution's reputation and funding opportunities.
