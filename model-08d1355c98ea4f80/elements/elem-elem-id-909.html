Research Infrastructure Mgt ()
Research Infrastructure Management oversees the provisioning, maintenance, and optimisation of physical and digital resources supporting research activities. It consumes resource requests, usage data, and maintenance schedules, producing availability reports, resource allocation plans, and infrastructure performance metrics. This process enhances research efficiency, supports cutting-edge investigation, and maximises return on investment in research assets.
