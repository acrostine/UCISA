Digital Communication Mgt ()
Digital Communication Management orchestrates the creation, distribution, and governance of digital messaging across multiple channels within the institution. It consumes communication objectives and content inputs, producing consistent, accessible, and timely digital interactions for stakeholders. This process strengthens engagement, supports institutional branding, and facilitates effective information dissemination.
