Curriculum Retirement Mgt ()
Coordinates the phased withdrawal of obsolete or underperforming curricula, considering student progression and regulatory compliance. Produces formal retirement plans and communicates timelines to academic and administrative units. This process optimises academic portfolio management, freeing resources for emerging priorities and maintaining curriculum relevance.
