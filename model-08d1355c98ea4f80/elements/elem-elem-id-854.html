Student Recruitment Agent Mgt ()
This process oversees the selection, training, and performance monitoring of external recruitment agents who represent the institution internationally. It consumes contractual agreements, compliance requirements, and performance metrics to ensure agents operate effectively and ethically. The outcome includes a reliable network of agents driving targeted student recruitment, improved market penetration, and consistent messaging. This process adds value by expanding recruitment capabilities and maintaining quality standards across diverse regions.
