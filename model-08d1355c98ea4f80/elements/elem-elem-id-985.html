Campus Parking ()
Campus Parking manages allocation and access control for parking facilities across the university estate. It consumes parking demand data and regulatory guidelines, producing parking permits, usage reports, and enforcement actions. This process enhances campus accessibility, supports compliance with local regulations, and optimises parking resource utilisation for staff, students, and visitors.
