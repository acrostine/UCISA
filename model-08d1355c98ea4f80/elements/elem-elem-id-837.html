Programme Enrolment ()
Programme Enrolment manages the registration of students onto academic programmes, processing application data, eligibility verification, and admission decisions. It produces validated enrolment records and integrates with student information systems for academic and administrative use. This process underpins accurate student cohort management, enabling effective resource allocation and regulatory compliance.
