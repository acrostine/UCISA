Commercial Monitoring ()
Commercial Monitoring provides continuous oversight of commercial activities to ensure alignment with institutional objectives and risk management requirements. It consolidates data from reporting, contingency planning, and contract performance management processes to identify trends, issues, and opportunities. Inputs include commercial performance data, contractual obligations, and market intelligence, while outputs comprise performance reports, risk assessments, and action recommendations. This capability supports informed decision-making, enhances commercial viability, and mitigates risks associated with external partnerships and revenue-generating activities within the Higher Education context.
