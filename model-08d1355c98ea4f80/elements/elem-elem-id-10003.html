Research ()
The Research domain encompasses the comprehensive capabilities that support the institution's research ecosystem. It includes Research Opportunity & Planning to identify and strategize potential research activities aligned with institutional goals. Research Delivery manages the execution of research projects, ensuring adherence to quality and compliance standards. Research Improvement drives continuous enhancement of research methodologies and outputs. Research Impact measures and maximises the societal, economic, and academic benefits derived from research activities. Research Training develops the skills and knowledge of researchers to maintain excellence and innovation. Research Administration oversees governance, ethical compliance, and operational support for research initiatives. Research Funding secures and manages financial resources to sustain research activities. Research Publications facilitate the dissemination and visibility of research findings, enhancing the institution's academic reputation.
