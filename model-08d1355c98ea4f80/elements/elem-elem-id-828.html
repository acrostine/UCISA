Organisational Design ()
Organisational Design shapes the structure, roles, and governance frameworks to support strategic goals and operational efficiency. It uses workforce data, strategic plans, and stakeholder input to create effective organisational models. The output includes organisational charts and role definitions that enable clear accountability and support evolving Higher Education demands.
