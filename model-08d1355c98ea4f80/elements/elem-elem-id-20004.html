Business Capability Management ()
Business Capability Management within the Strategy & Governance domain establishes and maintains a comprehensive view of the institution's capabilities to align strategic objectives with operational effectiveness. It integrates processes such as Benefits Management, Change Portfolio Management, Enterprise Architecture, Organisational Design, Programme & Project Management, and Quality Management to ensure capabilities evolve in response to institutional priorities. Inputs include strategic plans, stakeholder requirements, and performance data, while outputs comprise capability assessments, development roadmaps, and governance frameworks. This capability enables informed decision-making, optimises resource allocation, and drives continuous improvement across Higher Education operations.
