Assessment Marking & Feedback ()
Assessment Marking & Feedback manages the grading of submitted assessments and the provision of constructive feedback to students. It consumes completed assessment submissions and marking criteria, producing marked scripts, grades, and feedback reports. This process supports transparency, timely communication, and continuous student improvement by closing the feedback loop. It contributes directly to academic quality and student development.
