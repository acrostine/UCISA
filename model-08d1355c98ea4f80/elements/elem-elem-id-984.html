Environmental Mgt ()
Environmental Management monitors and improves the campus's environmental impact through sustainability initiatives and regulatory compliance. It integrates environmental data, policy requirements, and stakeholder input to produce sustainability reports and improvement plans. This process promotes ecological responsibility, reduces operational risks, and advances the university’s commitment to sustainable development.
