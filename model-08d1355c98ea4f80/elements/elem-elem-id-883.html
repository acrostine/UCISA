Student Placement Mgt ()
Student Placement Management coordinates practical work experiences, internships, and clinical placements, producing structured opportunities aligned with academic programs. It consumes placement provider information, student eligibility data, and regulatory requirements. This process bridges theoretical learning with real-world application, enhancing employability and professional readiness of students while maintaining compliance with accreditation standards.
