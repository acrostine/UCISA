UCISA - Feedback

Everything on this site is free to read, and that has been a deliberate choice, not an accident. I have built this because good enterprise architecture should be visible, not locked away, and because the model only gets better with real people looking at it and pushing back. Any feedback is welcome, whether it is a typo or a missing link. But the ones I will treasure most are the questions, the ones where someone is curious enough to ask why something is built the way it is. Thank you for the time.
