Student Mobility
Enrolment Status Mgt

This flow communicates the placement status (e.g., accepted, completed, withdrawn) for students participating in exchange or study abroad programmes from Student Mobility to Enrolment Status Management. Enrolment Status Management needs this to reflect the student's temporary or permanent absence from the home institution; without it, enrolment records would inaccurately show the student as present, affecting attendance monitoring and funding claims.
