Library Collection Resource Mgt ()
This process oversees the selection, acquisition, cataloguing, and maintenance of library materials such as books, journals, and digital media. It consumes procurement requests, budget allocations, and usage statistics, producing a curated, well-maintained collection aligned with curriculum and research needs. It ensures resource relevance and availability, directly contributing to effective teaching and scholarly activities.
