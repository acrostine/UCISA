Employee Support ()
Employee Support delivers advisory services, wellness programs, and conflict resolution resources by leveraging staff feedback and organisational policies. It consumes personal and professional needs data alongside HR guidelines to tailor assistance. This process improves staff wellbeing and satisfaction, thereby maintaining a motivated and resilient workforce critical to institutional success.
