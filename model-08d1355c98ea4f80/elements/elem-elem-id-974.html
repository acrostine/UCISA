Enterprise Content Mgt ()
This process oversees the organisation, storage, and lifecycle of digital and physical content across the institution. It consumes diverse content types such as documents, multimedia, and publications, and produces structured repositories and content delivery mechanisms. This supports efficient access, collaboration, and regulatory compliance for academic and operational needs.
