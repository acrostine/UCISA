Event Mgt ()
Event Mgt organises and coordinates marketing-related events such as open days, webinars, and alumni gatherings. It consumes event objectives, logistical requirements, and promotional materials to deliver seamless experiences. The process produces event schedules, attendee communications, and post-event reports, fostering community engagement and institutional visibility.
