Financial Reporting ()
Financial Reporting compiles financial transactions and balances into statutory and management reports. It consumes transactional data and accounting records to produce accurate financial statements, audit reports, and compliance disclosures. The process promotes accountability, regulatory adherence, and informed governance within the institution.
