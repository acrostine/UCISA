Commercial Activity Issue Mgt ()
Commercial Activity Issue Management handles the identification, assessment, and resolution of problems arising during commercial engagements. It consumes issue reports, stakeholder feedback, and contractual terms to prioritize and address concerns effectively. The process produces issue logs, resolution plans, and communication updates. This management protects institutional relationships and operational continuity by ensuring timely and transparent handling of commercial challenges.
