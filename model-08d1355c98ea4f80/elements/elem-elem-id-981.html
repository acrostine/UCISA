Campus Security ()
Campus Security orchestrates safety measures, surveillance, and incident response within the university grounds. It relies on threat assessments, access control systems, and emergency protocols to deliver secure environments and timely interventions. This process safeguards university assets, ensures personal safety, and maintains a secure atmosphere conducive to learning and research.
