Asset Mgt ()
Asset Management maintains comprehensive records of institutional assets, tracking acquisition, depreciation, and disposal. It consumes asset acquisition data and generates up-to-date asset registers and valuation reports. This process ensures optimal utilisation and safeguarding of physical and intangible assets, supporting accurate financial statements and compliance requirements.
