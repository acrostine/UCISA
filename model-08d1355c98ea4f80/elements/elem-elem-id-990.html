Procurement & Purchasing Mgt ()
Procurement & Purchasing Management coordinates acquisition of goods and services, ensuring compliance with policies and value for money. It consumes requisitions and vendor evaluations to generate purchase orders and contract documentation. The process facilitates efficient supply chain operations and supports academic and operational needs.
