Accommodation Facilities Mgt ()
Accommodation Facilities Management oversees the maintenance, repair, and upgrade of residential buildings and infrastructure. It consumes maintenance requests, inspection reports, and asset data to produce maintenance work orders and facility status updates. This process ensures safe, compliant, and functional living environments, enhancing occupant satisfaction and supporting institutional asset longevity.
