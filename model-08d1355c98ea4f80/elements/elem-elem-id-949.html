Employee Performance Mgt ()
This process produces performance evaluations and development plans by utilizing employee data, job objectives, and feedback inputs. It consumes inputs such as performance metrics, manager assessments, and peer reviews to inform decisions. Employee Performance Management enhances individual effectiveness and aligns staff contributions with institutional goals, fostering a culture of continuous improvement and accountability.
