ICT Service Support ()
ICT Service Support provides technical assistance, incident management, and problem resolution for all institutional ICT services. It consumes user reports and system alerts, delivering timely responses and solutions to maintain service continuity. This process minimizes downtime, enhances user experience, and sustains the reliability of critical ICT infrastructure.
