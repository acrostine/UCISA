Research Funding ()
The Research Funding capability enables a Higher Education institution to secure and manage financial resources necessary for research activities. It encompasses identifying appropriate funding opportunities, including grants, contracts, and partnerships, aligned with the institution's research priorities. This capability ensures efficient administration and compliance in managing awarded funds, facilitating optimal allocation and reporting. By sustaining a robust funding base, it supports the institution's strategic research goals and enhances its competitive position in the research landscape.
