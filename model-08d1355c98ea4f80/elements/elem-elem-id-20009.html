Corporate Governance ()
Corporate Governance ensures effective oversight and accountability within the Higher Education institution by establishing strategic direction, policies, and controls. It integrates processes such as Business Continuity Management, Compliance Monitoring & Reporting, Internal Audit & Reporting, Policy & Regulation Management, and Risk Management to safeguard institutional integrity and sustainability. Inputs include regulatory requirements, institutional strategy, and stakeholder expectations, while outputs encompass governance frameworks, compliance reports, risk assessments, and audit findings. This capability delivers organisational value by fostering transparency, mitigating risks, ensuring legal and ethical compliance, and supporting informed decision-making at all levels.
