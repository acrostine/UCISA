Programme Transfer Mgt ()
Programme Transfer Management handles requests and approvals for students transferring between academic programmes within the institution. It consumes transfer applications, academic transcripts, and programme requirements to assess eligibility and impact on progression. This process facilitates student mobility within the institution, ensuring academic continuity and accurate record updates to support student success and institutional reporting.
