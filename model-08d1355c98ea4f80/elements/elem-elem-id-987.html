Mail Mgt ()
Mail Management handles the receipt, sorting, distribution, and dispatch of physical correspondence and parcels for the institution. It utilises incoming and outgoing mail data and service agreements to ensure efficient delivery and tracking. This process supports effective communication channels critical to administrative and academic operations.
