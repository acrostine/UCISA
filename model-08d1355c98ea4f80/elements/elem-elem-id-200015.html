Information & Communication Technology Mgt ()
Information & Communication Technology Management encompasses the strategic planning, delivery, and oversight of ICT resources to support the institution's academic, research, and administrative functions. It integrates processes such as Application Lifecycle Management, Digital Communication Management, End User Computing, High Performance Computing, ICT Service Support, Identity & Access Management, and Infrastructure Lifecycle Management to ensure reliable, secure, and efficient technology services. This capability enables seamless access to digital tools and services, fosters innovation through advanced computing resources, and maintains operational continuity. By managing ICT assets effectively, the institution enhances user experience, supports digital transformation initiatives, and mitigates technology-related risks.
