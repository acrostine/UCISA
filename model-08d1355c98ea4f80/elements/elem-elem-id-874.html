Student Engagement & Retention ()
Student Engagement and Retention drives initiatives to enhance active participation in academic and extracurricular activities while monitoring attrition risks. It uses attendance data and student satisfaction surveys, producing targeted engagement programs and retention interventions. This process improves student satisfaction and reduces dropout rates.
