Business Continuity Mgt ()
Business Continuity Management produces robust plans and strategies to ensure critical academic and administrative functions persist during disruptions. It consumes risk assessments, institutional priorities, and resource availability data. This process safeguards institutional reputation and operational resilience, enabling uninterrupted student services and research activities.
