Collaborative Opportunity Mgt ()
Collaborative Opportunity Management facilitates identification and engagement with external partners and stakeholders to co-develop research initiatives. It consumes information on potential collaborators, funding calls, and institutional research priorities, producing joint proposal plans and partnership agreements. This process enhances research capacity by leveraging complementary expertise and shared resources, increasing the institution's competitiveness for collaborative funding opportunities.
