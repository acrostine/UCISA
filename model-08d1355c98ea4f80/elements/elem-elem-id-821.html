Strategic Plan Mgt ()
Strategic Plan Management oversees the implementation, monitoring, and adjustment of the strategic plan throughout its lifecycle. It consumes progress reports, performance data, and stakeholder feedback to produce updated action plans and risk assessments. This process ensures alignment of operational activities with strategic goals, maintaining momentum and adaptability in achieving institutional aspirations.
