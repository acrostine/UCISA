Manage Research Relationships ()
This process coordinates and nurtures collaborations between researchers, industry partners, funding bodies, and other stakeholders to maximise research impact. It consumes stakeholder requirements, partnership proposals, and ongoing research activities to maintain alignment and mutual benefit. The output includes formal agreements, joint initiatives, and sustained engagement networks. It strengthens institutional capacity to attract funding and amplify research contributions.
