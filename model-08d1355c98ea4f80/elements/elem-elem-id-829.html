Change Portfolio Mgt ()
Change Portfolio Management evaluates, prioritises, and balances the portfolio of change initiatives within the institution. It utilises project proposals, resource availability, and strategic priorities to optimise investment decisions. The process delivers a coherent change portfolio plan that supports effective resource allocation and risk mitigation in Higher Education transformation efforts.
