Commercial Opportunity Assessment ()
Commercial Opportunity Assessment examines identified opportunities by analyzing feasibility, risks, and alignment with institutional capabilities and strategic objectives. It consumes data from market analysis, internal resource evaluations, and stakeholder input, producing prioritized opportunity portfolios with recommended actions. This process supports informed investment decisions, ensuring resources focus on commercially viable and strategically beneficial initiatives.
